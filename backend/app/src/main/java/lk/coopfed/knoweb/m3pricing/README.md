## For M4: pricing an order line

Call `PricingQueries.resolveTradePrice(seller, buyer, skuId, uom, orderedQty, date, scope)` in the buyer's (or seller's) scope. It finds the relationship in force on the date (M1 `lookupRelationship`), reads the TRADE list it binds, takes the newest version whose `apply_from` is on or before the date, and returns the line whose tier is the highest not above the quantity (doc 10 A-03: the ordered quantity). The price is per unit, tax-exclusive, four decimals; empty means no price (not sellable under that relationship). The buyer reads the list through the `buyer_read` policy: the published versions of the list its relationship binds, nothing else of the seller's.

## Rules the code keeps

- A relationship binds the id of **version 1** of a list (`root_price_list_id`); every later version carries it, so a new version needs no relationship change.
- A line is written only while its list is a DRAFT: the handlers guard it and the trigger `pricing.line_of_a_draft` refuses anything else, even for the migrator.
- SetLines stores the whole set or nothing: when any line is refused the answer lists every outcome and nothing is written, audited or published.
- Publication runs the validator again (a SKU may have been deactivated since), dates the lines `apply_from`, publishes the draft and supersedes the previous published version.
- "Today" for a list is the calendar date in the business time zone (an entity has no business date, CR-19A-8).

## Deviations from the implementation guide

1. **`root_price_list_id`** is a column 23A section 3 does not have. Reason: relationships bind one id (M1 `price_list_id`) while every version is its own row; walking `source_version_id` in a policy and in every lookup is costlier and harder to read.
2. **Draft lines carry `effective_from`** = the day they were set, because the column is NOT NULL in 23A's DDL while 23A section 7 says "effective_from = null until publish"; publication sets it to `apply_from`.
3. **Events and audit for drafts.** Doc 23 section 4.1 leaves draft edits unaudited and names no event for CreatePriceList, DraftNewVersion or SetLines; the build requires every handler to audit and publish (AGENTS.md), so `PRICELIST_LINES_SET` and the events `price_list.drafted.v1` and `price_list.lines_set.v1` exist.
4. **`GET /v1/pricing/resolve/trade`**, not POST as 23A section 5 writes: it changes nothing and so needs no Idempotency-Key.
5. **Paths** are `/v1/pricing/lists...` as 23A section 5; the scaffold's `/v1/pricing/price-lists` is gone.
6. **Permission codes**: `prc.pricelist.author` and `prc.pricelist.publish` of 23A section 3.1, and `prc.pricelist.view` for the reads, which the guide does not name (as M2's `cat.sku.view`, CR-22A-3). `prc.pricelist.publish` requires the second factor in the catalogue and on the handler: only TRADE lists exist so far, and 23A asks MFA for TRADE.
7. **The engine's model is in the package `lk.coopfed.knoweb.engine`**, not `engine.model` and `engine.snapshot` (23A section 4): Spring Modulith treats a sub-package of a module as internal, and the engine is pure Kotlin with no way to declare a named interface.
8. **`app_rw` may DELETE from `pricing.price_list_line`** (listed in `SchemaRulesIntegrationTest.DELETE_BY_DESIGN`): SetLines replaces a draft's lines ("upsert draft lines", 23A section 7). The trigger `pricing.line_of_a_draft` refuses a delete once the list is published, so no published line is ever deleted.
9. **The rule vocabulary is Java, not JSON schemas** (M3-05): 23A section 3.1 keeps one JSON schema per kind under `seed/m3/rule-schemas`; `RuleVocabulary` checks each kind in one plain method instead and answers a message id per broken rule, which a fresh reader can follow and the screen can show. The predicate and benefit are stored as the JSON of 23A section 3 with snake_case keys (`sku_id`, `uom_code`, `min_qty`, `days_to_expiry`, `bill_total_from`; `kind`, `value`), decimals as text, as the till will read them.
10. **Rule events, reads and reasons** (M3-05): AuthorRule publishes `discount_rule.drafted.v1` (23A names no event; the build requires one); the reads of rules use `prc.pricelist.view` (23A names no read code); WithdrawRule keeps its reason in the audit record, since 23A's table has no column for it.
11. **SKU scope only for ceilings and policies** (M3-06, M3-07): `control_price` and `mrp_policy` have `sku_id NOT NULL` and no `tag_code`; the exclusion is `(sku_id, daterange)` and the policy's key `(sku_id, owner_entity_id)`. Tag scope needs a governed-tag query M2 does not publish; the migration that brings it adds the column and widens both to 23A's coalesce form. A ceiling matches a line only in the same unit (no unit conversion yet, 23A section 11).
12. **Second factor on every publication** (M3-06): `prc.pricelist.publish` carries the second factor in the catalogue, so RETAIL and ADVISORY publications ask for it as TRADE's do (23A: "mfa for TRADE via handler"). One permission, one rule; a society publishes its shelf list rarely.
13. **Shelf prices are rupees with two decimals, with no tiers** (M3-06): a RETAIL or ADVISORY line is refused with `.retail_precision` or `.tier_not_allowed`. The engine prices a retail line per unit whatever the quantity; a quantity price is a QUANTITY_BREAK rule.
14. **"Assign to shops" is the society** (M3-06): doc 23 section 3.1 (G-01, G-02) has one RETAIL list per MPCS for all its shops and no per-shop deviation, so a list has no location assignment; the retail price at a shop is the owner's list, bounded by that shop's shelf.
15. **Ceilings on the date the line applies**: SetLines checks a draft against the ceilings in force today, PublishPriceList against those in force on `apply_from`. A later gazette below a published line is the gazette review's (M3-08); the engine caps the price meanwhile (`capReason` CONTROL_PRICE).
16. **`GET /v1/pricing/resolve/retail`** is one line, read-only (23A: POST resolveBasket, a whole basket with rules): the shelf price, what the snapshot will carry. The basket form waits for the till's snapshot (M3-09).
17. **Rescission** keeps its reason and the rescinding gazette in the audit record (the table has no column for them) and refuses a last day before yesterday: a ceiling was law on the days it held. EnterControlPrice has no such guard: a gazette entered late is still law from its date.
18. **Lines carried forward** (M3-06): DraftNewVersion copies the source's lines into the draft, dated today until publication (23A section 7). The previous version's lines are still not closed at `apply_from − 1` (the lookups read the newest version in force).
19. **Advisory lines are read by every scope** through a policy `advisory_read` on `price_list` and `price_list_line` (published and superseded ADVISORY versions), and control prices and MRP policies through `everyone_reads` (23A section 3: "control_price, advisory lists and advisory rules readable by every authenticated scope"; the MRP policy so that a society's lookup can fall back to the Federation's row). **The control price is the Federation's in SQL as well as in the handler** (`V0006`; wave 2, RLS-04 and RLS-05; CR-23A-1; `docs/progress/deviations/2026-10-06-wave2-pricing-rules.md` (1), (2)): `own_write` and `own_update` on `control_price` require `owner_entity_id = (SELECT kernel.system_entity())` beside the class and entity tests, `everyone_reads` and both `advisory_read` policies admit the Federation's rows only (a row that is somehow not the Federation's is a ceiling for nobody), and `buyer_read` on `price_list` admits the buyer of an ACTIVE relationship only (a suspended buyer's invoices are the record of the price it was charged). With no system entity configured nobody reads or writes a ceiling: fails closed; every selling environment sets `coop-erp.system.entity-id`. `mrp_policy` is unchanged: a society's manager sets its own. `RlsMatrixIntegrationTest` records `control_price` as `federationRowsOnly`; `RetailPricingPostgresIntegrationTest` reads the Federation's row in every class but NONE and refuses a society's row at the database.

Deferred for the demo (also in `docs/PROGRESS.md` and `docs/PLAN_TO_M2.md`): the closure of the previous version's lines at `apply_from − 1` (the lookup reads the newest version in force instead); units other than the SKU's base unit on a trade line (M2 publishes no conversion query); `pricing.review_raised.v1` for a trade price above the lowest MRP (the line carries the review flag only); WithdrawDraft; the publish fan-out consumer and the snapshot contributor (M3-09: the till gets its prices through the signed snapshot; the resolution stays in the engine, and `RetailPriceResolution` loads the same inputs the contributor will). From M3-06 and M3-07: tag scope and the unit conversion of a ceiling; the gazette review (LIST_ABOVE_CEILING_AT_GAZETTE) and the batch-MRP review (M3-08); M2's consumer of `mrp_policy.changed.v1` (`sku.multi_mrp_policy`); the urgent change-log rows of an entry or a rescission (M3-09). From M3-05: FREE_ITEM rules (the engine does not evaluate them yet); a tag in place of an item in a rule (M2 publishes no tag query); AmendDraftRule; advisory rules and AdoptAdvisoryRule; the rule-expiry job (a rule past `valid_to` stays ACTIVE in the table; the engine checks validity by date); the ceiling check of a FIXED_PRICE rule at activation (with M3-06); the change-log rows of an activation or withdrawal for the owner's shops (with the snapshot contributor, M3-09); a rules screen (M3-10).
