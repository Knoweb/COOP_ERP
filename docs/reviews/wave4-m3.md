## m3pricing/internal/queries: central basket resolution (1)
- **M3R-01 backend/app/src/main/java/lk/coopfed/knoweb/m3pricing/internal/queries/RetailPriceResolution.java:231** [high] [correctness] `resolveBasket` looks the control price up with `controlPrices.ceilingFor(skuId, null, date)`; the store's SQL is `... and ceiling_uom_code = ?`, and `= NULL` never matches, so a gazetted control price is never applied by the central basket resolution. (PR #334)
  Scenario: a control price of Rs 220/KG for rice is in force; the society's shelf line is Rs 240/KG and no lower batch MRP is on the shelf. `POST /v1/pricing/resolve/retail` returns unitPrice 240.00 and `controlPriceApplied` null, while the till (which loads ceilings from the snapshot) caps at 220.00 with `capReason CONTROL_PRICE`. 23A section 6 says central `resolveBasket` "loads the same inputs ... and calls the same function" because ingestion re-validation and M4 rely on it agreeing with the till; this one silently drops the legal ceiling. The single-line `resolve` (line 123) passes the line's UOM and is correct; the PR's only basket test has no control price, so nothing catches it. Fix: look the ceiling up per basket line with that line's UOM (or load every in-force ceiling of the SKU and let the index match by unit, as the snapshot does); add a test with a control price below the list price.
  Verification: **CONFIRMED** by reading. `ControlPriceStore.ceilingFor:72-84` binds `uomCode` straight into `ceiling_uom_code = ?`; with null the predicate is unknown for every row. `resolveBasket` is reached from `PricingController.resolveBasket` (the only caller today).
  Verified by: Claude Opus 5.5 (second pass), 12 Oct 2026. Suggested fix scope: S, no migration, no design decision.

## m3pricing/internal/list: review consumers and the trade review (3)
- **M3R-04 backend/app/src/main/java/lk/coopfed/knoweb/m3pricing/internal/list/ReviewConsumers.java:63-68,109-114** [medium] [correctness] Both consumers compare `max(l.price)` over every unit, tier and published version of a list with a single per-unit figure, ignoring the unit: the gazette consumer ignores the event's `ceilingUomCode`, and the batch consumer compares lines of any unit with the printed MRP. `min(l.uom_code)` is reported next to a `max(l.price)` that may belong to another unit. Only `status = 'PUBLISHED'` is read, so a SUPERSEDED version still in force until the new `apply_from` is ignored. The authoring validator, by contrast, matches the ceiling by unit (`ControlPriceStore.ceilingFor(sku, uom, date)`). (PR #334)
  Scenario: rice is listed per KG at 210 and per 5KG bag at 1,000; a gazette sets 220/KG. `max(price)` is 1,000 > 220, so LIST_ABOVE_CEILING_AT_GAZETTE is raised with `listPrice 1000`, a false review in the exception queue; conversely v1 (in force this week, 150) was SUPERSEDED by v2 (85, from next Monday) and a batch with MRP 90 arrives today: no BATCH_MRP_BELOW_LIST review although the shelf price in force is 150. Fix: compare per (sku, uom) with the ceiling or MRP of the same unit (or convert through M2), and select lines in force on the event's date (or `effective_from >= today`) rather than by status alone.
  Verification: **CONFIRMED** by reading. `ControlPriceEntered` carries `ceilingUomCode` (unused); `PublishPriceListHandler:139-141` marks the previous version SUPERSEDED at publish, not at `apply_from`.
  Verified by: Claude Opus 5.5 (second pass), 12 Oct 2026. Suggested fix scope: S to M, no migration, small decision (which version "in force" means for the review).
- **M3R-05 backend/app/src/main/java/lk/coopfed/knoweb/m3pricing/internal/list/ReviewConsumers.java:127-133** [low] [correctness] The gazette consumer yields one breach per (owner, kind) but de-duplicates on (event type, sku, `controlPriceId`) only, so for an owner with two list kinds above the ceiling (the Federation's ADVISORY and TRADE lists) the first committed review suppresses the second; which one survives depends on the unordered query.
