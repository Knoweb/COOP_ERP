## Fix group A: one credited-quantity rule per invoice line

Findings: M4MONEY-01 (BUILD AMENDED), M4MONEY-02 (BUILD AS SUGGESTED), M4MONEY-04 (BUILD AMENDED), M4MONEY-05 (BUILD AS SUGGESTED), M4MONEY-13 (DECIDE THEN BUILD, built last). One PR, `fix(m4): credit an invoice line once across settlement, claim and credit note`. No migration. Decide first: A-1 (what a DAMAGED claim may cover) and A-2 (partial approval frees the rest), both below.

### Combined fix

1. **A shared read in `internal/invoice`**, say `InvoiceCredits.creditedByLine(invoiceId)`: for every line of the invoice, the sum of `qty`, `line_total` and `tax_amount` over `kernel.document_line` rows whose `reference_line_id` is the invoice line and whose document (`kernel.document`) has `doc_type_code = 'CN'`. Filtering on the type matters: debit notes (#258) will reference the same lines. Reads only (ArchitectureTests), as `InvoiceSettlements` does. The seller's own scope sees every CN it issued, so RLS is not in the way.
2. **Lock before reading**, in every credit path: `documents.lockForLinking(invoiceId)` as soon as the invoice is known and before the invoice lines and the credited quantities are read. Today `requireDue` takes that lock after the lines are priced and the CN is issued, so two credit notes on one invoice in flight at once both see the old credited quantities. `pg_advisory_xact_lock` is re-entrant in one transaction, so `requireDue` (or its successor in group B) keeps locking.
3. **The cap**, `remaining = billed.qty - credited.qty`, applied in each path:
   - `IssueCreditNoteHandler.chosenLines`: a seen-set on `invoiceLineId` (`m4.creditnote.line_duplicate`, new id, three languages); `want.qty() > remaining` refused with a new id `m4.creditnote.exceeds_billed` carrying `skuId`, `billed`, `credited`, `remaining` (one id for all three paths, because the rule is the credit note's; reusing `m4.creditnote.qty_invalid` with the same details is acceptable if the team prefers no new string). Also the scale guard of M4MONEY-04: `qty.stripTrailingZeros().scale() > 3` refused with the existing `m4.creditnote.qty_invalid`, as RaiseClaim and ApproveClaim already do.
   - `SettleDiscrepancyHandler`: `qty = damaged.min(remaining)`; a line with `remaining <= 0` is skipped like a line that was never billed; when every line is skipped the discrepancy settles with no money (the path already exists for a short-only discrepancy, and the audit record says `creditNoteId: null`).
   - `ApproveClaimHandler`: an accepted quantity above `remaining` is refused with `m4.creditnote.exceeds_billed`, not silently capped. Today `qty.min(billed.qty())` can write a `claim_decision_line.approved_qty` of 10 while the credit note credits 7; after the fix the decision and the credit note always agree, and the seller approves less or rejects.
4. **The remainder rule** (M4MONEY-05): extend `IssueCreditNoteHandler.priced` to take the line's remaining quantity, net and tax. When `qty` equals the remaining quantity the credit line's `line_total` is `billed.lineTotal - credited.lineTotal` and its `tax_amount` is `billed.taxAmount - credited.taxAmount`; otherwise the figures are computed as now and capped at the remaining net and tax. `CreditNoteTypeHandler.validate` checks only that unit price, rate and total are present, so the kernel does not recompute the total and the rule holds at issuance.
5. **RaiseClaim's open quantity** (decision A-1): `open = received - damaged_qty (GRN line) - claimedBefore(line)`, for every claim kind. The GRN's damaged quantity is the discrepancy's to credit (SettleDiscrepancy); a claim is for what was found wrong later (the handler's own words). The per-line cap of item 3 is the backstop whichever order the two paths run in.
6. **`ClaimReads.claimedBefore`** (decision A-2, M4MONEY-13): for a claim with an APPROVED decision count `claim_decision_line.approved_qty`, for an undecided claim `claimed_qty`, for a REJECTED one nothing (as today). One subquery. Built only after items 1 to 5 are in, as the finding says.

### Tests the PR must carry

Both orders of settlement and claim on one damaged line (the reproduction scenario of the finding, 13 units on a 10-unit line, refused at the second act); one request naming a line twice; two credit notes that together exceed a line; a quantity of 0.0004 refused; two half credits of a 2 x 0.3350 line credit 0.67 in total (a unit test of `priced` if the fixture prices stay whole rupees); a partial approval of 2 on a claim of 10 followed by a claim of 8 accepted and of 9 refused. Two existing tests change: `ClaimHandlersPostgresIntegrationTest.aRejectedClaimFreesItsQuantity` raises 10 on a GRN captured with damage (`receive(true)`), which item 5 makes 8; `theDecisionWaitsForTheInvoiceAndForEveryPhotograph` is unaffected (`receive(false)`).

### Per finding

- **M4MONEY-01, valid YES.** Re-read: `ClaimReads.claimedBefore` sums claim lines only; `SettleDiscrepancyHandler:171` and `ApproveClaimHandler:156` cap at `billed.qty()`; `requireDue` is invoice-level; the two credit notes carry the same `reference_line_id`. The scenario holds in either order. Suggested fix CORRECT in substance, amended in three places: the lock must move before the reads (item 2), ApproveClaim must refuse rather than cap (item 3), and the GRN's damaged quantity is subtracted for every claim kind, not only DAMAGED (item 5; a WRONG_GOODS claim on units the GRN already recorded as damaged is the same double credit). Best business answer: one rule, "a billed unit is credited once, whoever credits it", which is what #209 promised and what doc 24 section 3.6 ("reduces the invoice's open balance") assumes.
- **M4MONEY-02, valid YES.** `chosenLines` (:147-166) has no seen-set and compares with `billed.qty()`. Fix CORRECT; it is item 3 plus the duplicate guard.
- **M4MONEY-04, valid YES.** `Quantity` (m4trading.yaml:1572) has no scale; `document_line.qty` is `numeric(14,3)`; `priced` multiplies the unrounded value. Fix NEEDS CHANGES: take the guard, not `multipleOf: 0.001`. `multipleOf` on a JSON number is evaluated in floating point by most validators (0.3 is not a multiple of 0.001 in binary), it would change the shared `Quantity` of every M4 operation and regenerate the web client, and the module's own convention is the guard (RaiseClaim :155, ApproveClaim :273).
- **M4MONEY-05, valid YES.** The arithmetic is right; the overshoot is at most half a cent a slice. Fix CORRECT (item 4).
- **M4MONEY-13, valid YES, needs decision A-2.** The query excludes only REJECTED decisions. The finding's doubt (is holding the rest deliberate?) is answered by consistency: RejectClaim frees the whole quantity ("the quantity is free to claim again", README), so a seller who approves 1 of 10 must not lock the other 9 for good while a seller who rejects all 10 frees them. The seller still decides every re-claim, so nothing is forced on it. Fix CORRECT, one subquery, after items 1 to 5.

## Fix group B: credit after the invoice is paid

Finding: M4MONEY-03 (DECIDE THEN BUILD). Needs decision B-1 first. One PR after group A, `feat(m4): a credit note beyond what is due stays unapplied and is applied later`, plus a CR against 24A section 6 and doc 24 section 3.6 recording the decision. No migration.

- **Valid YES.** `requireDue` (InvoiceSettlements:45-52) refuses any credit above `gross - credited - settled`; after a cash receipt both SettleDiscrepancy (:193) and ApproveClaim (:171) throw `m4.creditnote.exceeds_due`, and SettleDiscrepancy being one transaction also blocks the short quantity on the same discrepancy. Recorded as decision (7) of `2026-09-29-feat-m4-payments-credit-limit.md`; ApplyCreditNote "still deferred" in `2026-09-28-feat-m4-short-delivery-credit-note.md`. A paid invoice with damaged goods is a dead end today.
- **Suggested fix: option (a) CORRECT, but it needs no migration.** The verifier expected "an unapplied amount on `doc_credit_note`, or an allocation table". Neither is needed: doc 24 section 3.6 already says an unapplied credit note "is applied later with a link", and `kernel.document_link` keys on `(from, to, link_type)`, so one CN can carry a CREDITS link to its origin invoice and another to a later invoice. The unapplied amount of a credit note is then a sum, `gross - Σ CREDITS links from the CN`, never stored (AGENTS.md: balances are sums).
- **Recommended decision B-1** (written for recording on the architect's delegation): *A credit note is issued for the goods in full. Its money is applied to the invoice it credits only as far as that invoice is still due: the CREDITS link carries `min(gross, due)` and is not written when nothing is due (the kernel requires a positive amount). What is not applied is the credit note's unapplied amount, `gross - Σ its CREDITS links`; it lowers the buyer's exposure as 24A section 6.3's `unappliedCredits`, and the seller's accounts apply it to another open, undisputed invoice of the same pair with ApplyCreditNote (24A section 6; `bil.creditnote.issue`), a CREDITS link from the credit note to that invoice under `lockForLinking` and the kernel's open-balance check, recomputing that invoice's `credited_amount`. Nothing is refunded: version 1 records money and never moves it (ADR-01); a refund, when the buyer wants one, is a matter outside the system and is recorded, if at all, as the next payment receipt's reference. `doc_credit_note.invoice_document_id` keeps naming the origin invoice, whose lines the credit note's lines reference.* Alternatives rejected: a refund path (moves money, ADR-01); keeping the refusal (a dead end with no record of what the buyer is owed, and it blocks the short-quantity settlement on the same discrepancy); an on-account credit balance table (a stored balance); a negative payment receipt (misuse of the PRC and of its postings).
- **Build.** `InvoiceSettlements.requireDue` becomes `applyUpToDue(invoiceId, gross)` returning the amount linked (0 to gross); the three CN paths write the link only when the amount is positive, and `creditedFromLinks` stays as it is (it sums links, so the cache keeps meaning "applied to this invoice"). `CreditNoteView` and `InvoiceView` gain `appliedAmount` and `unappliedAmount`. `ExposureCalculator` subtracts the seller's unapplied credits to the buyer (the 24A formula already has the term; README item 24 and the class comment change). A new `ApplyCreditNoteHandler` (`POST /v1/trading/credit-notes/{id}/apply`, Idempotency-Key, `x-permission: bil.creditnote.issue`; guards: the seller's own issued CN, an open undisputed invoice of the same seller and buyer, `0 < amount ≤ min(unapplied, due)`; audit CREDIT_NOTE_APPLIED; event `credit_note.applied.v1`, which 24A names). The web credit-note screen shows applied and unapplied, the invoice screen offers "apply a credit note" when the pair has one. `PaymentHandlersPostgresIntegrationTest:346` (asserting `exceeds_due`) becomes a test of the unapplied amount. The seller-side postings do not change: CN GOODS SELLER debits REVENUE and VAT_OUTPUT and credits RECEIVABLE whether or not the money is applied, which is a credit balance on the receivable, as an accountant books it.
- Size M, risk low to medium (exposure and the credit-note screens change; the kernel does not). Needs group A's lock ordering first so the application and the per-line cap run under the same invoice lock.

## Fix group C: one lock per order

Findings: M4MONEY-06 (BUILD AMENDED), M4MONEY-07 (BUILD AS SUGGESTED), M4MONEY-12 (BUILD AS SUGGESTED). One PR, `fix(m4): one advisory lock per order shared by both parties; sorted locks`. No migration, no decision.

### Combined fix

1. One helper, say `OrderLocks.lock(jdbc, orderId)` in `internal/order` (public, used by `internal/delivery` too): `select pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext('order-' || ?))`. All callers use it, so the key moves from the bare `order.id().toString()` of `OrderDecision` to the prefixed form without anyone left on the old key.
2. **AcceptOrder and RejectOrder** (`OrderDecision.requireUndecided`): take the lock first, then re-read the order with `documents.findById` and check SUBMITTED, then read `order_allocation`. Today the status is read before the lock.
3. **AmendOrder and CancelOrder**: after `guards.ownOrder` (the buyer's row lock on its own document, which stays), take the order lock, then read `order_allocation`, and in Cancel the `fulfilled_qty` sum.
4. **IssueDeliveryNote**: collect the distinct order ids of the note's lines (`DeliveryReads.orderIds(drops)`), sort them (UUID's natural order), lock each, then run `AllocatedLines.find(..., true)` as now. `CreateDeliveryNote` needs no lock: its check is advisory and Issue re-checks.
5. **IssueInvoice** (M4MONEY-12): `grnIds` a `TreeSet<UUID>` (or sorted list) before the lock loop; the duplicate-invoice query is unchanged.

### Why this cannot deadlock

Accept, Reject, Amend and Cancel each take exactly one order lock. Amend and Cancel take a row lock on the buyer's own `kernel.document` row before it; Accept and Reject take no row lock the buyer's side ever waits on (the seller inserts its own rows, and V0001 has no foreign key from `order_allocation` to the document). IssueDeliveryNote is the only transaction holding several order locks, and it takes them in sorted order, then row locks on its own `order_allocation_line` rows and the issuance lock on its own DN document; Accept inserts those same allocation rows only while holding the order's lock, so the two serialise on the order lock and never hold each other's row. Two IssueDeliveryNotes over overlapping orders lock in the same order. The kernel's `lockForLinking` uses `hashtextextended` (a bigint key space) and the order lock `hashtext` (two int4 keys), so they cannot collide. `hashtext` collisions between two order ids are a serialisation, not a deadlock.

### Per finding

- **M4MONEY-06, valid YES.** `requireUndecided` tests the status from an unlocked `findById` before the advisory lock; Amend and Cancel never take that lock. Both commit. Fix NEEDS CHANGES: the "smallest change" (`findByIdForUpdate` in `sellersOrder`) cannot work. `kernel.document` has `own_update ... USING (scope_class() = 'OWN' AND owner_entity_id = scope_entity())` (V0050:200), and a `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` under RLS returns only rows that pass the UPDATE policy, so the seller's `findByIdForUpdate` of the buyer's order returns empty and `orElseThrow` turns into a 500. The kernel says so itself in `lockForLinking`'s comment (a row lock on a document the caller does not own needs the UPDATE policy, hence the advisory key). The advisory lock on both sides, as the finding's first sentence proposes, is the fix.
- **M4MONEY-07, valid YES.** Cancel sums `fulfilled_qty` unlocked (:81-84); `AllocatedLines.find` reads the order status with `findById` (:53-59); the two take no common lock. Fix CORRECT (items 3 and 4). Note the kernel's compare-and-set in `addStateTransition` does not help here: IssueDeliveryNote never moves the order's status.
- **M4MONEY-12, valid YES.** `LinkedHashSet` keeps request order (:119), the lock is in the loop (:140), the kernel maps no SQLState 40P01 (no handler found). Fix CORRECT (item 5).

Size S, risk low (lock-only changes; the two-thread reproduction tests the verifier wrote are the tests to add, plus a sorted-lock unit check).

## Fix group D: payments from the right party, each cheque once

Findings: M4MONEY-08 (DECIDE THEN BUILD, small), M4MONEY-09 (DECIDE THEN BUILD). One PR, `fix(m4): record payments from a suspended buyer; refuse a cheque recorded already`, with a CR against 24A section 3 (the cheque rule) and a note to doc 24 section 3.7. Decisions D-1 and D-2 first. No migration required; an optional index migration.

- **M4MONEY-08, valid YES.** M1's `lookupRelationship` filters `status = 'ACTIVE'` itself (RelationshipQueriesImpl:52), so the `.filter(ACTIVE)` in the handler (:137) is redundant and a SUSPENDED buyer's money is refused. Doc 21 section 4 (line 277): SuspendRelationship, "open documents unaffected". Correction to the finding: M1 has no TERMINATED status (V0011: DRAFT, ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, REPLACED); a relationship that ended has `effective_to` in the past. Fix NEEDS CHANGES, in the small: neither of the two suggestions covers a receipt with no settlements (money on account, no invoice to take a `relationship_id` from), so the cleanest is the second suggestion made precise. **Decision D-1 (recommended):** *A payment is recorded against the pair's relationship row of status ACTIVE or SUSPENDED in force on the day received, else the pair's most recent ACTIVE or SUSPENDED row (a relationship that ended with invoices still open); DRAFT and REPLACED rows never qualify. `m4.payment.no_relationship` stays for a pair that never traded. The ACTIVE check remains for new trading (CreateOrder, AcceptOrder, AmendOrder), not for settlement.* Build: an additive query on M1's published contract, `RelationshipQueries.settlementRelationship(seller, buyer, onDate, scope)` (name to taste), with its RLS matrix rows; the handler calls it; the chosen-settlement path already requires the invoices to be the pair's. Size S, risk low (a published query contract gains a method; nothing removed).
- **M4MONEY-09, valid YES.** `trading.cheque` is keyed by receipt only (V0006:43-48); the handler inserts with no look-up (:170-177); 24A's DDL has no constraint either. Fix CORRECT in shape (a guard under an advisory lock, no unique index, because a bounced cheque is re-presented with the same bank, number and date, and "bounced" lives in `cheque_outcome`). **Decision D-2 (recommended):** *The key of a cheque is (seller, payer, bank, cheque number), with the bank compared as `upper(btrim(bank))` and the number as `ltrim(btrim(cheque_no), '0')`; `dated` is not part of it (the same number with another date from the same drawer is a mistake or worse, and a replacement cheque has a new number). A cheque is recorded again only when every earlier receipt of this seller from this payer carrying it has a BOUNCED outcome; otherwise `m4.payment.cheque_recorded` (new id, three languages) naming the earlier receipt. The look-up runs under `pg_advisory_xact_lock` on the key. Doc 10 item 6 (cheque headroom provisional; bounce = reversal + flag) stands.* Including the payer in the key is not cosmetic: two drawers at one bank can hold the same number (different accounts), and the demo loader (`DemoPayments.cheque`) issues `400100 + invoice index` in two lanes with different sellers and payers, which a seller-only key would refuse on a fresh load. Reversal receipts (`reversal_of is not null`) carry no cheque row and are skipped by the join anyway. Build: the guard in `RecordPaymentReceiptHandler` after the cheque fields are checked; a test with two receipts of one cheque (refused), a bounce then a re-presentation (accepted), and two payers with one number (accepted). An expression index on `trading.cheque` is optional (V0009 if wanted; 75 distributors and 800 societies do not need it yet). Size S, risk low.

## Fix group E: the buyer's journal

Finding: M4MONEY-10 (recorded deferral). Two parts.

- **Valid YES.** Every `JournalPostingsReady` is published with the seller as owner, and the owner is not a choice: `OutboxWriter:70` stamps the outbox row with `kernel.scope_entity()` of the session, and `EventConsumerDispatcher:158` runs consumers in that owner's scope. So the seller's handlers cannot publish the buyer's postings, and M9's `JournalPostingsConsumer` records in the owner's scope only. The map rows `GRN GOODS BUYER` and `INV GOODS BUYER` (posting-map.yaml:15-17, 24A section 3.1 lines 310-314) have no producer. Doc 24 section 3.9: "GRN at the buyer: inventory/payable".
- **Part 1, the GRN side: BUILD AS SUGGESTED, S, no migration, no decision.** `ConfirmGrnHandler` runs in the buyer's (receiver's) scope. After the audit records it publishes `JournalPostingsReady(grnId, "GRN", number, receiverEntityId, postings.postings("GRN", "GOODS", "BUYER", Map.of("cost", Σ received_qty × unit_cost)))` when the total is above zero. `PostingMapper` reads the amount by `amount_source` name (:41), so `cost` works. M9 dedupes by `document_id` within the owner's RLS, and a GRN id belongs to one entity, so no collision. For a GRN from a local supplier (no M4 seller) the posting is equally the buyer's purchase and is published too. Test: `kernel.committedEvents()` carries the posting with the right owner and amount.
- **Part 2, the invoice and credit-note side: DECIDE THEN BUILD, M, migration yes.** Decision E-1 (recommended): *The buyer's books record a supplier invoice or credit note when the buyer's accounts acknowledge it, by a command of the buyer's own, `AcknowledgeTradingDocument(documentId)` (INV or CN of which the caller is the counterparty, issued, not acknowledged before), permission `bil.invoice.acknowledge` (new, ENTITY, in `seed/m1party/permissions.yaml` and the Trading Buyer role), with the buyer's row `trading.document_acknowledgement (document_id PK, acknowledged_by, acknowledged_at, owner_entity_id, counterparty_entity_id)` on the party_read template (V0009 or the next free number), audit TRADING_DOCUMENT_ACKNOWLEDGED, events `invoice.acknowledged.v1` / `credit_note.acknowledged.v1` and `journal.postings_ready.v1` in the buyer's scope with the INV GOODS BUYER rows (GRN_ACCRUAL/PAYABLE net, VAT_INPUT/PAYABLE tax) or new CN GOODS BUYER rows (PAYABLE/INVENTORY net, PAYABLE/VAT_INPUT tax, added to the map). The buyer-side rows of a payment receipt (bank/payable) are not published: the buyer's cash book is its own record and doc 24 section 3.9 lists PRC on the seller's side only; this is left to the accountant's review of the roles (doc 10 A-01).* Alternatives rejected: a consumer of `invoice.issued.v1` in the buyer's scope (the kernel dispatches a consumer in the event owner's scope only, so it would be a kernel change to the event contract for one module's need); the seller publishing the buyer's postings (idea 3, and the outbox would not let it); removing the map rows (the design wants them, and the acknowledgement is how accounts departments work: the payable is booked when the invoice is received and checked against the GRN). One check for the builder: M9's `RecordJournalPostingsHandler` dedupes by `document_id` alone (:74-79); the seller's and the buyer's postings of one invoice share the id and are kept apart only by `journal_posting`'s own_read policy. Make the dedupe `(owner_entity_id, document_id)` explicitly, or at least test that both sides are held. Size M (command, migration, slice, seed, web button, M9 check), risk medium (new permission and seed, a migration).

## Fix group F: dispute order by sequence

Finding: M4MONEY-11 (BUILD AS SUGGESTED). `fix(m4): order an invoice's dispute acts by sequence, not clock`. Migration yes, no decision.

- **Valid YES.** `InvoiceDisputes.latest` orders by `recorded_at desc, dispute_event_id desc` (:32); both are the writing instance's clock. The two acts serialise under the `invoice-dispute-` advisory lock, so only skew between instances shows it. Low is right.
- **Fix CORRECT.** V0009 (or the next free number): `ALTER TABLE trading.invoice_dispute ADD COLUMN seq bigint GENERATED ALWAYS AS IDENTITY;` and `CREATE INDEX invoice_dispute_invoice_seq ON trading.invoice_dispute (invoice_document_id, seq);`. PostgreSQL fills the identity for existing rows when the column is added; on an insert-only table that is insert order. The inserts in Dispute and Resolve name no `seq`, so they need no change; `latest` orders by `seq desc`. `recorded_at` stays for display. The `clock_timestamp()` alternative is cheaper but still a clock; AGENTS.md says sequence numbers order events. Size S, risk low; no `make reset` (a new migration only).

## Fix group G: exposure per order line

Finding: M4MONEY-14 (BUILD AMENDED). `fix(m4): count the uninvoiced part of each accepted order line in the exposure`. No migration; no decision beyond confirming the in-transit rule below.

- **Valid YES, recorded** (README item 24; deviation (3) of 29 Sep). The `not exists` in `acceptedNotInvoiced` (:144-148) is per order. The reference chain for a per-line rule exists: invoice line `reference_line_id` → GRN kernel line → `reference_line_id` → `doc_delivery_line.line_id` → `order_line_id` (CaptureGrnHandler:157 writes the delivery line as the GRN line's reference).
- **Fix NEEDS CHANGES.** 24A's `allocated - fulfilledInvoiced` taken literally as `allocated - invoiced` over-reports for good: an order line allocated 10, dispatched 10, received and invoiced 8 (short 2) would keep 2 x tier price in the exposure for ever, although DR-2 says the short quantity is never owed (the README's own caveat). Per order line, the uninvoiced value is `(allocated_qty - fulfilled_qty)` (not yet dispatched) plus, for each delivery line of the order line, `dispatched_qty` while no confirmed GRN names its drop, else `received_qty - invoiced_qty` of the GRN line (0 once invoiced), all floored at 0 and priced at `tier_price`; a cancelled order still owes nothing. Size M (one query, a test with two notes on one order and a short delivery), risk low (a warning-only figure, ADR-12).

- **A-1 (M4MONEY-01).** A claim may cover only units the GRN did not already record as damaged: RaiseClaim's open quantity is `received - damaged_qty - claimedBefore` for every claim kind; the GRN's damaged quantity is credited by SettleDiscrepancy alone. Rejected: letting a DAMAGED claim cover GRN-damaged units with the per-line cap as the only stop (two paths for one credit, and the buyer is told "exceeds billed" after the seller has already settled).
- **A-2 (M4MONEY-13).** A partial approval decides the approved quantity only; the rest is free to claim again, as a rejection frees the whole. Rejected: holding the un-approved quantity for good (inconsistent with RejectClaim; the escalation path that would be the remedy is not built).
- **B-1 (M4MONEY-03).** The credit note stays whole; its money is linked to the origin invoice up to what is due, the rest is the credit note's unapplied amount (a sum over its links), subtracted from exposure and applied later by ApplyCreditNote with a CREDITS link; no refund in v1 (ADR-01). CR against 24A section 6 and doc 24 section 3.6. Rejected: refund; keep the refusal; a credit balance table; a negative receipt.
- **D-1 (M4MONEY-08).** Settlement needs the pair's relationship of any status but DRAFT or REPLACED, in force on the day or the most recent; the ACTIVE check stays for new trading. Rejected: refusing a suspended buyer's money (the suspension exists because it owes); taking the relationship from an invoice (fails for money on account).
- **D-2 (M4MONEY-09).** A cheque is (seller, payer, normalised bank, normalised number), recorded once unless every earlier receipt carrying it bounced; `dated` is not in the key. CR against 24A section 3. Rejected: a unique index (cannot see `cheque_outcome`); a seller-only key (two drawers at one bank; the demo loader).
- **E-1 (M4MONEY-10).** The buyer's invoice and credit-note postings are published by the buyer's own acknowledgement command, in the buyer's scope, with CN BUYER rows added to the map; the GRN's buyer posting is published by ConfirmGrn without a decision; PRC buyer rows are deferred to the accountant's review (A-01). CR against 24A sections 3.1 and 6 and doc 24 section 3.9. Rejected: a counterparty-scoped consumer (kernel change); the seller publishing for the buyer (idea 3, and the outbox stamps the session's entity).
