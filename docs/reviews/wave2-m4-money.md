## m4trading/internal/claim + internal/invoice: credit notes (5)
- **M4MONEY-01 backend/app/src/main/java/lk/coopfed/knoweb/m4trading/internal/claim/RaiseClaimHandler.java:158** [high] [data integrity] **CONFIRMED** The same damaged goods can be credited twice: once by SettleDiscrepancy and again by a DAMAGED claim. A claim's open quantity is `received - claimedBefore`, and it ignores the damaged quantity the GRN already recorded. ApproveClaim (`ApproveClaimHandler.java:151-156`) and SettleDiscrepancy (`SettleDiscrepancyHandler.java:171`) each cap only at `billed.qty()`, not at what earlier credit notes already credited on that invoice line. (PR #209, #232)
  Scenario: invoice line A bills 10 x 100 (VAT 18%), and a line B bills 10 x 500, so the invoice is 7,080 due. The GRN recorded 3 of A as damaged, so the discrepancy is raised and the seller settles it with CN 354.00. The buyer then raises a DAMAGED claim for 10 of A, because open = 10 - 0. The seller approves it in full and CN 1,180.00 is issued. Line A has now been credited 13 units (1,534.00) against 1,180.00 billed. `requireDue` only checks the invoice total, so both credit notes pass. #209's "no double credit" promise does not hold across the discrepancy and claim paths. Fix: track credited quantity per invoice line, as the sum of CN lines whose `reference_line_id` is the invoice line. Every credit note path (IssueCreditNote, SettleDiscrepancy, ApproveClaim) caps at `billed.qty - creditedQty(line)`. RaiseClaim's open quantity should also subtract the GRN line's `damaged_qty` once a discrepancy carries it, or the quantity a discrepancy settlement credited.
  Verification: the whole chain holds by reading. `doc_grn_line` has CHECK `damaged_qty <= received_qty` (V0001:179), and IssueInvoice bills `receivedQty` (`IssueInvoiceHandler.java:189`), so damaged units are billed. SettleDiscrepancy credits `damaged_qty` (capped only at `billed.qty`). `ClaimReads.claimedBefore` (:126) sums earlier claim lines only, never the discrepancy's damaged quantity or any credit note. ApproveClaim prices `qty.min(billed.qty())` with no look at earlier CN lines. The two credit notes link to the same invoice line through `reference_line_id`, and the only shared check is the invoice-level `requireDue`. The path also works in the other order: the claim is approved first, then the discrepancy is settled. Reproduction test `m4money01_damagedGoodsCreditedTwice`, with the fixture of 10 rice at 120 (2 damaged) and 4 dhal: CN 283.20 by the settlement, then a claim for all 10 rice approved for CN 1,416.00. 12 units are credited on a 10-unit line (1,699.20 against 1,416.00 billed).
  Suggested fix scope: no migration. The credited quantity per invoice line is a sum over `kernel.document_line.reference_line_id` of the issued CN lines, readable today; a helper in InvoiceSettlements used by all three paths, taken under the invoice's `lockForLinking`. RaiseClaim's open quantity needs a small design decision: whether a DAMAGED claim may cover units the GRN already recorded as damaged at all. The simplest rule is `received - damaged_on_grn - claimedBefore` for DAMAGED claims, with the per-line credit cap as the backstop either way. Fix together with M4MONEY-02 and -05.

  Verification: the behaviour holds as described.   Suggested fix scope: needs a design decision (architect), probably a CR against 24A/doc 24.
