Cooperative Retail and Distribution System
05  -  Software Requirements Specification
Baseline requirements package  --  document 5 of 10
Requirements are phrased in EARS form (When <trigger>, the system shall <response>) per ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 practice. Each carries a unique ID (domain-prefixed), a delivery phase (P1 core spine / P2 control & reporting / P3 extensions), and the question reference it traces to. This is a working baseline, not exhaustive to every question in the register  --  see Document 8 for full question-level traceability.1. Party and Relationship Management
