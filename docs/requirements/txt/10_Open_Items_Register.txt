The 13-item ratification batch
Presented to stakeholders as defaults that stand unless objected to  --  not as open questions to be redesigned. Each already has design rationale recorded against it in Document 9.
 1. G-02 deviation = per-bill discount only, no standing shop price override
 2. Weighted-average costing as an entity-level policy, never per-transaction
 3. Repack reversal permitted only while output stock remains unsold
 4. Recipe-first repacking; ad-hoc conversion needs higher permission
 5. Recipes shareable via the same promote-to-shared flow as SKUs
 6. Cheque receipts release credit headroom provisionally; bounce = reversal + flag
 7. Phase-1 customer credit: no interest, no guarantors
 8. NIC optional, and only for customers granted credit
 9. Offline write-offs captured as drafts, approved online
 10. Idle-lock defaults: till 2 - 3 min / back office 10 - 15 min / admin 5 min
 11. Cash-tender rounding to nearest rupee; card/account tenders exact
 12. English short name mandatory on every SKU
 13. No discretionary POS discount of any kind  --  rule engine only (ADR-14)
