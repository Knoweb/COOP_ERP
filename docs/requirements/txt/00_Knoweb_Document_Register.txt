00  -  Knoweb Document Register
v0.5  --  18 September 2026  --  replaces v0.4 (10 September, PDF) and v0.2 (14 August). Live document.
Index of every document of the requirements baseline and the design phase: file, version, status and what it contains. Numbers are identifiers, not reading order. Versioning convention: files are never edited in place on Drive; a superseded version is replaced by a new versioned file and the old one is moved to trash. Design and implementation documents are issued as PDF; the live registers (00, 10) and the folder entry point (00_Start_Here) are Word documents so they can be edited in place. This register decides which version is current whenever two documents disagree.

3. Repository mirror
From 17 September 2026 every document above is also committed to the code repository: PDFs and Word files under docs/requirements/ and docs/design/, read-only text extracts under the txt/ subfolders, and Markdown sources for any document revised or created thereafter under docs/sources/. The repository's docs/README.md holds the operating instructions; docs/DECISIONS_PENDING.md is the Markdown source of document 10; AGENTS.md at the root carries the standing instructions for the AI coding tools the team uses.

5. Status notes and change control
Documents 01 - 13 are stable baseline content pending federation sign-off of the 3 September 2026 change requests; they change only by change request with Federation sign-off. Design documents are drafts until the Stage 5 review; a module's published contract (design §5) freezes when a dependant designs against it and changes thereafter by change request. Document 10 is the live register, updated at every Stage 2 gate and the Stage 4 and 5 reviews; document 09 records every closed decision; this register records every version change. The Ratification Defaults RFC (doc 10 §2) is still to be tabled; the Numbering RFC closed with 24B.
