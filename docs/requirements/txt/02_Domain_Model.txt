The only mechanism by which a bill may ever be discounted.3. Key relationships and cardinalities
 An MPCS or Distributor has many Shops (1-->N). A Shop belongs to exactly one MPCS or Distributor.
 A Trading relationship links exactly two entities and carries exactly one active price list at a time, effective-dated. An entity may hold many trading relationships (a distributor trades with COOPFED and with many MPCS).
 A SKU is owned by exactly one entity but may be traded by any entity holding stock of it. A SKU has many Batches, many Barcodes, and may appear as input or output in many Repack recipes.
 A Batch belongs to exactly one SKU and exactly one holding entity/location. Selling price is not a batch attribute  --  it lives at SKU x MPCS; only cost and MRP are batch-level.
 A Customer has exactly one global identity and zero-or-more entity-scoped Accounts (one per MPCS it trades with), and zero-or-more Phones (many-to-one to the identity, since numbers are reused and reassigned in practice).
 A Numbering series belongs to exactly one till position (for till-issued documents) or exactly one entity + document type pair (for back-office documents)  --  never to a physical device.
4. Patterns applied consistently
 Shared master data: global namespace, per-entity ownership and status  --  applied to SKUs, and reusable for repack recipes.
 Global identity, local account: shared identity, separate account and balance per counterparty  --  applied to Customers, and structurally identical to how a Distributor's credit relationship with COOPFED works.
 Append-only documents: every document is created once, transitions through defined statuses, and is corrected only by a new document that references it. Nothing is ever silently overwritten.
 Entity-scoped visibility: who may see what is a property of the relationship between two entities (or entity and shop), never a global default. Detailed in the Matrices document.
