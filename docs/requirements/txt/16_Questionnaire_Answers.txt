Before reading: how the model evolved after the questionnaire

The questionnaire was drafted early, and four of its framings were superseded by what stakeholders subsequently
clarified. Reading the answers requires knowing these shifts:

   · One structure, not two parties: the questionnaire speaks of "central and shops" as a two-party structure.
       The real structure is four tiers: COOPFED at the apex, ~75 distributors (themselves large MPCS), ~800
       MPCS, and shops -- and a shop is a location of its MPCS or distributor, not a legal entity. Questions asked
       about "a shop" are answered at the legally correct level, usually the MPCS.

   · Firm sale won: A-01 resolved as firm sale, with ownership and risk transferring at GRN confirmation.
       Consignment appears in the questionnaire as a live option; it is not.

   · No members: K-01 resolved as no -- cooperative membership is entirely out of scope. The system knows
       "registered customers" (a retail-credit concept) instead of members.

   · SVAT is gone: SVAT, listed in the tax questions, was abolished with effect from 1 October 2025. Only
       standard VAT exists in the design.
