Cooperative Retail and Distribution System
12  -  Domain Glossary
Baseline requirements package  --  document 12  --  LIVE DOCUMENT
The ubiquitous language. Use these exact terms in code, tests, commits, and UI copy  --  the definition here is the meaning IN THIS SYSTEM, which sometimes differs from general usage. Propose additions via the same review path as any document change.Structure
TermMeaning in this systemCOOPFED / FederationThe apex legal entity. Sees all data view-only; stewards the shared SKU catalogue; holds no admin rights over other entities' users.DistributorA large-scale MPCS trading between COOPFED and ordinary MPCS. Holds a deposit with COOPFED that sets its credit headroom.MPCSMulti-purpose cooperative society  --  a legal entity owning shops and optionally a central store. Sets one retail price list for all its shops.ShopA trading location of an MPCS or distributor. NOT a legal entity. Cannot see sibling shops' data.Entity vs locationThe system's core distinction: inter-entity movement = commercial sale with invoice; intra-entity movement = transfer at cost, no invoice.Trading relationshipA configured link between two entities carrying its own price list and credit settings. Tiers are data, not schema.Back-office organisationThe administrative presence of an entity  --  users, roles, inter-entity documents. 876 exist today.
Documents and lifecycle
TermMeaning in this systemDocumentAn immutable business record (order, GRN, invoice...). Final at issue; corrected only by a new linked document, never edited.GRNGoods received note. Confirmation transfers ownership and risk to the receiver  --  the pivot event of the trading model.Direct dropDelivery where bill-to is the MPCS but ship-to is one of its shops; shop staff sign the GRN on the MPCS's behalf.Order lockPoint (configurable window before ETA) after which the buyer can no longer amend or cancel.BackorderThe unfulfilled remainder of a partially fulfilled order, left open unless cancelled.Payment receiptSeller-posted record that money was received against invoice(s). Releases headroom. The system never processes actual payment.Cheque (provisional)A payment receipt that releases headroom provisionally; a bounce triggers a gated reversal and flags the relationship.ClaimDamage/shortage/quality dispute against a delivery or batch, with photo evidence; approval yields a credit note.VoidCancellation of an issued document. It keeps its number  --  void is a status, never a deletion.Reversal / compensating documentThe universal correction mechanism: a new document that restores prior state and references what it corrects.
Catalogue and inventory
TermMeaning in this systemSKUSmallest distinctly tracked item. Global namespace; owned by one entity; status draft --> local --> shared; meaningless system code; English short name mandatory.Alias mappingLink preserving a local SKU's sales history when it is promoted to a shared SKU.BatchA stock lot of one SKU at one holder. Carries cost, MRP, expiry. Cost and MRP live here  --  never on the SKU.MRPGovernment maximum retail price  --  a batch attribute. Selling price is validated against the MRP of the batch being depleted.FEFOFirst-expired-first-out  --  default depletion assumption for expiry-relevant SKUs, manually overridable.Weighted average costThe costing method, set as an entity-level policy. No per-transaction choice.UOM conversionFactor between units of the SAME product (case <--> single). One SKU, no transformation.Repack recipeDirectional many-to-many BOM turning input SKU(s) into a different output SKU with expected yield. Recipe-first; ad-hoc conversion is permission-gated and exception-flagged.Weigh stickerIn-store printed barcode (GS1 prefix 20 - 29) encoding weight/price for loose goods; manual weight entry is the no-printer fallback.OversellNegative-stock sale, permitted with cashier acknowledgement (or offline race), reconciled and flagged  --  never blocked.
POS, offline and numbering
TermMeaning in this systemTill positionA logical checkout lane at a shop (S03-T1). Owns its numbering series. Survives device replacement  --  series follows the position, not the hardware.Gapless seriesStrictly sequential document numbers per till position (or per entity + doc type for back office). Assigned at issue from a local counter; never reused.Posting referenceCentral's internal ID added to a synced document  --  metadata only, never printed, never replaces the till number.Gap checkSync-completeness test: a missing number in a till series signals a sale event that has not arrived.One-writer ruleEach record type has exactly one authoritative writer (till: its sale events; central: catalogue/prices). Eliminates merge logic entirely.Local cacheTill-resident copy of catalogue, prices, rules, tax categories, batch/MRP data, tagged customers, permissions  --  everything needed to sell offline.Park / resumeSuspending an in-progress sale and recalling it  --  works offline.Blind closeTill closing where the cashier declares counted cash before seeing the system figure.Z-reportEnd-of-day till summary, printed on the local thermal printer, offline-capable.
Customers and credit
TermMeaning in this systemRegistered customerA person/business with a system identity  --  NOT a cooperative member. Membership, shares, patronage are out of scope.Customer account (khata)Per-MPCS credit account under one global identity: limit, balance, repayments, ageing. No interest or guarantors in phase 1.Phone as lookupPhone numbers find a customer but never key one  --  numbers churn and are reassigned. Internal ID is the key; suspected reuse prompts staff confirmation, never auto-merge.Committed exposureOpen invoices + accepted-but-undelivered orders  --  the figure checked against a credit limit.Warn-not-blockCredit breaches show a banner; trade is never stopped on derived exposure.Discount ruleTarget + trigger + benefit + validity + constraints. The ONLY way any price reduction happens at a till.Free item lineRule benefit posted as a zero-priced line that deducts stock and carries the rule ID.MarkdownRule-driven price reduction for expiry/damage bands  --  same engine as discounts.
Control and governance
TermMeaning in this systemDual control / SoDSegregation of duties: requester != approver, enforced hardest on write-offs.Write-offPermission-gated disposal of loss with witness and, where practical, photo. Offline capture = draft; approval is online.Audit trailAppend-only record of every privileged action, per the catalogue in doc 07. Hash-chaining arrives in phase 2.Visibility matrixWho sees what across the hierarchy (doc 07 §2). Political boundaries  --  treat as hard requirements.Ring deploymentPOS update rollout: pilot --> wave cohorts --> fleet, with automatic rollback on health-check failure.Version floorMinimum client version the server accepts, enforced after a grace period.Phase / wave / pilot gatePhase = functional scope (1: spine, 2: control & reporting, 3: extensions). Wave = geographic rollout group. Pilot gate = go/no-go at ~40% spend.
