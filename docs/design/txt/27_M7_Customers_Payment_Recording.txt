Module design for the registered customer and the digitised khata: one global identity, a
credit account per MPCS with limit, balance and ageing, account postings from till
tenders and repayments, customer payment receipts, statements, the banking record of
takings, and the privacy obligations that come with holding people's data.

What this is and is not. A registered customer is a retail-credit concept ­ a person a society has agreed to trust for a
khata ­ and has nothing to do with cooperative membership, shares or votes (ADR-16, K-01). The khata is frequently
the largest receivable a society carries (I-02), so it gets the same discipline as the inter-entity ledger: postings are
append-only, balances are sums, and nothing is edited. Because these are people's records, the module holds the
minimum and knows how to give it back or take it away (ADR-27, PDPA).

3. Domain model

Schema customers. Customer payment receipts use the kernel document base with an M7 extension; they do
not reuse M4's trading.doc_payment_receipt (rule R4: no cross-schema writes). Doc 24A's payer_kind
= CUSTOMER branch is therefore withdrawn (change request CR-27-2).

9. Non-functional design

Aspect             Design
9.1 Capacity
                   Customers: hundreds of thousands federation-wide; accounts per MPCS: hundreds to a few thousand; postings:
9.2 Offline        one per account tender or repayment, partitioned monthly. The customer snapshot for a shop carries the MPCS's
                   accounts (a few thousand rows at most).
9.3 Privacy
                   Lookup, account tender within cap, and repayment capture work from the snapshot; registration and account
9.4 Controls       changes are online. Balance shown offline is the last synced balance minus local tenders plus local repayments
9.5 Visibility     (provisional).

                   Minimal data; NIC as salted hash plus last four; phone values never in event payloads or logs (ids only); customer
                   snapshot encrypted at rest on tills; access and erasure workflows; responsible officer per entity; every read of a
                   customer record outside the sales path is audited (accounts screens).

                   Postings insert-only; limit increases MFA; adjustments SoD (request  approve) and MFA; reversals MFA;
                   banking shortfalls REVIEW; offline limit breaches REVIEW.

                   A customer's data is visible to the registering MPCS and, for the account, to that MPCS only; another MPCS
                   opening an account for the same person sees the identity (name, phone) but not the other society's balance
                   (M-05 spirit); Federation view-only.
