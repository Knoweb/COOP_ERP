      1. Modelling principles

      Ten rules govern every table in this model. Where a table departs from one, the departure is stated.

#     Rule                          Statement

P1 One owning entity per row        Every operational row carries owner_entity_id. A document between two legal entities carries
                                    both parties, but exactly one of them is the owner (the issuer). Tenancy scoping, visibility and audit
                                    all key off this column.

P2 Identifiers are generated where Primary keys are UUID version 7: globally unique, time-ordered, safe to generate on a disconnected

      the record is born            till. Business-facing numbers are separate attributes.

P3 Documents are immutable          After issuance a document header and its lines are never updated or deleted. Any change is a new
          once issued               document that references the original (Part C).

P4 Ledgers are append-only          Stock movements, account postings, document state history, the outbox and the audit log are
                                    insert-only. Balances are derived, never edited. The application role holds INSERT only.

P5 Reference data is                Prices, tax rates, control prices, relationships and conversions carry effective_from /
          effective-dated           effective_to; a past transaction can always be shown to have been correct at the time.

P6 One writer per record type       The till is the only writer of its own sale, session, receiving and count records; central is the only
                                    writer of everything else, pushed to tills read-only. No merge logic exists (ADR-10).

P7 Trilingual text with English as name_en mandatory; name_si, name_ta optional except where a status requires them; ICU

      the anchor                    collations on each.

P8 Money and quantity are exact Money numeric(14,2) LKR; unit prices and costs numeric(14,4); quantities numeric(14,3)
                                                       in the base unit. No floating point.

P9 Time is stored twice             occurred_at in UTC and occurred_local as the device reading; received_at stamped by
                                    the server; sequence numbers, not clocks, order events.

P10 Nothing is hard-deleted         Reference rows carry a status; users are deactivated; documents are voided by compensating
                                    documents. The only DELETE privilege belongs to migration tooling.

3. Part A – Entity, location and tenancy scoping

Three kinds of legal entity and one kind of place. A shop is a location, not an entity: it holds stock and issues
receipts, but the legal party to every transaction is the MPCS or distributor that owns it. This distinction is
enforced by the absence of legal attributes on location.

3.7 Tenancy scoping and visibility enforcement

One shared database; ownership is a property of rows. Every database session carries the caller's scope as
settings (app.scope_entity_id, app.scope_location_id, app.scope_class,
app.granted_entities, set with SET LOCAL per transaction); row-level security policies read them. A
transaction without a scope fails closed.

What the classes guarantee: no query runs without a declared scope (a missing scope fails closed); a distributor
cannot see an MPCS's internal data beyond the documents they trade (FR-GOV-020); a shop sees nothing of a
sibling shop (M-05) because shop-scoped users carry a location scope and shop-internal tables filter on it; the
buyer sees the seller's invoice, never the seller's cost basis, because PARTY reads go through masking views;
the Federation and external grantees read everything in their scope and write nothing.

Invariants for Part A.

• A-I1. Every operational table has a non-null owner_entity_id or derives one through a non-null
   location_id.

• A-I2. location.owner_entity_id is immutable after creation.
• A-I3. At most one ACTIVE entity_relationship per (seller, buyer) at any date.
• A-I4. A till_position belongs to exactly one SHOP and its position_no is unique within it; positions

   are retired, never deleted.
• A-I5. A device holds at most one till position at a time; the change is an audited event.

4. Part B – Catalogue, units, batches and price ceilings

Three ideas: the SKU is what is counted and sold; the batch is a physical supply identity carrying expiry and the
printed MRP; the stock lot is an entity's holding of a batch at a location, in a condition, carrying that entity's cost.
Separating batch from lot lets one physical batch be owned successively by the Federation, a distributor and an
MPCS at three different costs (ADR-38).

4.9 Price resolution at the till (data-level statement)

For a scanned line the effective selling price is min( list price, batch term, control price in force ) where the batch
term is the lowest printed MRP among batches with positive on-hand at the location (AUTO_LOWEST), the scanned
batch's MRP (BARCODE_RESOLVED) or the operator's pick (PICKER, only when the candidate gap exceeds the
threshold), and is absent for SKUs without a printed MRP; then discount rules apply (one line rule by priority, one bill
rule); then the control price is re-checked as a hard cap; then tax is decomposed and cash tenders round to the rupee
at receipt level. Every receipt line stores batch_id, mrp_applied, control_price_applied, cap_reason,
rule_id so its legality is reproducible from the row alone. The same library computes it at central and on the till
(ADR-31).

• B-I1. sku.short_name_en is non-null for every status; _si and _ta are non-null for SHARED.

• B-I4. stock_lot.qty_on_hand equals the sum of movements for that lot; a nightly reconciliation asserts it

• I-F8. Control prices, segregation pairs and posting maps are data with their own tables, never

   configuration-register items (P10 of doc 17).
• I-F9. An API client's scopes never exceed the granting administrator's permissions; the same containment

   rule as roles.
• I-F10. Every module-owned document extension table inherits the kernel immutability trigger and is

   partitioned by the same key as the base.
• I-F11. Only the primary till issues LOCATION-series documents; other tills capture drafts (ADR-47).
• I-F12. Customer repayments allocate oldest-first through customers.allocation; entity payments settle specific

   invoices through SETTLES links (I-07, ADR-46).
