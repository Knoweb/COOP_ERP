      1. Modelling principles

      Ten rules govern every table in this model. They are stated once here and applied throughout; where a table
      departs from one of them, the departure is called out explicitly.

#     Rule                     Statement

P1 One owning entity per row   Every operational row carries owner_entity_id. A document between two legal entities carries
                               both parties, but exactly one of them is the owner (the issuer). Tenancy scoping, visibility and audit
                               all key off this column; there is no second mechanism.

P2 Identifiers are generated where Primary keys are UUID version 7 (time-ordered universally unique identifiers). A till creates receipts

      the record is born       offline with no central coordination; a UUIDv7 key is globally unique, sortable by creation time, and

                               never collides on synchronisation. Business-facing numbers (document numbers, SKU codes) are

                               separate attributes.

P3 Documents are immutable     After issuance a document header and its lines are never updated or deleted. Any change is a new
          once issued          document that references the original (Part C). This is what makes the audit trail and the gapless
                               series trustworthy.

P4 Ledgers are append-only     Stock movements, document state history and the audit log are insert-only tables. Balances are
                               derived from movements, never edited directly. Database privileges enforce this: the application role
                               holds INSERT only.

P5 Reference data is           Prices, tax rates, control prices and conversion factors carry effective_from / effective_to. A
          effective-dated      past transaction can always be shown to have been correct at the time.

P6 One writer per record type  The till is the only writer of its own sale events and receipts; central is the only writer of catalogue,
                               prices and rules, pushed to tills read-only. No table has two writers, so no merge logic exists
                               (ADR-10).

P7 Trilingual text with English as Names carry three columns: name_en (mandatory), name_si, name_ta. English is the universal

      the anchor               fallback and the collation anchor; Sinhala and Tamil columns use ICU (International Components for

                               Unicode) collations for correct sorting and search.

P8 Money and quantity are exact Money is numeric(14,2) in Sri Lankan rupees (LKR); quantities are numeric(14,3) in the SKU
                                                       base unit. No floating point anywhere.

P9 Time is stored twice        Every event stores occurred_at in UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) and occurred_local as
                               the device clock reading. Offline devices have unreliable clocks; the server stamps received_at
                               on arrival, and sequence numbers, not clocks, order events.

P10 Nothing is hard-deleted    Reference rows carry a status; users are deactivated; documents are voided by a compensating
                               document. The only DELETE privilege in the system belongs to migration tooling, not the
                               application.

3. Part A ­ Entity, location and tenancy scoping

The federation has three kinds of legal entity and one kind of place. A shop is a location, not an entity: it holds
stock and issues receipts, but the legal party to every transaction is the multi-purpose cooperative society
(MPCS) or distributor that owns it. This distinction is the single most important structural fact in the model and is
enforced by the absence of any entity-level attributes on location.

4. Part B ­ Catalogue, units, batches and price ceilings

Three ideas structure this part. The SKU is what is counted and sold; the batch is a physical supply identity
carrying expiry and the printed maximum retail price (MRP); the stock lot is an entity's holding of a batch at a
location, carrying that entity's cost. Separating batch from lot is what allows one physical batch to be owned
successively by the Federation, a distributor and an MPCS at three different costs.

For a scanned line, the effective selling price is: min( list price, lowest printed MRP among batches with positive
on-hand at the location, control price in force ), then discount rules apply, then the control price is re-checked as a
hard cap. When multi_mrp_policy = BARCODE_RESOLVED and the scanned barcode carries a batch_id, that
batch's MRP replaces the "lowest" term. When the policy is PICKER, the operator's choice is recorded on the line with
the alternatives presented. In every case the receipt line stores batch_id, mrp_applied,
control_price_applied and cap_reason, so the legality of every line is reproducible from the row alone.
