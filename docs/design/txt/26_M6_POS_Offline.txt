Cooperative Retail and Distribution System

26 ­ M6 POS & Offline

Module design for the till: selling, tendering as record, printing trilingual receipts,
deducting stock, running a session with blind close, hosting the shop-management
screens, and doing all of it for two days without central ­ then synchronising without ever
merging. Also the central side that ingests, re-validates and watches the fleet.

The till is a small, complete system. It has its own database, its own gapless counters, its own copy of the rules, its
own audit log and its own outbox. Central never edits what it produces and it never edits what central sends. That
symmetry (ADR-10) is why there is no conflict resolution anywhere; every other design choice in this module protects
it. The second commitment is speed at the counter: three taps to sell, and nothing that requires reading more than a
word.

                          Two days: about 400 receipts per till per day at the planning basis, 2­4 KB each with movements; the local

                          Kiosk lock-task; device-owner managed; keystore-held database key; Argon2id PIN hashes; operator snapshot
