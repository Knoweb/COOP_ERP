32 – Sync Contract

The contract between a till and central: what moves in each direction, in what unit, in
what order, how it is acknowledged, how versions and gaps are handled, and what
happens on every failure that matters. This is the one interface that is expensive to
change once terminals are in the field, so it is specified before the till is designed.

The contract in one paragraph. A till writes its own facts into a local outbox with a dense sequence number. It
uploads them in order, one batch in flight at a time, and central acknowledges the highest sequence it has durably
applied. Central never edits, re-numbers or merges what a till sends; a fact it cannot accept is quarantined and
flagged, never lost and never allowed to block what follows. In the other direction, central publishes versioned
snapshots of reference data per shop; a till downloads the delta since its version and applies it atomically. No record
has two writers, so there is nothing to reconcile.

Response

                        POST /sync/v1/devices/{device_id}/batches with device authentication; gzip body: batch_id (UUIDv7), first_seq,

3.4 Acknowledgement

Aspect           Design
Content
                 batch_id; last_applied_seq (authoritative); per-event outcomes: APPLIED, DUPLICATE, FLAGGED (with codes),
Meaning          QUARANTINED (with reason); server_time; clock_offset_ms; current snapshot_version for the location; pending

Lost ack
                 Everything at or below last_applied_seq is durably at central. The till marks those outbox rows acknowledged and
                 retains them for the retention window (S8, DR-3: 7 days), then purges.

                 If ingestion failed part-way, last_applied_seq is the last committed chunk; the till resends from last_applied_seq +
                 1 with a new batch_id. Already-applied events in the overlap are answered DUPLICATE.

                 The till resends the same batch_id; central finds first_seq ≤ cursor and answers from state; no double application

Aspect           Design
Path
                 Photographs never travel inside a batch. The till requests a pre-signed PUT for each attachment (object key

Limits

                 Images are downscaled on the device to at most 1600 px on the long edge and about 300 KB; a bundle

5.2 Versions and deltas

Aspect                    Design
Version
Delta request             Central keeps one monotonic snapshot version per location. Any publication that changes the shop's snapshot
Full snapshot             (price list published, rule activated, control price entered, SKU shared, lot changed by a GRN elsewhere in the
Atomic apply              entity, operator added) records a change-log row and bumps the version.
apply_from
Cadence                   GET /sync/v1/locations/{location_id}/snapshot?since=N returns a delta from N+1 to current: per table, upserted
                          rows and tombstones, with the new version. If N is older than the change-log retention (DR-4: 30 days) or the
Consistency with uploads  device has no version, the response points to a full snapshot.
Staleness warning
                          A pre-built compressed file in object storage per location, rebuilt when the delta would exceed a threshold or
                          nightly, with a manifest (tables, row counts, hashes, version) and a pre-signed resumable download. Target
                          under 20 MB with thumbnails.

                          The device loads a delta or full snapshot into staging tables, verifies hashes, then swaps into the live cache and
                          records the version in one local transaction. Selling continues on the old version until the swap; the swap waits for
                          the current sale to complete.

                          Rows with an apply_from business date later than the shop's current business date are held in pending and
                          activated at the shop's day-open, so every till in the federation switches prices on the same trading day
                          regardless of when it downloaded.

                          Version check on every heartbeat (5 min) when online; download at most every 15 minutes (NFR-PRF-040)
                          unless central marks the change urgent (control price entered, permission revoked), in which case the next
                          heartbeat downloads immediately. An optional push wake-up (DR-2) shortens this; the design does not depend
                          on it.

                          Every upload reports snapshot_version_in_use; central re-validates prices against that version, so a legitimate
                          sale made on a slightly stale list is recognised as such rather than flagged as an error.

                          A till whose snapshot is older than a configured age (default 3 days) warns the supervisor that ceilings and prices
                          may be stale; it keeps selling.

Aspect                Design
Transport
                      TLS 1.2 or later to the edge; device client certificate or bearer token from the device credential; no anonymous

Replay                content_hash on every bundle; signed permission snapshot; snapshot manifest hashes; the ack is over the same

                      A suspended device receives 403 and a signed revoke; the application wipes caches and locks; unacknowledged
                      outbox rows are preserved for recovery by an administrator.

                      Customer snapshot carries name, phone, balance, limit only; PIN hashes are Argon2id; no card data exists.

                      Per device: batches per minute and bytes per hour; global: ingest capacity; 429 with retry_after; prioritised
