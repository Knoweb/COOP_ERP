24 – M4 Trading Documents

Module design for the inter-entity flow from order to settlement: order, delivery note with
drops, goods received note as the ownership pivot, discrepancy and claim, invoice,
credit and debit notes, payment receipt with settlement to specific invoices, statements,
and the warn-only exposure picture. This is where the state machines the Stage 2 plan
called for live; numbering detail waits for the tax advisor (24B).

One event carries the whole module. grn.confirmed.v1 is the moment ownership and risk transfer (firm sale,
ADR-03). Before it, the goods are the seller's problem; after it, the buyer's. Batches get registered from it, lots get
created from it, invoicing is permitted by it, and loss attribution depends on its timestamp. Everything in this module is
either on the way to that event or a consequence of it.

1. Purpose and scope

M4 owns the documents that pass between two legal entities and the ledger picture they produce: what was
ordered, what was dispatched, what was received, what is disputed, what is owed and what has been paid. It
never moves money (ADR-01) and never blocks on credit (ADR-12); it records, links and computes.

3. Domain model

All documents use the kernel base (doc 18 part C): header, lines, typed links, state history, attachments. M4
adds extension tables in schema trading, keyed one-to-one on document_id and inheriting immutability.

9. Non-functional design

Aspect                      Design
9.1 Capacity
                            Orders and DNs in the thousands per day; GRNs in the tens of thousands (shop receipts dominate); invoices and
9.2 Offline                 receipts in the thousands. All on the partitioned document base; extension tables partitioned alike. Exposure
9.3 Visibility              recomputation is incremental per event, not a full sum.

9.4 Immutability and links  GRN capture and confirmation, POD capture: fully local; ingested as bundles in sequence; the expected lines for
                            a drop travel to the shop in the snapshot on DN issue (urgent flag when the ETA is within a day).
9.5 E-invoicing readiness
9.6 Auditability            PARTY reads through masked views: the buyer never sees the seller's cost or margin columns, the seller never
                            sees the buyer's retail data; discrepancy and claim evidence is visible to both; the arbiter sees both
                            (FEDERATION_VIEW plus the arbiter role's write on arbitration only).

                            Every issued document is immutable; every correction is a link (REVERSES, CREDITS, DEBITS, SETTLES,
                            DISPUTES); the open balance of an invoice is derived from links, never stored except as a cached column
                            recomputed by the same events.

                            The invoice snapshot (header, lines, both VAT numbers, tax point, hash) is what an IRD adapter will submit;
                            e_invoice_status is metadata; nothing on the invoice changes when submission arrives (J-04).

                            Every transition in Section 4 is an audit event; arbitration and reversals require MFA; the allocation run stores the
                            availability snapshot it decided on, so a fairness dispute can be replayed (C-03).
