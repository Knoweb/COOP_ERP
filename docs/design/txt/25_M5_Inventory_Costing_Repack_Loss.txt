Two invariants and one temptation. Movements are the only way stock changes, and every movement cites a
document; balances are sums, materialised for speed and reconciled nightly. The temptation is to reject a sale that the
records say is impossible ­ two offline tills selling the same last unit ­ and the answer is never: the sale happened, the
lot goes negative, an exception is raised, and the next count fixes the record (E-09).

3. Domain model

Schema inventory: stock_lot, stock_movement, repack_recipe (doc 18 v0.2 part B) plus the tables introduced
here.

    3.1 Lot                  Design

Aspect                       stock_lot(location, batch, condition GOOD | DAMAGED, qty_on_hand, unit_cost,
Aggregate                    received_at, last_movement_seq). A lot is one entity's holding of one batch at one location in one
                             condition; damaged units received on a GRN form a DAMAGED lot (doc 24 §6.1), sellable only through an M3
Cost                         markdown rule or written off.
Re-pointing
                             unit_cost is the acquisition cost of that lot: trade price for goods bought from another entity, keyed cost for
                             local supply, recipe-derived cost for repack outputs, restated by landed cost in phase 2 (M10).

                             On batch.corrected.v1 lots move to the replacement batch (same qty and cost); on sku.merged.v1 lots of
                             the alias SKU's batches remain (batch identity carries the SKU) and queries resolve through the alias.

3.2 Movement ledger and balances

Aspect                       Design
Ledger
                             stock_movement(movement_seq per location, location, batch, condition, qty_delta,
Balances                     movement_type, unit_cost_at_movement, document, line, occurred_at, operator,
Negative lots                device) (doc 18 part B). Insert-only. movement_seq is dense per location: assigned by the till for its own
Batch attribution of a sale  movements (inside the receipt bundle) and by central otherwise; the two sequences are kept apart by a source
                             column so both stay dense.

                             stock_lot.qty_on_hand =  qty_delta for the lot (B-I4); updated in the same transaction as the movement;
                             the nightly StockReconcileJob recomputes and raises a REVIEW on drift, never silently fixes.

                             A SALE movement may take a lot below zero (offline oversell). The lot is flagged negative_since;
                             lot.negative.v1 goes to the exception queue; the next count or receipt clears it. Availability never counts
                             negative lots.

                             The receipt line carries the batch the till resolved (M3 engine: scanned, picked or lowest-MRP); the deduction hits
                             that lot. If that lot is exhausted, the deduction still hits it (negative) rather than silently moving to another batch, so
                             the record shows what the till believed.

    3.3 Costing              Design

Aspect                       Moving weighted average per entity per SKU (E-03, IAS 2): entity_sku_cost(entity, sku,
Method                       qty_on_hand, avg_cost) updated on every receipt-type movement (RECEIPT, TRANSFER_IN at the
                             receiving entity is at cost so unchanged, REPACK_PRODUCE, OPENING_BALANCE) as (qty × avg +
Lot cost versus entity       qty_in × cost_in) / (qty + qty_in); issues (SALE, WRITE_OFF, TRANSFER_OUT within the entity
average                      unchanged, REPACK_CONSUME) carry unit_cost_at_movement = avg_cost at that moment.
Damaged units
                             The lot keeps its acquisition cost for traceability and for claims; the entity average is the costing figure for cost of
                             goods sold, valuation and write-off value. Both are stored; neither is recomputed retroactively.

                             A DAMAGED lot carries the same unit cost as its GOOD sibling; the loss is recognised when written off or when
                             marked down (M3).

Aspect                   repack_recipe(owner, input sku, input qty, output sku, output qty,
Recipe                   expected_loss_pct, status) (ADR-07, B-06); the output SKU is a distinct M2 SKU (origin

Execution

Reversal                 REPACK_PRODUCE (output at cost = consumed cost / produced qty); yield variance recorded on the document

3.7 Transfers and opening balances

Aspect                   Design
Transfer (XFR)
                         Within one entity only. Issued at the source location on M4's transfer_request.approved.v1 or directly by
Opening balance (OPB)    the entity for warehouse-to-warehouse: TRANSFER_OUT at source at cost; in-transit until the destination
                         confirms receipt (offline-capable on the till, LOCATION series) posting TRANSFER_IN; unconfirmed after
                         transfer_receipt_days raises a REVIEW.

                         At onboarding: counted quantities with batch data and cost, signed by the entity and the Federation onboarding
                         officer (two signatures as state transitions); posts OPENING_BALANCE movements; the only way stock enters
                         without a GRN or repack (R-02).

3.8 Loss

Aspect                   Design

Categories               loss_category: DAMAGED_IN_TRANSIT, DAMAGED_IN_STORE, EXPIRED, THEFT,
                         SHRINKAGE_UNEXPLAINED, STAFF_CONSUMPTION, SAMPLES, DONATION, OTHER (F-01). Each
                         write-off line carries one; reporting never aggregates across them without showing the split.

Write-off (WOF)          Requester drafts (offline on the till allowed) with lines, category, batch, qty, photographs; a witness distinct from
                         the requester confirms; an approver distinct from the requester approves within their value band (entity avg cost
                         basis); posting creates WRITE_OFF movements. For a single-staff shop the MPCS approver acts as remote
                         witness with photographs mandatory (doc 18 DR-7 adopted).

Attribution              The loss belongs to the entity owning the lot when the write-off is posted (ADR-03); DAMAGED_IN_TRANSIT on
                         the seller's side is the seller's write-off of returned goods; on the buyer's side it is a claim (M4), not a write-off.

Expiry tasks             expiry_task created when a batch enters its window (SKU or tag expiry_warning_days): a count task for
                         the holding location and a hand-off to M3's markdown rules; on expiry date, remaining quantity becomes a
                         suggested write-off (category EXPIRED) that still needs the full lifecycle.

Tolerances and shrinkage loss_tolerance(sku | tag, pct or qty) below which count variances post without escalation (F-10);
                                      shrinkage percentage per shop is a projection of SHRINKAGE_UNEXPLAINED and negative-lot clearances over
                                      sales (F-09), visible to the Federation view-only.

9. Non-functional design

Aspect                 Design
9.1 Capacity
                       Movements are the highest-volume table after receipt lines: about 15 million a day at the horizon (five lines per
9.2 Offline            receipt). Partitioned monthly by received_at; the lot table is small (locations × live batches). Balance updates
9.3 Consistency        are single-row increments in the ingestion transaction; the entity average update is one row per (entity, sku) per
9.4 Controls           receipt-type movement.
9.5 Visibility
9.6 Phase 2 readiness  Count entry, write-off draft, repack at a shop and transfer receipt are till-local documents in the LOCATION series
                       (primary till) and arrive as bundles; the till deducts sales against its lot cache immediately and the ledger at
                       central is authoritative after ingestion.

                       Lot balance = sum of movements (B-I4) enforced by the transaction pattern and verified nightly; negative lots are
                       visible, never hidden; the reconciliation job raises, never fixes.

                       Write-off is the highest-fraud-risk lifecycle: SoD at INSTANCE level, witness rule, photographs, value bands, MFA
                       on approval, every step audited; adjustments the same minus the witness; every negative lot and every
                       tolerance-cleared variance lands on the exception report.

                       Costs and margins are OWN and Federation view-only (M-04); the buyer of a relationship never sees the seller's
                       lots except through availability; shops see only their own location (M-05).

                       Ageing, reorder points and ABC cycle counting are projections over this ledger; nothing in the phase-1 schema
                       changes for them.
