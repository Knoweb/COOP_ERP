Why this module is first and why it is small. Every row in the system carries an owning entity; every request carries
a scope and a permission set; every trading document names a relationship. M1 is where those facts are created and
nowhere else. It has almost no business process of its own, which is exactly why it must be frozen first: a change here
ripples through all nine modules.

1. Purpose and scope

M1 answers four questions for the rest of the system: who exists (entities, locations, till positions, devices), who
trades with whom on what terms (relationships), who may do what (users, roles, permissions, grants), and what
each caller may see (scope and policy class). It owns the reference data and the policy; the kernel enforces the
policy on every request and database session (doc 19 sections 1 and 3, doc 18 section 3.7).

3. Domain model

Tables are those of doc 18 part A; this section states the aggregates, their invariants and the two change
requests raised against doc 18. Owner of every table here: schema party (entities, relationships, locations,
positions, devices) and schema security (users, permissions, roles, assignments, grants, pairs).

3.1 Entity

Aspect                  Design
Aggregate
Invariants              entity with responsible officer and financial-year settings. Root of ownership for everything else.

Change request CR-21-1  Exactly one FEDERATION row. entity_code immutable and unique. Status never reaches a terminal 'left' state
                        (FR-GOV-050). costing_policy is WEIGHTED_AVERAGE in phase 1.

                        Add responsible_officer_user_id (nullable until the officer is appointed) and
                        data_governance_signed_on (date, nullable) to entity for FR-GOV-060; both audited on change.

3.2 Trading relationship

Aspect                  Design
Aggregate
Invariants              entity_relationship (effective-dated) with its terms; the anchor for M3 price lists and M4 exposure.

Tier rule               seller ≠ buyer; one ACTIVE row per (seller, buyer) per date (exclusion constraint); a relationship's
                        price_list_id must belong to the seller (validated against M3's query at activation); credit-limit change
Intra-entity movements  requires bil.creditlimit.change with MFA and a reason.

                        Phase 1 permits Federation→distributor and distributor→MPCS. Federation→MPCS is not blocked by schema,
                        only by a configuration flag allow_direct_federation_mpcs (default off), so the change later is data (doc 17
                        M1 note).

                        Never a relationship. A transfer between two shops of one MPCS is an M5 document; the schema check is
                        simply seller ≠ buyer.

    3.3 Location        Design

Aspect                  location with primary_till_position_id.
Aggregate
Invariants              owner_entity_id immutable (A-I2). Only WAREHOUSE and SHOP may hold stock (enforced by M5's query
                        refusing OFFICE). A SHOP needs a primary till before it can issue location-series documents (A-I7).
Language and hours      connectivity_spec_met must be true for status ACTIVE.

                        Language drives the till, receipts and back-office sessions opened at that location (doc 19 section 5.1).

3.4 Till position and device

Aspect                  Design
Aggregates
Invariants              till_position (logical lane) and device (hardware).

Hand-offs               A position belongs to exactly one SHOP and its position_no is unique there; positions retire, never delete. A
                        device holds at most one position; the assignment change is an audited event with old and new (A-I5). A device
                        below the version floor after grace cannot be assigned to a new position.

                        Enrolment creates the device row from the management agent's staging record (doc 31); position assignment
                        triggers series creation through the kernel numbering service (doc 32 section 8); suspension publishes
                        device.suspended.v1 which the sync gateway turns into a signed revoke.

3.5 Users, roles, permissions

Aspect                  Design
Aggregates
                        app_user; permission (catalogue, federation-owned, versioned as a whole); role with role_permission
Invariants              carrying limits jsonb; user_role with scope columns; sod_pair (new, CR-21-2).

                        A user has one home entity. A role is owned by one entity or is a federation template. Scope of an assignment
                        must lie within the grantor's own scope. No user may grant a permission they do not hold. An entity always
                        retains at least one holder of gov.user.manage. FEDERATION-scope permissions appear only in
