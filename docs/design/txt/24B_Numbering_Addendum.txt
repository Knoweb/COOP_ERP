     24B ­ Numbering Addendum

     Closes the six open numbering questions as decisions. No design changes result; the
     document-type seed flags and the decision log are updated, and NFR-CMP-030 moves
     from gating to advisory.

     handled as an ordinary change request against this addendum. Until then these decisions stand and the risk is owned
     by the programme, recorded in doc 10.
