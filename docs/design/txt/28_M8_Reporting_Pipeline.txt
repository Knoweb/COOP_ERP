Cooperative Retail and Distribution System

28 – M8 Reporting Pipeline

Module design for the pipeline, not the reports: projections built from the event stream
into a reporting schema on the replica, end-of-day aggregates keyed to each shop's
day-close, a per-entity freshness stamp on every federation-level figure, a
decision-driven report catalogue rendered through the A4 pipeline, spreadsheet export,
the analyst SQL role, and the exception queues. Individual report definitions arrive from
the pending workshop and slot into this pipeline without changing it.

Three commitments. Every projection is rebuildable from the outbox archive, so a wrong report is fixed by a rebuild,
never by editing figures. Every federation-level number carries the freshness of its least-recent contributor, because
some shops are always offline and a figure without that stamp misleads. And the catalogue is decision-driven: a report
enters it with the decision it supports and the audience that makes it (O-01), or it does not enter at all.

9. Non-functional design

Aspect                Design
9.1 Latency
9.2 Volume            Projection lag under 60 s in steady state at 1,000 events/s; during reconnection bursts projections lag and the
                      stamp says so; dashboards read the replica (replica lag added to freshness).
9.3 Columnar trigger
                      Receipt facts: about 3 million rows a day at the horizon, lines 15 million; partitioned monthly; roll-ups keep
9.4 Isolation         dashboards on small tables; indefinite retention with cold partitions detached to object storage and re-attachable
9.5 Security          for historical runs.
9.6 Privacy
                      When end-of-day aggregation for the federation exceeds reporting.end_of_day_window_minutes on the
                      replica, or dashboard queries exceed 2 s at p95, a columnar store is introduced (doc 19 DR-3); because facts are
                      rebuildable from the archive, it is a rebuild, not a migration.

                      Consumers run in the worker profile; rendering and exports in the rendering queue; the replica serves reads; the
                      primary bears only projection writes.

                      RLS on every projection; the analyst role cannot reach operational schemas; every report run and export is
                      audited as a read; exports carry the scope and stamp in the file.

                      Customer projections carry account ids and balances, never names or phones (names resolve at render time
                      under M7's RLS for the OWN MPCS only).
