Cooperative Retail and Distribution System

23 ­ M3 Pricing & Rules

Module design for every price a buyer pays and every reduction a customer receives:
trade price lists per relationship, one retail list per MPCS, Federation advisory prices, the
closed-vocabulary rule engine that is the sole source of any discount or markdown, the
two legal ceilings (printed MRP per batch and the gazetted control price by date), and
the price-resolution engine that runs identically at central and on an offline till.

Two facts shape this module. First, a gazetted maximum price is law from its publication date and binds all stock
regardless of what is printed on older packs (Consumer Affairs Authority Act s.20(5)); selling above it is an offence with
fines in the millions of rupees. So the control price is a hard block at every point a price is set, printed or charged.
Second, the till must compute the same bill offline that central would compute online, so resolution is one pure library,
versioned, with no server dependencies (ADR-31).

           Binding           price_list(kind TRADE | RETAIL | ADVISORY, owner_entity, name, version, status) with

           Versions          A TRADE list is referenced by one or more entity_relationship rows (M1); the Federation's uniform list by
                             every Federationdistributor relationship; a distributor's negotiated list by one relationship each. A RETAIL list

                             forward; the previous version's lines are closed at apply_from - 1. History is queryable; nothing is edited.

9.1 Engine performance
                          Pure functions over in-memory inputs; a 30-line basket with 200 active rules resolves in under 5 ms on the
9.2 Determinism and       reference ARM terminal; rule indexing by sku and tag at snapshot load, not per scan.
parity
                          The engine is deterministic given (snapshot version, engine version, business date, inputs); central re-validates

9.4 Snapshot volume
                          Engine changes bump engine_version; the snapshot schema carries the minimum engine version; a till below
9.5 Legal safety          it warns and keeps selling with its own version (central re-validates with that version).

9.6 Privacy and security  Retail lines for the assortment (a few thousand rows), rules (tens to hundreds), control prices (tens), policies;
                          deltas are small except on a list publication day.

                          Property-based tests assert that no engine output ever exceeds the control term; the label job and the authoring
                          check share the same function; control-price entry is MFA-gated and audited with the gazette reference.

                          No personal data; trade lists are PARTY-visible only for the bound relationship; advisory rules public within the
                          federation.
