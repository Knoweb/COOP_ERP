- **Expired and short-dated stock (wave 2: M5-01, with M3-02), decided on the architect's delegation (6 Oct, branch `docs/wave2-decisions`), CR-25A-1 item 1:** (1) A lot is **expired when its expiry date is before the business date** (Asia/Colombo, from `Clock` plus `coop-erp.business-timezone`, passed into the queries; never SQL `current_date`, which is yesterday between 00:00 and 05:30 Colombo); stock is sellable through the printed date. (2) Expired lots are **never picked** for a delivery or a transfer (`lockCandidates`, `pickBatches`), **never counted as available** (`availability`, the `fefo_rank`) and **never price candidates** (`inStockBatches`, which also feeds AUTO_LOWEST: an older expired batch with a lower printed MRP would otherwise hold the shelf price down). They stay visible in `balances` with a null rank and an "expired" badge. (3) **The sale fallback keeps expired lots** (a sale is a fact and the lot is where the units came from) and writes REVIEW `SALE_OF_EXPIRED` when the batch posted against is past expiry on the receipt's date. (4) Expired stock leaves by the write-off lifecycle (category EXPIRED) or by return to the supplier where the terms allow; the nightly `ExpiryScanJob` of 25A section 7 that drafts those write-offs is a separate ticket. (5) **Short-dated stock is not excluded inside an entity**: it enters the markdown window (`inventory.expiry_warning_days`) and M3's EXPIRY_MARKDOWN rules, which the society authors and funds; sending it to a busier shop is how a society clears it. (6) **For a delivery note to another entity** the seller's `inventory.dispatch_min_shelf_life_days` (new ENTITY item, default 0, the Federation may set a default) excludes lots with less life left; the seller decides its cut-off, the buyer's protection is the GRN discrepancy. (7) "Expired" is derived from the date at query time: no quarantine condition, no movement at midnight. Rejected: rank last and flag (expired goods still ship when nothing else is there); a QUARANTINE or EXPIRED lot condition moved by a nightly job (schema, movements and a job for what a date comparison gives); one Federation-wide shelf-life minimum (dairy and rice differ by months). Why: selling expired food is an offence under the Food Act that public health inspectors enforce (wording to confirm with Federation legal); the midnight edge (a lot expiring today is sellable today) is a test per query. Build note: the demo's opening stock carries future expiry dates (deviation 8 of `2026-09-29-m5-stock-control-decisions.md`); the fix PR checks `DemoShopStock` against the demo date so no demo pick starts failing.
