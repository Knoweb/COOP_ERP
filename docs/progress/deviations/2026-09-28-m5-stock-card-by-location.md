- **The stock card is read by location, whole history, no paging (28 Sep, branch `feat/m5-stock-card`; deferred for the demo, M5-04/M5-12):** 25A section 7 names `/skus/{id}/movements`; the demo's read is `GET /v1/inventory/locations/{locationId}/movements?skuId=`, because a stock card is one location's book of one item and RLS already narrows by location. It returns the item's whole history at the location with no date window and no paging; with a demo's few movements that is enough, but a busy shop needs a `from`/`to` window with the balance brought forward and a page size as a configuration item. It does not name the document of a movement (the GRN, transfer or receipt number): `MovementResponse` carries only the document id. Also deferred, M6 central screens: the receipts list, receipt card and till sessions screens were not started (handover close of 28 Sep); when built, the receipt read has no operator or till name to show (`ReceiptResponse` carries `deviceId` and the number, whose display `M101-S01-T1-RCT-n` names the till), and an oversold sale shows as a negative lot in M5, not as a receipt flag.
