- **Erasure, retention and one society per person (wave 2: M7CR-03, M7CR-09, M7CR-10, M7CR-11, M7CR-13), decided on the architect's delegation (6 Oct, branch `docs/wave2-decisions`), CR-27A-1 items 3 to 5:** (1) **The erasure guard**: every account of the customer at every society CLOSED at zero balance, locked for the transaction through `customers.lock_accounts_for_erasure(p_customer)` (SECURITY DEFINER, VOLATILE, under the cross-tenant pattern, with an `app_seed` UPDATE policy on `customer_account` because `FOR UPDATE` is refused under RLS without one), and none closed within `customers.erasure_wait_days` (FEDERATION item, default 3: the two-day offline window plus a day; the Act's own response period for a request is longer, so the member loses nothing); refusals `m7.privacy.open_balance` (kept), `m7.privacy.account_open`, `m7.privacy.recently_closed`. A till fact that lands after erasure is posted, never rejected, and flagged (`ACCOUNT_CHARGED_CUSTOMER_INACTIVE` with status ANONYMISED); the society settles it through REOPEN against the account number the receipt names; this is the documented limitation doc 27 section 3.2 already names. (2) **What erasure leaves, the engineering reading of doc 10 L-05 pending Federation legal**: under the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 the right to erasure (section 16) yields to retention a law requires and to the controller's legal claims; a society's books of account (co-operative and tax law; L-05 assumes 7 years) are such a retention. Erased or redacted: names, phones, NIC hash and last four, attributes, tags, consents, and the module's own free text about the person (`data_subject_request.notes = null, outcome = 'ERASED'` on every request of the customer; the erasure request's own outcome is the fixed `ANONYMISED`; `GRANT UPDATE (notes, outcome)` on that table, m7customers V0003). Retained for `customers.retention_years` (FEDERATION item, default 7, seeded now although the purge job waits for doc 10 D-02): postings, allocations, CPRs with their `reference`, the account row with its number and history, adjustment reasons, kernel audit rows and the DSAR records with coded outcomes, all keyed by the opaque customer id (doc 27 section 6.7). No UPDATE grant is added to `account_history.reason`, `account_adjustment.reason` or `doc_customer_payment.reference`: the first two are the society's record of its own decisions, the third is on an issued document, and opening them weakens the insert-only guarantee the whole system rests on. Officer free text is **guarded at write** (`PersonalDataText.require`, `m7.field.personal_data`) against Sri Lankan phone (`0\d{9}`, `+94\d{9}`) and NIC (`\d{9}[VX]`, `\d{12}`) patterns in every reason, reference, note and outcome; names cannot be detected and are the accepted limitation; `ChangePhone` and `DeactivateCustomer` pass the reason as the audit's `reason` argument, never in `after`; a privacy-scan test after erasure asserts no phone or NIC pattern and not the fixture's name across the module's text columns. (3) **NIC on a limit raise** (M7CR-03): `AmendAccountLimits` accepts an optional `nic`; when the limit rises above `customers.nic_required_above_limit` (seeded at last: DECIMAL, FEDERATION, default 0) and the customer holds none, `nic` is required; cap and hard-block changes alone never ask; one `NicCapture` serves OpenAccount, AmendAccountLimits and RecaptureNic. Rejected: refusing alone (a zero-limit account could never be raised). (4) **The access export is a command** (M7CR-11): `POST /v1/privacy/requests/{id}/export`, `cus.privacy.fulfil`, guards the request's society, FULFILLED, kind ACCESS, customer not anonymised, `requireResponsibleOfficer`; audit `DSAR_EXPORT_DOWNLOADED` with whether the recomputed hash still equals `export_sha256`; `nic_hash` leaves the export (`select` by column name), `nic_last4` stays; `export_sha256` means "hash at fulfilment"; storing the export under an object key waits for the kernel attachment API. (5) **One person holds credit at one society in v1** (M7CR-13): `account_holder_read` stays for the designed end state (ADR-15); `nic_holders` and `phone_holders` answer "held elsewhere" without identifying the holder, so the federation never gets two identities for one person; the member at another society's counter is told to use their own society or ask it for a transfer. The cross-society opening path (a definer `identity_for_account_opening(phone)` answering id, status, masked name and whether a NIC is held to an OWN scope; B inserting its CREDIT_ACCOUNT consent and account row) is sketched in CR-27A-1 and built after the joint-controller agreement (doc 10 L-02, ADR-26) is signed: without it society B has no lawful basis to read A's member's identity, and the case is an edge against the demo priority. Rejected: building the path now; dropping `account_holder_read`.
