- **Payments and exposure, decided on the architect's delegation (29 Sep, branch `feat/m4-payments-credit-limit`):** (1) **No HOLD on credit**: doc 24 FR-BIL-080 and ADR-12 say exposure is warned, never blocked, and 24A defines no hold state or override, so AcceptOrder accepts an order over the limit and publishes `exposure.warning.v1` when the acceptance crosses a threshold of `trading.exposure_warn_thresholds`; the order desk shows what the acceptance takes the exposure to. (2) **Allocations are insert-only rows of `trading.payment_allocation`, not SETTLES links**: `kernel.document_link` refuses a negative amount and counts every SETTLES link against the open balance for good, so a bounced cheque could not reopen the invoice for the next payment; a reversal writes negated rows and the invoice's `settled_amount` is their sum. The REVERSES link stays. The kernel is not changed. (3) **The exposure is computed on read** (`ExposureCalculator`), not cached in `relationship_exposure` by a consumer; no unapplied credits (a credit note is always applied to its invoice) and no freshness yet; an order counts as invoiced once an invoice bills a GRN of a drop that carried it. (4) **The cheque's outcome is an insert-only row** (`trading.cheque_outcome`), not an update of `cheque.status`. (5) **Oldest first skips a disputed invoice**; an overpayment stays on account (24A's unapplied amount), it is never refused. (6) **RecordChequeOutcome takes `bil.payment.record`** for both outcomes (24A's code for cheque outcomes); a bounce reverses with the MFA waived as a system consequence (24A 6.3). (7) **A credit note may not credit more than is still due** (`m4.creditnote.exceeds_due`), since the kernel's CREDITS check no longer sees the payments. (8) **The receipt prints on screen only**; the A4 print of a PRC, ApplyReceipt, DisputePayment, a manual reversal and statements are not built. (9) **The credit limit stays M1's**: set at opening or by `AmendRelationshipTerms` (`bil.creditlimit.change`, MFA); M4 reads it. The demo sets Point Pedro MPCS's limit at opening (a fresh load), since amending would close the relationship row open orders were placed under.
