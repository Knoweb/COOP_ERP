- **M7 remainders, decided on the architect's delegation (29 Sep, branch `feat/m7-limits-privacy-snapshot`):** (1) **AmendLimit, SetHardBlock and SetOfflineCap are one command** (`POST /v1/accounts/{id}/limits`), MFA only when the limit goes up (config `customers.limit_increase_mfa_max_age`, PT10M, as M1's credit limit); suspend, reinstate and close ask none. (2) **A reversed allocation is a row of `allocation_reversal`**, not `allocation.reversed_at`: the ledger stays insert-only. (3) **An adjustment is an `account_adjustment` row**, the document its ADJUSTMENT posting cites, not a kernel document (no type exists); the requester can never approve (`m7.adjustment.same_person`), whatever the SoD pair table says. (4) **The till's repayment is `customer_payment.issued.v1`**, consumer `m7.repayments`; the CPR is kept in `doc_customer_payment` (origin TILL), not in kernel.document; adding `customer_payment.issued` to the gateway's bundle types (content hash) is a kernel change for the platform pair. (5) **Privacy requests have a CORRECTION kind**; the access export is rebuilt on download and its SHA-256 kept on the request (no object key); the erasure guard counts balances at every society; only the registering society records a request and only its responsible officer answers. (6) **The snapshot contributor feeds no change log**, as M1's and M2's do not yet; the urgent fan-out on a suspension comes with the till's delta sync. (7) **Deferred**: statements as A4 documents and the statement job (the renderer runs on the worker only), SMS statements (M9), banking, receivables by bucket, the payload-scanning privacy gate.
