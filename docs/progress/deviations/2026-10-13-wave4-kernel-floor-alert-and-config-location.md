It is evaluated after the claim on a batch that holds the cursor (not on a 409 or a stored acknowledgement) and on every heartbeat. A later raise of the floor alerts again; a floor lowered and raised again to the same value does not. The heartbeat's UPDATE locks the cursor row only when it moves the column (once per floor), so a batch claimed in that instant may get one 409 `sync.batch_in_flight` and retries.
