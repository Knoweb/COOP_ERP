- **Member identity and the view-all classes (wave 2: RLS-01, RLS-02, TWK-30, M9-06's `fed_view`), decided on the architect's delegation (6 Oct, branch `docs/wave2-decisions`), CR-18-2:** (1) **FEDERATION_VIEW and EXTERNAL_TIMEBOXED do not read personal data of natural persons by policy.** m7customers V0003 drops `fed_view` and `ext_view` on `customers.customer`, `customer_phone` and `customer_consent`, and `ext_view` on `data_subject_request` (its `notes` and `outcome` are free text; `fed_view` stays there until the redaction of `2026-10-06-wave2-m7-erasure-and-retention.md` lands, then goes too); m9integration V0006 drops `fed_view` on `integration.notification_contact` (an address is personal data). The credit book (`customer_account`, `account_posting`, `allocation`, `allocation_reversal`, `account_history`, `account_adjustment`, `doc_customer_payment`) keeps the two policies: money, no name. (2) **The same line one layer up, in the permission resolver**: an operation marked `x-federation-view: false` in its OpenAPI slice is excluded from the set `JdbcPermissionResolver.resolve` hands a FEDERATION_VIEW session (`SliceOperations.readPermissions()`; the slice is loaded at start, so no migration and no seed); marked: M1 user, device and credential administration reads, the privacy-request reads and exports, M7 customer personal-data views. The rule in one sentence, for doc 18 section 3.7 and 21A section 3: *the Federation reads its members' trading, stock, finance and reporting data; it does not read their staff administration or their customers' personal data; identity reaches it through a module's audited query or a named export when one is specified.* (3) `RlsMatrixIntegrationTest.EXCEPTIONS` takes `onlyTheOwnerReads` for the three customer tables and the contacts table. Reasons: doc 27 section 9.3 requires every read of a customer record outside the sales path to be audited, which a FEDERATION_VIEW SQL read cannot be; the joint-controller agreement that would give the Federation a lawful basis to read several million members' names and phones (doc 10 L-01, L-02; ADR-26) is not yet commissioned; Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 (sections 5 and 6: purpose limitation and data minimisation) asks for the narrowest reach while that is open; no Federation code reads `customers.*` today, and M8 has its aggregates from the account tables. Rejected: keeping doc 18's "Everything" for personal data and auditing at the API only (the SQL read stays unaudited); a SECURITY DEFINER masking view (`customer_id, owner, status, language`) for the Federation (no consumer in v1; add it when one exists); a `federation_view` flag in the permission table (a migration and a seed for a fact the slice already knows). M7CR-12 was dropped for following the documents; this decision changes the documents first, which is the right order.
