- **A till's facts at the gateway and in M6 (wave 2: M6-01 to M6-10, TWK-09, TWK-23, TWK-26, M8-04's cause), decided on the architect's delegation (6 Oct, branch `docs/wave2-decisions`), CR-32-1:** (1) **A malformed payload is quarantined at the kernel gateway, before the outbox, never in a module** (M6 D1): `EventApplier` checks the generic doc 32 section 3.1 shape of every bundle type (`document.document_id` a UUID, `document.issued_at` an instant, every money, quantity and price field present a decimal string, every UUID field a UUID, `lines` an array with integer `line_no >= 1` unique, `tenders` with integer `seq >= 1` unique, a non-blank `kind` and a decimal `amount`) in a `BundleShape` helper beside `BundleHash`, and session events through a small `kernel.api.DevicePayloadCheck` SPI implemented in `m6pos` (the kernel never imports M6), quarantining as `SCHEMA` with the existing `SYNC_EVENT_QUARANTINED` ALERT and `sync.anomaly.v1`. Why at the gateway: `m5.sales`, `m7.tenders` and `m8.shop-sales` each consume the same outbox event, so a module-local refusal lets M5 deduct stock and M8 count a sale M6 never recorded; doc 32 S4 and section 3.3 step 6 already say "store raw in sync_quarantine with the reason; advance the cursor; raise SYNC_ANOMALY", which is distinct from refusing a fact for a business reason. The check stays at the shape the till sends (`Facts.kt`: no `line_id`, no `batch_id`, tax "0.00") and adds no business rule. Rejected: lenient parsing in each module; an M6 quarantine table; renumbering bad lines (central inventing facts). (2) **Module consumers apply and flag**: `TOTAL_MISMATCH` and `TENDER_MISMATCH` within `pos.receipt.total_tolerance` (default 0.01 per receipt), `TOTALS_MISSING`, `NO_SESSION`, `SESSION_CLOSED`, `RECEIPT_REPLAY_DIFFERS` (ALERT; the first copy is kept, as M5's `movementsCiting` keeps it; a second session close with other amounts the same), `SERIES_FOREIGN` (a device raises only its own RCT TILL_POSITION series at its own location; the check moves into the kernel's `observeDeviceNumber(seriesId, number, scope)` returning an outcome, so a till can never push the society's invoice series forward) and `NUMBER_JUMP` beyond `pos.series.max_jump` (default 10,000; raise by at most the gap, ALERT); M5 writes `SALE_BATCH_SUBSTITUTED` only for a named batch M2 does not know (the no-batch FEFO fallback is the normal path and stays silent) and `SALE_LINE_SKIPPED` for a null or non-positive quantity; nothing restocks on a sale receipt. (3) **How the shop sees what central questioned** (D2): the receipts screen gains a "flagged" filter and translated flag texts (`pos.flag.<CODE>`, en/si/ta, an unknown code falls back to itself); quarantined uploads and flagged receipts become exception items for the shop's location (28A `exception_item` from REVIEW and ALERT records and `sync.anomaly.v1`); the till's resend (doc 32 section 8) is the remedy for a quarantine. Rejected: an e-mail per flag; a separate quarantine screen in v1. (4) **Paging** (D3): receipts and sessions per business day, newest first, 50 a page up to `pos.list.max_page` (200), an opaque `(issued_at, document_id)` cursor, lines and tenders in two `= any(?)` queries, single-receipt and single-session reads, sessions as the full outer join of `till_session` and `till_session_close`. Rejected: offset paging (unstable while sales arrive). (5) **A quarantine row is resolved, never purged by time** (TWK-26, D-10): kernel V0084 adds `resolved_at`, `resolution CHECK IN ('REPAIRED','DISCARDED')`, `resolution_reason`, makes `raw_event` nullable, grants UPDATE on those columns only (the `object_upload` pattern, pinned by `SchemaRulesIntegrationTest`) with a transition trigger that lets a row be resolved once; `ResolveQuarantine` (sync slice, `sync.quarantine.resolve`, the MPCS administrator, reason code and text, audited, `sync.quarantine.resolved.v1`); a nightly job nulls `raw_event` of rows resolved more than `sync.quarantine.raw_retention_days` (default 30) ago; the row stays as the record that a fact was refused and why. For `FORBIDDEN_FIELD` alone the raw event is stored with the values of the offending keys replaced by `[removed]` (the key stays, so the person sees what the till sent and where; the value is never needed for the repair). Rejected: a time-based purge of unresolved rows (S4: "never lost"). (6) **Below the version floor, facts are still accepted** (TWK-09, D-2): after the grace `uploadBatch` is accepted with `FLOOR_NOTICE` in the ack, and `getSnapshot` and `listChanges` answer `426 sync.app_below_floor` instead, so the till keeps selling on its last snapshot and the staleness warning tells the supervisor. This is CR-30-1 point 4 ("facts are always accepted") applied to the code; doc 31 section 6 and doc 32 section 3.3 step 1 are the superseded text. Operations: `sync.app_version_floor` is not raised above a shipped version until CR-30-1's updater exists (`infra/deploy/README.md`). (7) **Day-close closes the location the envelope names** (TWK-23): the outbox row's `location_id`, set by central from the device record, never the payload's; a differing payload value is logged and ignored; the M6 `SessionHook` fact (`location.sessions_closed.v1`) is M6's own ticket. Deferred: M6-07 (voids and refunds: the till emits neither event; M5, M6 and M8 ship together when it does, with 26A's `RefundCrossSessionHandler` decisions; M7's consumer already exists, so the release note says the three must land in one release).
