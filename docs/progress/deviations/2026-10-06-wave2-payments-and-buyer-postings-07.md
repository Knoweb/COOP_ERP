- **Payments, cheques, the buyer's GRN posting, exposure per line and extension rows (branch `fix/m4-payments-cheques-and-buyer-grn-posting`), where the decisions `2026-10-06-wave2-payments-and-cheques.md` (1), (2), `2026-10-06-wave2-buyer-postings.md` (1), (3), (5), `2026-10-06-wave2-credit-once-per-line.md` (5) and `2026-10-06-wave2-extension-rows-after-issue.md` met code they did not foresee:** (1) **"the scope's side"** of M9's consumer is the issuer role of the document type in the kernel's registry (`kernel.document_type.issuer_role`, read through `DocumentTypes`): SELLER for INV, CN and PRC, BUYER for a GRN. Decision (3) says "records only the lines of the scope's side and logs BUYER lines at WARN", but the GRN's postings are BUYER lines published in the receiver's own scope (decision (1)), so a plain "SELLER only" would drop them; a line of the side that is not the issuer's is what is logged at WARN and left out; a type of neither side (HOLDER: M5's) keeps every line, as before; no new event field was needed. PR 14's `m9.journal.buyer` takes the lines of the side that is not the issuer's, in the counterparty's scope. (2) **The dedupe names the owner, but the table does not hold two owners of one document yet**: `integration.journal_posting` has `UNIQUE (document_id, seq)` (m9 V0001), so the seller's and the buyer's postings of one invoice would collide on seq 1 although the handler's check is now `(owner_entity_id, document_id)`. No m9 migration number is allocated to this PR, and nothing in it posts two owners under one document id (the GRN's own id is the buyer's alone), so the plan's test "M9 holds the seller's and the buyer's postings of one invoice apart" moves to PR 14, whose m9integration `V0006` must widen the key to `(owner_entity_id, document_id, seq)` before the buyer's INV and CN lines arrive (recorded in the M9 README as its next item). (3) **The frozen-contract check**: M1's `RelationshipQueries` gains `settlementRelationship` (CR-21A-7 section 6.1), which `tools/check-frozen-contracts.mjs` sees as a change to the frozen M1 contract; open PR #277 sets `info.x-change-request: CR-21A-7` for M1's other wave 2 change, and the check needs a value different from main's, so this PR raises `m1party.yaml` `info.version` to 1.4.2 (Java only, the slice's shape unchanged, explained in its header) rather than name the same change request; whichever of the two merges second keeps both lines. (4) **The GRN's cost is rounded per line**, `Σ round(received_qty × unit_cost, 2)`, not the decision's bare `Σ qty × unit_cost`: IssueInvoice rounds each line's net to the cent, and only the same rounding makes the GRN accrual clear per GRN–invoice pair (the property test draws four-decimal prices). (5) **Exposure per order line**: a GRN line counts an item of a drop once (CaptureGrn aggregates the drop's delivery lines of one item and references the first), so what it received and no invoice billed is shared over that item's delivery lines in line order, each up to its dispatched quantity and the last taking the rest (an over-delivery is billed); only delivery lines of issued notes count, because `fulfilled_qty` rises at IssueDeliveryNote, not at the draft. (6) **The settlement relationship is looked up on the date received**, as decided, not today; within the ACTIVE and SUSPENDED rows in force that day an ACTIVE one wins (only ACTIVE rows are kept apart by M1's exclusion constraint).
