- **A billed unit is credited once, and a credit note beyond what is due stays unapplied (wave 2: M4MONEY-01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 13, 14), decided on the architect's delegation (6 Oct, branch `docs/wave2-decisions`), CR-24A-3 items 1 and 2:** (1) **One rule for the three credit paths** (SettleDiscrepancy, ApproveClaim, IssueCreditNote): a shared read `InvoiceCredits.creditedByLine(invoiceId)` sums qty, net and tax over `kernel.document_line` rows of type CN referencing the invoice line (type-filtered: debit notes reference the same lines); `documents.lockForLinking(invoiceId)` is taken as soon as the invoice is known, before lines and credits are read; the cap is `remaining = billed - credited`; IssueCreditNote refuses a line named twice (`m4.creditnote.line_duplicate`) and a quantity over the remainder (`m4.creditnote.exceeds_billed`, carrying sku, billed, credited, remaining) and a scale over 3 (the existing `qty_invalid`; a guard, not `multipleOf`, which most validators evaluate in floating point and which would change the shared `Quantity` of every operation); SettleDiscrepancy credits `min(damaged, remaining)` and settles with no money when nothing remains; ApproveClaim **refuses** an accepted quantity above the remainder rather than capping it, so `claim_decision_line.approved_qty` and the credit note always agree. The remainder rule (M4MONEY-05): the last slice of a line credits `billed - credited` of net and tax exactly, so two half credits of a 2 x 0.3350 line credit 0.67 in total. (2) **A-1: a claim covers only units the GRN did not already record as damaged**: RaiseClaim's open quantity is `received - damaged_qty - claimedBefore` for every claim kind; the GRN's damaged quantity is SettleDiscrepancy's alone to credit. Rejected: the per-line cap as the only stop (two paths for one credit, and the buyer hears "exceeds billed" after the seller has already settled). (3) **A-2: a partial approval decides the approved quantity only**; `claimedBefore` counts `approved_qty` for APPROVED, `claimed_qty` for undecided, nothing for REJECTED, so the un-approved rest is free to claim again, as a rejection frees the whole. (4) **B-1: the credit note is issued for the goods in full; its money applies to the origin invoice only as far as it is due.** `requireDue` becomes `applyUpToDue(invoiceId, gross)`: the CREDITS link carries `min(gross, due)` and is not written when nothing is due (the kernel requires a positive amount); the unapplied amount is `gross - Σ its CREDITS links`, a sum, never stored (AGENTS.md); it lowers the buyer's exposure as 24A section 6.3's `unappliedCredits`; a new `ApplyCreditNote` (`POST /v1/trading/credit-notes/{id}/apply`, `bil.creditnote.issue`; guards: the seller's own issued CN, an open undisputed invoice of the same pair, `0 < amount <= min(unapplied, due)`; audit `CREDIT_NOTE_APPLIED`, event `credit_note.applied.v1`, 24A names both) writes a second CREDITS link under `lockForLinking` and recomputes that invoice's `credited_amount`; `doc_credit_note.invoice_document_id` keeps naming the origin invoice. Nothing is refunded: version 1 records money and never moves it (ADR-01); a refund, if the buyer wants one, happens outside the system. The seller's postings do not change (CN GOODS SELLER debits REVENUE and VAT_OUTPUT, credits RECEIVABLE, a credit balance on the receivable as an accountant books it). No migration: `kernel.document_link` keys on `(from, to, link_type)` and doc 24 section 3.6 already says an unapplied credit note "is applied later with a link". Rejected: a refund path (ADR-01); keeping the refusal (a dead end for a paid invoice with damaged goods, and it blocks the short-quantity settlement on the same discrepancy); an on-account balance table (a stored balance); a negative payment receipt (misuse of the PRC and its postings). (5) **Exposure per order line** (M4MONEY-14): the uninvoiced value of an accepted order line is `(allocated - fulfilled)` plus, per delivery line, `dispatched_qty` while no confirmed GRN names its drop (goods on their way will be billed; the in-transit question the reviewer left open is settled as "count it"), else `received_qty - invoiced_qty` of the GRN line, all floored at 0 and priced at `tier_price`; a short quantity is never owed (DR-2), a cancelled order owes nothing.
