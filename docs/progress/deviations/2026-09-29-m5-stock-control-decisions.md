- **Stock control, decided on the architect's delegation (29 Sep, branch `feat/m5-counts-writeoffs-repack`):** (1) **No CNT, ADJ or RPK document yet**: a count and its adjustment are one `count_task` with insert-only expectation and lines, a repack a `repack` row and its reversal a `repack_reversal` row, the movements citing their ids (as transfers do); the **WOF document is issued** (LOCATION series), because a write-off's photographs attach to a kernel document. The adjustment is approved on the count (`/counts/{id}/approve|reject`), not on `/adjustments`. (2) **One tolerance and two band limits per entity** as configuration items instead of the per-SKU/tag `variance_threshold` and `loss_tolerance` tables; the nine loss categories of F-01 are an API enum, not a `loss_category` table. (3) **The book at submit is the lot as it stands** (expectation at start plus every movement since, E-06/DR-8): no movement is read back, selling goes on, nothing is frozen; one open count per location so one variance is never posted twice. (4) **Schedule and start are two commands** that the web form runs together; FULL and SKUS scopes only. (5) **A remote witness is a witness at a single-staff location** (size band S or `inventory.single_staff`), allowed by `inventory.remote_witness_allowed` and only for a holder of inv.writeoff.approve; the witness may also approve, the requester never. (6) **The approver's limit** is the highest `max_value` of the permission on the approver's roles (none is no limit). (7) **Plan and execute a repack in one step, one input lot, the quantity taken chosen by the operator**; the expected output scales the recipe; the reversal restores the input at its cost. (8) **The demo's write-off is categorised EXPIRED on a lot whose printed expiry is still ahead**: the demo's opening stock carries future expiry dates, and no demo path creates an expired batch. (9) **The e2e approves nothing**: approval asks for a fresh second factor the development realm lacks; the spec proves a count within tolerance posting at once and the stock card showing it, and reads the demo's approved count.
