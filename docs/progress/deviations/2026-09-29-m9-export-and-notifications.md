- **M9's accounting export and notifications, decided on the architect's delegation (29 Sep, branch `feat/m9-accounting-export-notifications`):** (1) **Two tables beside 29A's**: `journal_posting` holds the postings of `journal.postings_ready.v1` as they arrive (M9 reads no module's tables), and `notification_contact` holds who at an entity is reached for a role code (M1 keeps no e-mail or telephone of its users, doc 21 section 9.3). (2) **The export is generated in the request**, not by the worker, as CSV only; its file is rebuilt from the lines on download, not kept in object storage, and the recorded SHA-256 proves it unchanged. (3) **29A's overlap guard is replaced by "each posting exported once"** (`journal_line.posting_id` unique): an export over a period already exported takes what arrived since, which is the supplement; the day-close guard and the provisional flag wait for M8's freshness query; acknowledge, regenerate and the unacknowledged-review job are not built. (4) **No `posting_map_registry`** and no posting-map viewer: the roles travel on the event. (5) **Templates and rules are rows of a migration**, not seed YAML; DefineTemplate/DefineRule, the TemplateValidator and entity-owned rules wait for their ticket; the read code `int.notify.view` is added. (6) **Templates, rules and contacts are readable by every session** (`USING (true)`): the dispatcher reads them in the event owner's scope, the renderer after the commit with none, and a buyer's contacts are read in the seller's scope; no operation serves a contact. (7) **The kernel's dispatcher** (kernel.internal, not kernel.api) takes the counterparty of an event with no `counterpartyEntityId` as the party of `sellerEntityId`/`buyerEntityId` that is not the owner, so ROLE_AT_COUNTERPARTY reaches the buyer of M4's documents. (8) **No IN_APP adapter** (no bell yet) and **no `provider_config`**: the SMS provider is `coop-erp.integration.sms.provider`, `log` only. (9) **Deferred**: API clients, the public API subset, providers and the health job, e-invoicing, the bundle assembler (M9-02, M9-05 rest, M9-06, M9-07).
