- **Claims and transfer requests, decided on the architect's delegation (29 Sep, branch `feat/m4-claims-transfer-requests`):** (1) **The seller's decision on a claim is the seller's row** (`claim_decision`, `claim_decision_line` with the accepted quantity per line, which records a partial acceptance), not 24A's decision columns on the buyer's `doc_claim`; InspectClaim is folded into the decision (its findings). (2) **The goods go back on the buyer's act**: 24A section 7 and 25A section 6.2 have M5 post RETURN_TO_SELLER on `claim.approved.v1`, but that event is delivered in the seller's scope, which cannot write the buyer's stock; the buyer's DispatchClaimReturn publishes `claim.return_dispatched.v1` in its own scope and M5 consumes that. The seller's re-receipt of the goods is not built. (3) **Photographs are added after the raise** (AddClaimPhoto), not referenced by RaiseClaim: the kernel presigns uploads for an existing document only. A claim with no photograph may be decided (the demo loader cannot upload); a PENDING or FAILED one blocks the decision. (4) **An approved claim is credited on the seller's invoice of the GRN**, at the invoice line's price and rate, never more than billed or than is due; no invoice yet refuses the approval (`m4.claim.invoice_first`), as for a damaged discrepancy. (5) **A transfer request is two rows** (the shop's request, the society's decision) and the transfer's id is not written back: M5's issue row names the request and M4 reads it (`InventoryQueries.transferOfRequest`); the shop may leave the source to the society, which names it on approval, because a shop session reads no other location (M1). Within one society only; a society asking its distributor stays an order. Reads carry `inv.stock.view`, which shop staff hold. (6) **M4-12 DropSnapshotContributor is deferred** until the till's GRN capture reads `expected_drop`. (7) **Kernel/api changes (additive)**: M5's published `IssueTransfer` and `TransferIssued` gain `transferRequestId` (old constructors kept), `InventoryQueries` gains `transferOfRequest`, M5's api gains `StockReturnedToSeller`; M4's `IssueCreditNoteHandler` helpers are public for the claim handler; no kernel change.
