- **Where the M5 stock-control fixes depart from the plan's letter (7 Oct, branches `fix/m5-expiry-limits-counting-and-cost` and `fix/m5-cost-rules`, plan PR 10):** (1) the cost rules, item (5) of `2026-10-06-wave2-stock-movements.md`, are their own PR as the plan allows, and `CostService` and `MovementType` live in `m5inventory` (`internal/ledger`, `api`), not in `shared-engine` as the plan's file list says; nothing in the engine changed; (2) an out movement "at a given cost" is an out movement whose `Movement.unitCost` is set (null keeps the entity average), so no new movement type was needed; (3) a count line's `counted_at` is held between the start of the count and now before the book is read, so a browser clock a little early or late neither reaches back before the count nor forward past submit; (4) a transfer issued by hand may still name an expired batch (moving it back to the stores to write it off); only the picks (`lockCandidates`, `pickBatches`) skip expired lots, as decided; (5) the short rows re-picked at dispatch are locked in FEFO order by the same `lockCandidates` the reservation uses, not in the ledger's `LotKey` order (decision (4) names write-off approval and transfer issue only); (6) a write-off whose every photograph FAILED where one is required answers `m5.writeoff.photos_required`, and a PENDING one `m5.writeoff.photos_incomplete`; (7) the repack's shortfall reason is the request's new optional `varianceReason`, kept in the `REPACK_YIELD_EXCEPTION` audit, with no column; (8) the till facts with no hook yet are announced as `till_fact.not_applied.v1` (a handler must publish), and `transfer.issued.v1`, `repack.executed.v1` and `writeoff.requested.v1` from central (no device on the scope) are ignored by that consumer; (9) the demo's write-off is witnessed by the society office (`m101-office`, granted `inv.writeoff.witness`) and approved by the manager, since the approver may no longer be the in-person witness.
