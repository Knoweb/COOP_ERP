- **What a retry needs, versus doc 19 section 7 "phone numbers are hashed in the log" (26 Sep, branch `fix/notifications-attachments-review`):** the log stays hashed; the recipient of a QUEUED notification is held in clear in `kernel.notification_pending` for at most 24 hours (the retry window doc 19 sets), readable by the owning entity alone, and cleared once the row is SENT, SUPPRESSED or FAILED. Without it a retry could run only on the instance that queued the notification, and after a restart on none. The alternative, re-resolving the audience in the sweep, does not cover a direct send (a reset code) and needs the event payload from the archive. Decided 27 Sep 2026: sealed at rest with AES-256-GCM under a key from configuration that the database never sees (PR #145), on the architect's delegation. The placeholders of M1's direct send are a temporary password, so clear text for a day in the table and its backups was not acceptable; pgcrypto was rejected because the key would travel in SQL text (statement log, `pg_stat_statements`), re-resolving at retry because it misses direct sends, a shorter retention because it is still clear text. The row keeps the ciphertext and a key id (kernel V0063); the table's RLS stays owner-only. `CR-19A-8` item 1.
