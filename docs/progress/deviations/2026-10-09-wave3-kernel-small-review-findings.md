- **Small kernel findings of wave 3 (9 Oct, branch `fix/kernel-small-review-findings`), where the fix met behaviour the finding did not name:** (1) KRN-16: asking again for an attachment or module object after its upload window ended was the documented way back for a till that lost its connection (doc 32 section 4, "resumable"); it is now refused with `attachment.upload_expired` / `object.upload_expired`, and the client starts a new attachment id or key. A renewal inside the window still works. (2) KRN-18: the finding suggested firings from 02:30 to 06:00 and one at start-up; the job instead fires every fifteen minutes all day and acts only at or after the fleet cut-off (`coop-erp.business-date.cutoff-time`, 02:30 Colombo), which covers a start-up and a worker down for longer than the morning, and is the shape the 26 Sep cut-off deviation foresaw for per-location cut-offs. `coop-erp.business-date.cutoff-cron` now sets the repetition, not the cut-off. (3) M7M8M9-04: the job scope is told from a person's by "no user id and the all-zero entity", which `JobExecution.federationView` and `SystemScope.federationView` both carry; dropping the two functions waits for a run that reports no legacy row.
