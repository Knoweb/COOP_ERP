- **A document without a location, versus 19A section 13 (26 Sep, branch `fix/job-runner-business-day-review`):** the business date is "never from the wall clock", but an entity-level document (an order, a discount) has no location and, when the issuer acts entity-wide, no scope location either; `IssuanceProtocol` then takes the calendar date in the business time zone. An issuer acting at a location gives the document that location's date. To resolve when an entity has a business date of its own, or 19A names the location such a document belongs to. Decided 27 Sep 2026: an entity has no business date of its own; a document with no location, issued entity-wide, takes the calendar date in the business time zone, and a document that concerns one location (a GRN, a count, a write-off, a DISC raised on a GRN) names that location and takes its date (PR #145), on the architect's delegation. Nothing closes an entity's day, so an entity date would be a second day-close with no event to drive it; making ORD or DISC name a location would change 24A (`doc_order` has none, and an order is the buying entity's). The code already did this; the Javadoc of `BusinessDate` and `IssuanceProtocol` and the order test now say it. For M3 and M4: an entity-level document's day is its local issue date. `CR-19A-8` item 6.
