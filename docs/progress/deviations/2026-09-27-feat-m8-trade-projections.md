- **M8's trade projection is one row per event, versus 28A section 3's `trade_document_fact`, one updated row per document (27 Sep, branch `feat/m8-trade-projections`; decided on the architect's delegation):** the events of one document are published by both parties (the buyer submits, the seller accepts), the consumer writes in the scope of each event's owner, and row-level security lets no party update the other's row; an inserted row per event is also idempotent by its key. A document's status is its latest event.
