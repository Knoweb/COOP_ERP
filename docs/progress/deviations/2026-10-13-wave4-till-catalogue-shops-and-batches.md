- **Which shops and which batches the M2 change log and the till snapshot carry (wave 4, TD-01 to TD-05), decided on the architect's delegation (13 Oct, branch `fix/till-catalogue-reaches-every-enrolled-shop`):** (1) TD-01: an M2 change is recorded at every shop whose till can be enrolled or still syncs. `party.location.status` has four values (PLANNED, ONBOARDING, ACTIVE, DORMANT; m1party V0001) and no closed one, and a location is never deleted, so `CatalogueShops` passes no status filter (as M7's `SocietyShops` does): every SHOP location of the entity or of the federation. A PLANNED shop with no till yet costs a version bump only. (2) TD-02 and TD-04: doc 32 section 5.1 puts the batches a till holds in the Lots table set, "batches with positive on-hand at the location", from M5 with the batch identity from M2; the M2 snapshot table set is "SKUs in the shop's assortment" with their units, conversions, barcodes, tags, tax categories and thumbnails, and no batches. The till reads no batch row (`Catalogue.kt` reads `sku` and `operator` only). So the closer of the two options given is to drop `batch` from M2's till contract: `CatalogueSnapshotContributor` no longer serves it and `M2ChangeLogFanOut` no longer consumes `batch.registered.v1` or `batch.corrected.v1`. No narrower M2 batch table was built (current batches of the shop's assortment SKUs): section 5.1 does not ask M2 for one, and nothing on the till would read it. The batches reach the till with M5's lot contributor, scoped to the location's on-hand and fanned out to the shops holding the lot; change-log rows named `batch` already written since #336 are skipped by `SnapshotBuilder` ("no snapshot contributor serves the table", logged) and age out with the retention. M1M2M3M5-10 (BatchCorrected has no M5 consumer) is unchanged. (3) TD-03: `CatalogueShops` takes its scopes from `ScopedExecution` (`own(entity)`, `federationView()`) and lists in `inOwnTransaction`, on a connection of its own, so `restoreScope` is gone; a new ArchUnit rule (`onlyTheKernelBuildsAFederationView`) refuses a class outside the kernel and the demo loader that both constructs a `ScopeContext` and names `PolicyClass.FEDERATION_VIEW`. (4) TD-05: `UserUpdated` is fanned out urgent when its payload's `userKind` is not TILL or BOTH or its `status` is not ACTIVE; the payload has no prior kind, so an update of a back-office user who was never at a till is urgent too, reaching only the shops of her roles.
