- **An attachment settles after its URL expires, versus 19A section 9's "every 5 minutes, for PENDING rows" (26 Sep, branch `fix/notifications-attachments-review`):** the verifier offers a row only once its pre-signed PUT (fifteen minutes) has expired, so COMPLETE can come up to twenty minutes after the upload; a write-off approval waiting on evidence (doc 32 section 4) waits that long. The alternative, copying to an immutable key or bucket versioning, is provider-specific and is for the architect. Decided 27 Sep 2026: settle-after-expiry stays, and both halves of the wait are configuration: the upload URL's validity is the register's `attachment.upload_url_minutes` (15 by default) and the verifier's schedule `coop-erp.attachment.verify-cron` (every five minutes); settling on an "upload complete" call with a server-side copy to an immutable key is ticket 6.14 of `docs/PLAN_TO_M2.md`, for M5 if its write-off approval measures the wait as too long (PR #145), on the architect's delegation. The rule is what keeps the verified bytes unchangeable; the lever costs one register item and one property, while the copy changes the key an attachment is read from and belongs with the one screen that needs it. `CR-19A-8` item 2.
