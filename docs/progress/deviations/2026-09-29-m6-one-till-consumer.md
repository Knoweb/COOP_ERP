- **M6 applies a till's sessions and receipts in one consumer, `m6.till` (29 Sep, branch `fix/m6-session-before-receipts`):** 26A section 10 names a ReceiptBundleHook and a SessionHook; they stay two handlers, but one consumer queue feeds both, because only one queue keeps a device's order (one active consumer, the relay publishing by `source_seq`). The old queues `m6.receipts` and `m6.sessions` are no longer declared; on a running stack they stay bound in RabbitMQ and collect copies nobody reads until deleted (`make reset`, or delete the two queues in the management UI).
