- **The buyer's journal (wave 2: M4MONEY-10, M9-02, M9-01), decided on the architect's delegation (6 Oct, branch `docs/wave2-decisions`), CR-19A-13 and CR-24A-3 item 5, CR-29-1 item 3:** the M4 fix review (E-1: the buyer acknowledges each invoice by a command of its own) and the M9 fix review (D3: the kernel delivers the seller's event to a consumer in the counterparty's scope) disagree; resolved as follows. (1) **The GRN's buyer postings are published now by `ConfirmGrnHandler`**, which already runs in the receiver's scope: `journal.postings_ready.v1` with side BUYER, `GRN GOODS BUYER` rows (INVENTORY / GRN_ACCRUAL) at `Σ received_qty × unit_cost`, where the GRN line's `unit_cost` is the delivery note's price snapshot, the same basis the invoice uses, so GRN_ACCRUAL clears per GRN–invoice pair (a property test says so); a GRN from a local supplier is the buyer's purchase and is published too. Both reviews agree on this part. (2) **The buyer's INV and CN postings arrive by counterparty delivery, not by a click.** The payable exists when the invoice is issued: the document is immutable, issued to the buyer, and already readable by the buyer (PARTY); 800 societies' books must not depend on an accounts clerk acknowledging every invoice, and a missed click is a missing payable (the M9 reviewer's point, which outweighs the M4 reviewer's "that is how accounts departments work": three-way matching is the GRN discrepancy flow, which M4 already has). So the kernel's dispatcher gains **counterparty delivery** (CR-19A-13): a consumer registered with `party = COUNTERPARTY` runs in the OWN entity-wide scope of the payload's `counterpartyEntityId`, only for events whose source is central (never a till's), with its own inbox row, and only after the kernel has checked that the named counterparty is `kernel.document.counterparty_entity_id` of the document the event names (a module cannot write into an arbitrary entity's scope). M4 adds `counterpartyEntityId` and the BUYER lines to the seller's `JournalPostingsReady` (`INV GOODS BUYER`: GRN_ACCRUAL / PAYABLE net, VAT_INPUT / PAYABLE tax; new `CN GOODS BUYER` rows: PAYABLE / INVENTORY net, PAYABLE / VAT_INPUT tax, added to `posting-map.yaml`); M9 registers `m9.journal.buyer`, which records the BUYER lines only, under `own_write`; the existing `m9.journal` records SELLER lines only. (3) **Interim, in the first M4 PR**: `JournalPostingsConsumer` records only the lines of the scope's side and logs BUYER lines at WARN, so no early M4 change files the buyer's books under the seller; `RecordJournalPostingsHandler` dedupes by `(owner_entity_id, document_id)` explicitly (the seller's and the buyer's postings of one invoice share the document id). (4) **PRC buyer rows (bank / payable) are not published**: the buyer's cash book is its own record and doc 24 section 3.9 lists PRC on the seller's side; left to the accountant's review of the roles (doc 10 A-01). (5) **D5: every posting carries its document's business date as issued** (`document.business_date`, CR-19A-8): `JournalPostingsReady` gains `LocalDate businessDate`, filled by all six publishers (the cheque reversal takes the reversal PRC's date); M9 reads it and falls back to `occurredAt` only for events published before the change and replayed. A receipt recorded on 2 October for a cheque dated 30 September is an October document; `receivedOn` is a reference, not the date (doc 29 section 6.3: late paper is a supplement).
