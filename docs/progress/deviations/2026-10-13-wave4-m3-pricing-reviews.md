- **M3's central basket, pricing reviews and the audit review reader (wave 4, M3R-01 to M3R-10), decided on the architect's delegation (13 Oct; CR-19A-14):** (1) M3R-08: `kernel.api.ScopedExecution` and `DefaultScopedExecution` are removed; only a `@ScheduledJob` takes another entity's scope, through `JobExecution` (CR-19A-7). The two review consumers now only record the event in `pricing.review_trigger` (m3pricing V0007, the RLS template) in the scope the dispatcher gives them, and `PricingReviewJob` (`pricing-review`, every minute at :15 by default, `coop-erp.m3.pricing-review-cron`) reads the unchecked rows with `federationView()`, raises each review with `RaiseReview` in the list owner's `ownScopeOf`, and marks the row checked with `CheckReviewTrigger` in the row owner's scope (audit `PRICING_REVIEW_CHECKED`, INFO; event `pricing.review_check_completed.v1`). Reviews therefore appear within a minute of the event, not in the consumer's transaction. Both job commands carry `prc.pricelist.author` (a code the slice carries, as `tools/check-permissions.mjs` requires; the job has no user, so it is not checked). `ArchitectureTests.onlyScheduledJobsTakeAnEntitysScope` now also refuses, outside the kernel, `JobExecution.federationView`/`systemScope` outside a `@ScheduledJob` method and any call of another kernel.api method that returns a `ScopeContext` (bar `CurrentScope.get` and `ScopeContext` itself). (2) `AuditReviewReader` stays in kernel.api, generic: the caller passes the event codes (M3 its three), and kernel V0093's `list_audit_reviews(text[], integer)` answers only REVIEW types. (3) M3R-09: V0093 creates the NOLOGIN role `kernel_audit_reader` (with `createrole_self_grant = 'set'`, so the migrator gets SET, not INHERIT), which owns both definer functions and alone holds SELECT on `kernel.audit_event` (policy repeating the OWN/FEDERATION_VIEW test) and `audit_event_type`; `app_seed`'s grant and `audit_event_seed_read` policies (parent and partitions) are dropped. The index V0091 built without CONCURRENTLY stays: V0091 has run on every database, a merged migration is never edited, and nothing is left to build; no production database exists yet, so the lock it took was on small logs only. (4) M3R-02: tenders are mapped as doc 23 section 3.5 prices them (cash rounds, other tenders exact): CASH, CARD, KHATA as the engine's ACCOUNT, CHEQUE as CARD; VOUCHER is out of M3's scope (doc 23 section 1) and refused with 400 `m3.basket.tender_not_priced`; a fourth decimal of a quantity is 400 `m3.basket.qty_precision` (both mapped in the kernel's `ProblemResponses`). (5) M3R-04: "in force" is the newest PUBLISHED or SUPERSEDED version with `apply_from` on or before today (as `PriceListStore.versionInForce`), for both reviews; a gazette compares the lines in its own unit, a printed MRP the lines in the SKU's base unit; no conversion. (6) M3R-07: `status=SETTLED` answers none (no settle command yet) and every review is OPEN; `escalated` is taken out of the contract (no source for it until the exception-queue flow); the owner and the SKU code and name (in the caller's language) are filled. (7) M3R-03: `snapshotVersion` is null at central (not required); `BatchCandidate.thumbKey` and `printedMrp` are optional, central has no image per batch.
