- **Where the M6 receipt flags and paged lists depart from the plan's letter (6 Oct, branch `fix/m6-receipt-flags-and-paged-lists`, plan PR 13):** (1) `NUMBER_JUMP` "raises by at most the gap" without a kernel change: PR 03's `observeDeviceNumber` raises to the number it is given and reports the next number it had, so `RecordReceiptHandler` first observes number 1 (it moves only a series never used, which the receipt moves anyway) to learn the outcome and the next number, then observes the receipt's number or `next - 1 + pos.series.max_jump`; a `maxRaise` parameter on the kernel call would be cleaner and is left for the next kernel numbering change; (2) `SERIES_FOREIGN` is flagged for the kernel's FOREIGN and also for UNKNOWN on a device scope (a series id that does not exist or that the device cannot see, such as another entity's), since both are "not the device's own series"; (3) `RECEIPT_REPLAY_DIFFERS` is an ALERT audit record, not a flag on the receipt, because the first copy is kept and receipt rows are never updated; the session close's twin is named `TILL_SESSION_CLOSE_REPLAY_DIFFERS`; (4) the business-day filter is `issued_at` (a session's open, or its close for an orphan) between two midnights in the business time zone, as the fix review advised (it uses `receipt_by_location`), not the till's own `business_date` column; (5) the single reads are `getPosReceipt` and `getTillSession`, because `getReceipt` (M5) and `getSession` (session slice) are taken in doc 33's one operationId namespace; (6) the cross-tenant 404 is proved at the query (another entity's scope reads nothing under RLS), and the controller answers 404 for an empty read; (7) the Playwright case answers the receipts list with one flagged receipt (the demo data never holds one; a flagged receipt needs a till that breaks its own contract) and signs in as `m101-manager` (si) and `m103-manager` (ta); (8) the shop's exception items from REVIEW and ALERT records (decision (3), 28A `exception_item`) are M8's and not in this PR's file list; the new ALERT and REVIEW codes are there for it.
