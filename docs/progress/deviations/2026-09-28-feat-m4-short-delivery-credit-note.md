- **Settling a discrepancy, credit notes and invoice disputes, decided on the architect's delegation (28 Sep, branch `feat/m4-short-delivery-credit-note`):** (1) **No double credit (architect's decision, 28 Sep):** DR-2 stands, the invoice bills the RECEIVED quantity (ownership passes at the GRN). A short quantity was never billed, so SettleDiscrepancy settles it with no money: the seller accepts the buyer's count and the discrepancy reads SETTLED, with who and when, from the seller's own row in `trading.discrepancy_settlement` (V0005). Only quantity billed but not good is credited: damaged quantity is inside the received quantity (`doc_grn_line` CHECK damaged_qty <= received_qty) and so was invoiced; the settlement issues a credit note for exactly it, at the invoice line's price and VAT rate, never more than the line billed, and refuses (`m4.discrepancy.invoice_first`) until the invoice exists. An over-delivered quantity is billed as received and not credited here. 24A section 6's "ACCEPT_SHORT → IssueCreditNote" assumes an invoice at the dispatched quantity, which this build does not have; that line of 24A does not apply while DR-2 holds. (2) The seller never writes the buyer's discrepancy (AGENTS.md idea 3), so 24A's two-step propose/accept is collapsed into the seller's acceptance of the buyer's count; the settlement takes `bil.creditnote.issue` (with its MFA; DR-1 waives it only after an accepted proposal).
