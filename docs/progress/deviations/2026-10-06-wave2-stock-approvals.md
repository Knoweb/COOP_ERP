- **Approval and witness controls on stock (wave 2: M5-09, M5-10, M5-11, M5-14, M5-16, M5-18, M1A-04), decided on the architect's delegation (6 Oct, branch `docs/wave2-decisions`), CR-25A-1 items 2 to 4 and CR-21A-7 item 2:** (1) **Approval limits fail closed** (M5-09, D2): the approver's authority is the role grant's `max_value` (doc 25 section 7: "writeoff_bands as role limits (M1)"); m1security V0019 sets `limits_schema = {"properties":{"max_value":{"type":"number","minimum":0}},"required":["max_value"]}` on `inv.writeoff.approve` and `inv.adjust.approve` (the YAML too, for fresh databases; the seeder never updates an existing row); a grant without `max_value` (seeded templates, legacy roles) approves up to `inventory.approval_band1_limit` only; no grant at the write-off's entity is no authority (`m5.approval.limit_exceeded`, limit 0); the highest `max_value` over the user's assignments at the entity counts; the line is valued at the entity average (doc 10 A-05), falling back to the lot's `unit_cost`, and a zero-cost line routes to band 2 so it is never self-approved. Band 1 (≤ Rs 25,000) society accountant or branch manager; band 2 (≤ Rs 250,000) general manager; band 3 the chairman, with the board resolution reference in the approval reason; the two figures stay ENTITY items; the demo approver roles get explicit `max_value` in `demo-users.demo.sql`. Rejected: no limit = unlimited (today's fail-open on the highest-fraud-risk lifecycle, doc 25 section 9.4); bands from configuration only (one approver for every band); one permission per band (against doc 19's limits mechanism). (2) **A granted limit never exceeds the grantor's** (M1A-04, D9): for each numeric property of a `limits_schema`, the requested value is at or below the grantor's effective value for that code at the entity (the highest over their holdings; a holding with no limits is unlimited); enforced in `RoleGuards.permissions` (CreateRole, AmendRole) and `AssignRole.withinGrantor` as `m1.role.limit_exceeds_grantor`; shipped in the same PR as the first `limits_schema`, since alone it has nothing to test against. (3) **The approver differs from the in-person witness** (M5-10, D5): refused as `m5.writeoff.approver_is_witness` unless `remote_witness` is true (a single-staff location's remote witness holds approve and may also approve, photographs being the third eye); no SoD seed (an INSTANCE pair would also forbid the remote case). Doc 25 section 3.8 asked only witness ≠ requester and approver ≠ requester; this adds the third distinction where three people exist. (4) **A failed photograph upload does not block a write-off for ever** (M5-11): the witness accepts when no photo is PENDING, every photo is COMPLETE or FAILED and at least one is COMPLETE where required; FAILED ones are listed in the WITNESSED audit; evidence stays fixed after issue. (5) **Count tolerance by value too** (M5-14, D6): a line auto-posts only within quantity/percent **and** `inventory.count_tolerance_value` (ENTITY, default Rs 1,000); if the count's auto-posted total exceeds `inventory.count_autopost_value_cap` (ENTITY, default Rs 10,000) every line goes to review; a surplus on a lot whose book was ≤ 0 is always reviewed; every tolerance-cleared line is a REVIEW `COUNT_TOLERANCE_CLEARED` for the exception report (doc 25 section 9.4). Rejected: the per-SKU/tag tolerance tables (25A; deferred by deviation 15); value-only tolerance (pennies on fast movers would all escalate). (6) **Repack yield tolerance** (M5-18, D8): `inventory.repack_yield_tolerance_pct` (ENTITY, default 2) around the recipe's expected output; over-yield beyond it is refused on the web (`m5.repack.yield_out_of_range`: sellable stock from nothing), under-yield beyond it needs a reason and is REVIEW `REPACK_YIELD_EXCEPTION`; a future till repack bundle flags and never refuses. (7) **A self-received transfer is allowed and flagged** (M5-16): REVIEW `TRANSFER_SELF_RECEIVED` when `received_by = issued_by`; refusing would break the demo loader, and inside one entity the shop's count shows a loss in transit anyway.
