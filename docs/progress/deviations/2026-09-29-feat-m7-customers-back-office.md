- **M7 back office, decided on the architect's delegation (29 Sep, branch `feat/m7-customers-back-office`):** (1) **The ledger has no UPDATE at all**: 27A's `account_posting.allocated_amount` (with its UPDATE grant) is the sum of the allocation rows, computed on read. (2) **Postings are the account's, not the shop's**: `location_id` is `sold_at_location_id` and the policies have no location line, so a till at any shop reads the account's whole balance (the balance cache is the sum of all postings). (3) **`customer_account.owner_entity_id` is the MPCS** (no generated `mpcs_entity_id`), and the account carries a per-society `account_no`. (4) **Tags are never deleted**: `customer_tag.removed_at`. (5) **`doc_customer_payment` is not partitioned**; DSAR and banking tables come with their tickets. (6) **`cus.customer.view`** is the read code (27A names none). (7) **OpenAccount asks no fresh MFA yet**; the step-up comes with AmendLimit. (8) **Duplicate detection**: phone per 27A 6.1, NIC at account opening (`m7.account.nic_held`), names shown as likely duplicates on the register screen, not a guard. (9) **Demo charges** go through `PostAccountTender` (the handler the `m7.tenders` consumer feeds) with the till's system scope and `DEMO-KHATA-...` numbers, since the till does not yet write ACCOUNT tenders. (10) **Deferred**: limits, hard block, cap, suspend/close; CprBundleHook and reversal; snapshot contributor; statements as documents and SMS; adjustments; privacy requests and the anonymiser (not cheap without the DSAR workflow); banking.
