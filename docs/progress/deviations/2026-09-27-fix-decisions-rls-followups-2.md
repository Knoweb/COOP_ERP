- **"Today" for a trading relationship (27 Sep, branch `fix/decisions-rls-followups`; review of PR #143):** `SuspendRelationshipHandler` took today from the UTC clock while `AmendRelationshipTermsHandler` used the business time zone, so between midnight and 05:30 in Colombo a suspension also caught a row that had ended the day before. **Decided 27 Sep 2026: both read `RelationshipRules.businessToday(clock, zone)`, the calendar date in `coop-erp.business-timezone`, on the architect's delegation.** Options weighed: `BusinessDate` (19A section 13) is per location and a relationship has none, so it does not apply; a new kernel method for an entity's today (one more kernel contract for one module's need, over the same zone); the business time zone through one shared helper (chosen: the kernel's own zone property, which `IssuanceProtocol` also uses for a document with no location; one line to change if an entity business date is ever defined). It agrees with CR-19A-8 (#145, merged meanwhile): an entity has no business date of its own, and an entity-level fact takes the calendar date in the business time zone. `RelationshipHandlersTest` proves it at 00:30 Colombo.
