- **INV-07 shortfall draft boundary (22 Oct, branch `feat/INV-07-short-transfer-receipts`):** a positive short transfer receipt creates one destination-owned, insert-only `transfer_shortfall` DRAFT with `DAMAGED_IN_TRANSIT` lines and the frozen outbound costs, in the receipt transaction. It is intentionally an unposted, non-fiscal discrepancy record rather than an issued kernel ADJ document: M5 has no standalone ADJ issuance, approval or accounting lifecycle, so it has no ADJ number and no submit, approve, reject, close or M9 posting operation. TRANSFER_OUT remains the issued quantity and TRANSFER_IN is only the received quantity; creating or later resolving this record must never post COUNT_ADJUST, WRITE_OFF or another transfer movement. Cancellation and offline till receipt remain outside this phase.
