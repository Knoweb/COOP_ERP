- **M8's dashboard, exception queue and catalogue, decided on the architect's delegation (29 Sep, branch `feat/m8-dashboard-exceptions`):** (1) **Definitions and tiles are data in YAML seeds** read at start and validated (`DefinitionValidator`), not rows of `report_definition`/`dashboard_tile`: the register/activate commands, the audience check per definition, the definition editor and user pins (`user_dashboard`) wait for the editor; 28A's fixture render per definition is replaced by the one shared template the run test renders. (2) **Which tiles a user sees is decided by the data**, not a role list: a tile whose source has nothing for the scope is left out. (3) **The exception queue is computed on read** from the projections, not 28A's `exception_item` fed from the audit log; acknowledgement (kernel AUDIT_REVIEWED) and the escalation job with `exception_report.generated.v1` are not built; "escalated" is a flag (age over `reporting.exception_escalate_after`, or a discrepancy past its window). A bounced cheque stays listed until acknowledgement exists. (4) **The credit limit M8 knows is the one the latest `exposure.warning.v1` carried** (M8 reads no module's tables and `credit_limit.changed.v1` names no parties); exposure is recomputed with 24A's formula over the projections, and a buyer is listed while its exposure is at or past the lowest threshold any warning of the relationship crossed. 28A's `exposure_snapshot` is not built. (5) **Fill rate** is received over expected per GRN line, a line capped at what it expected; **on time** is a GRN received on or before the latest committed ETA of the orders its delivery note carried; both over the last eight weeks. (6) **Shop sales are `shop_sale_fact`/`shop_sale_line_fact`** from `receipt.issued.v1`, not partitioned, without tenders or cost, in place of 28A's `receipt_fact`/`receipt_line_fact`, `sales_daily` and `cash_daily` (no end-of-day). (7) The dashboard cache keys on the events this instance applied, so the web instance serves a counterparty's new figures only after the 60 s; the new config items `reporting.dashboard_cache_seconds` and `reporting.exception_escalate_after` are in the kernel's register (`seed/kernel/config-items.yaml`, module m8reporting). (8) An invoice disputed twice keeps its first DISPUTED row (the key is document, kind, owner), so a second dispute after a resolution is not listed; a row per event id waits for a change of that key.
