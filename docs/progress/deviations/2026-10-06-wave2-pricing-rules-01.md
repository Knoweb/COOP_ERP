- **Where the pricing rules fix departs from the plan's letter (6 Oct, branch `fix/m3-pricing-rules-and-backdating`, plan PR 12):** (1) a RETAIL line's price above zero is the handler's `price_zero` guard only (at SetLines and again at publication), not also `exclusiveMinimum: 0` in the slice: `SetLinesRequest` does not name the list's kind and the same `PriceListLineInput` serves TRADE and ADVISORY lines, so the schema cannot bound RETAIL alone without refusing a zero trade price the decision did not touch; the slice's description says so; (2) the warning "a fixed price at or above the current list price" at activation is not built: `ActivateRule` answers a bare id, so a warning needs a response shape change (and the engine already ignores such a rule, M3-01); it goes with the rule screen's next change; (3) `till/` is not touched: the till does not depend on `shared-engine` yet, so there is nothing to bump there, and the till must take engine 0.2.0 when it starts pricing with it; (4) the markdown's batch is judged only on the identified batch (scan under BARCODE_RESOLVED or a SKU without printed MRP, pick under PICKER) else the FEFO-first candidate, and the line is stamped with it only when the markdown applies; without one, the line keeps the lowest-MRP batch as before; (5) the five test classes pin the clock through a shared `@TestConfiguration` (`testsupport/PinnedClock`, a `@Primary` fixed `Clock` at 23:59:59.9 Colombo of the previous day) rather than `HistoricalTime`, which is off in tests and is for the demo loader only; the five classes share one extra Spring context; (6) the `before` of `CONTROL_PRICE_ENTERED` now also carries the superseded ceiling's id and previous end date, not only the retrospective entry's audit.
