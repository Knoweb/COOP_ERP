- **M4's mutations under the kernel's document ownership, versus 24A sections 3 and 6 (27 Sep, branch `feat/m4-01-trading-scaffold`):** 24A has the seller write on the buyer's order (AcceptOrder: allocations, tier price, ETA, lock, status ACCEPTED; IssueDeliveryNote: fulfilled quantities) and the receiver write on the seller's delivery note (ConfirmGrn: `drops.markReceived`), and gives every extension table `owner_entity_id` and a partition key. The kernel base as built admits a document's rows to its owner only (`kernel.document_owned`, K-07; the template's fourth case), and AGENTS.md idea 3 says no record is written by two parties. **Decided 27 Sep 2026 on the architect's delegation (`CR-24A-1`, accepted):** the seller's decision is the seller's own record (`trading.order_allocation`, `order_allocation_line`, owner the seller, counterparty the buyer, `party_read`), the order's ACCEPTED/REJECTED/LOCKED/FULFILLED states are derived by the queries, a drop's RECEIVED and a note's CLOSED state are read from the receiver's confirmed GRNs, the extension tables carry no owner column and follow their header, `doc_grn_line` carries the batch id written after issuance (the synthetic batch number needs the GRN's number and the kernel line is frozen), `trd.document.view` is the read code of the slice, and nothing is partitioned until the base is. Options weighed: a `party_write` policy for the counterparty (two writers on one record; the template says nothing else); a numbered answer document per step (a new type per answer, not in the registry). Reasons: the design already says it (idea 3, doc 18 section 3.7, the K-07 policies), the shape is the kernel's own, and each part is one table or one ALTER away from 24A's wording. 24A sections 3 and 6 and doc 24 sections 3.1 to 3.3 are to be re-issued.
