- **Pricing rules (wave 2: RLS-04, RLS-05, M3-01, M3-02, M3-03, M3-05, M3-07, M3-09, TWK-13), decided on the architect's delegation (6 Oct, branch `docs/wave2-decisions`), CR-23A-1:** (1) **Only the Federation writes a ceiling, in SQL as well as in the handler** (m3pricing V0006): `own_write` and `own_update` on `pricing.control_price` add `owner_entity_id = (SELECT kernel.system_entity())` beside the class and entity tests (the `fed_admin` form of CR-21A-1 item 2; `OwnPoliciesTestTheClassIntegrationTest` still finds the class test); `everyone_reads` admits the Federation's rows only, so a row that is somehow not the Federation's is a ceiling for nobody; `advisory_read` on `price_list` and `price_list_line` admits the Federation's PUBLISHED ADVISORY lists only. `mrp_policy` is left alone: 23A section 3 keys it by owner and the demo gives `prc.mrp_policy.set` to the society manager. (2) **A suspended buyer does not read the live trade list** (RLS-05, D-3): `buyer_read` on `price_list` requires `r.status = 'ACTIVE'`; the invoice is the record of the price a suspended buyer was charged, and `party_names_read` already uses ACTIVE. (3) **A control price may be backdated by at most `pricing.control_price_backdate_days`** (FEDERATION item, default 7) to the gazette's own effective date; such an entry is a REVIEW audit `CONTROL_PRICE_RETROSPECTIVE` carrying the superseded row's previous `effective_to` in `before`; anything older is refused (`m3.control_price.effective_from_past`). The table is the legal (effective-time) history; the audit row is the knowledge time. Rejected: refusing every past date (the system would record a ceiling later than the law says, understating overcharges); any past date (rewrites history silently). (4) **An expiry markdown follows the identified batch** (D4): the scanned (BARCODE_RESOLVED) or picked (PICKER) batch, else the FEFO-first in-date batch with stock; it matches only when `0 <= days <= D`; a candidate for pricing is `onHand > 0 && (expiry == null || expiry >= businessDate)`; the line is stamped with that batch so M5 depletes it, while `mrpApplied` stays the policy's MRP; `ENGINE_VERSION` goes 0.1.0 → 0.2.0 because results change and the till ships the same engine. Zero-benefit line rules are dropped before `choose` (M3-01), as the bill path already does. Rejected: the lowest-MRP candidate (discounts the wrong units and drifts the per-batch book); markdown only when a batch is scanned (most packs carry a GTIN without a batch). (5) **No free goods through a price** (D11 and TWK D-5): `FIXED_PRICE > 0`, `PERCENT_OFF < 100`, and a RETAIL list line's price `> 0` (`exclusiveMinimum: 0` in the slice and a `price_zero` guard, because a job or a till could set lines without HTTP); giving goods away is a DONATION or SAMPLES write-off (two signatures, witness, bands) or a FREE_ITEM rule; a fixed price at or above the current list price warns at activation; existing zero lines, if any, are corrected by a new list version, never edited. (6) `PublishPriceList` takes the list row `FOR UPDATE` and re-checks DRAFT (M3-05); the five test classes that keep `LocalDate.now(Asia/Colombo)` in a field take a fixed `Clock` (M3-09).
