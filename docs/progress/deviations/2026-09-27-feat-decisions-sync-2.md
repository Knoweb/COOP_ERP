- **The till's audit rows are applied in the ingestion transaction, not by a consumer of the outbox (27 Sep, branch `feat/decisions-sync`):** doc 32 section 3.1 names the kernel audit store as the handler of `audit.*` and 19A section 8's loop calls each handler inside the chunk's transaction; a consumer after the outbox could not quarantine a row it cannot read (the event would already be APPLIED). The rows are written by `DeviceAuditWriter`, not `AuditFacade`, because they keep the till's id, time and operator; `KernelRecorder` therefore does not see them and their tests read `kernel.audit_event`. Accepted codes are the catalogue's `offline_capturable` types; M6 seeds its own (MODE_SWITCH, DRAWER_OPEN_NO_SALE ...).
