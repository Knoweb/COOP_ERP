- **M7's credit book: states, credits and the till's facts (wave 2: M7CR-04, M7CR-05, M7CR-06, M7CR-07, M7CR-08, M7CR-14), decided on the architect's delegation (6 Oct, branch `docs/wave2-decisions`), CR-27A-1 items 1 and 2:** (1) **REOPEN**: `ChangeAccountStatus` gains `REOPEN`, CLOSED → **SUSPENDED** (not OPEN: the till keeps refusing tenders until the officer REINSTATEs), reason required, `cus.account.manage`, no MFA (doc 27 puts it on open and limit increases only), history `REOPENED` (m7customers V0004 recreates the CHECK on `account_history.action`), audit `ACCOUNT_REOPENED`, event `account.reopened.v1`. The officer's path after `ACCOUNT_CHARGED_NOT_OPEN` or a flagged till CPR is reopen, settle or reverse, close again. Rejected: settling on a CLOSED account (an implicit state machine in four handlers' guards); an automatic reopening by a till fact (central's posting would change a state, against the module's rule that states change what the till accepts, not what central posts). (2) **Credits settle charges** (M7CR-08): CREDIT and negative ADJUSTMENT postings allocate through `allocation` rows (`payment_posting_id` has no foreign key, so no migration): a CREDIT first against the open charge with the same `document_id` (a void's `receipt.voided.v1` carries the receipt's id), then oldest first; a negative ADJUSTMENT oldest first; a reversal re-applies the account's still-unallocated credit oldest first in the same handler; `Ledger.unallocated` is defined over the whole credit side (`PAYMENT`, `REVERSAL`, `CREDIT`, negative `ADJUSTMENT`), so `Σ open charges − unallocated = balance` holds by construction and is the property test under random interleavings of charges, credits, payments, reversals and adjustments, with `Σ allocations of a charge ≤ charge`; the statement's `settled` CASE follows. Rejected: leaving credits unallocated and documenting it (ageing is what a society acts on when it suspends for arrears, doc 27 section 6.3; a 90+ bucket on a voided sale would suspend a member who owes nothing). (3) **Deactivation and the snapshot** (M7CR-05): the guard is `status in ('OPEN','SUSPENDED') and balance <> 0`; the snapshot contributor returns ACTIVE customers only; `PostAccountTender` writes REVIEW `ACCOUNT_CHARGED_CUSTOMER_INACTIVE` (status and receipt number only) for a charge on a customer that is not ACTIVE, which also serves the anonymised case; the account is left untouched (27A); reactivation is not built and is recorded as deferred. (4) **A tender central cannot post is a flagged fact, not a refusal** (M7CR-06): the consumer's `uuid`, `decimal`, `date` helpers return null on unparseable text and `tenderSeq` becomes nullable; the handler keeps `m7.tender.malformed` only for a null receipt document id or an unknown kind (a contract breach with no subject to flag) and writes REVIEW `ACCOUNT_TENDER_MALFORMED` on the receipt (receipt number, the missing fields, the amount as text) for a null account, amount, date or seq, a non-positive amount or more than two decimals, posting nothing; the guard lives in the handler because `DemoCustomers` calls it too. Rejected: posting a rounded amount (records an amount the till did not send). (5) The existing-posting check moves under the account lock (M7CR-07); the dedupe table is deferred: `InboxGuard.applyOnce` and the gateway's content hash are the guards, the lock serialises the rest, and the README says so. (6) **M7 feeds the change log alone, from a consumer of its own events** (M7CR-14): `CustomerChangeLogFanOut` (`m7.snapshot`) on `customer.*` and `account.*` events, running in the entity-wide scope the dispatcher gives a central event, lists the society's shops and calls `ChangeLog.append` with `Change.upsert("customer", id)`, urgent for suspended, closed, reopened, deactivated and anonymised, non-urgent for balances; always UPSERT (the builder emits the tombstone when the contributor no longer returns the row); no coalescing (a row per charge per shop at 30 days' retention is acceptable and `lastChangePerRow` dedupes per delta); states first, balances second. The platform pair confirms before work starts whether a central event published inside a shop-scoped consumer carries the shop as `locationId`; if it does, the fan-out lists shops through a small SECURITY DEFINER `customers.shops_of()` under the cross-tenant pattern or an entity-wide `PartyQueries` call. M1 and M2 feed the change log in their own tickets.
