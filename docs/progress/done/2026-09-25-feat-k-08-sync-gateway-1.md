- **K-08 sync gateway, first part (25 Sep, branch `feat/k-08-sync-gateway-1`, started on `feat/k-02-identity-2`, merged with main after M1-04 to M1-09).** Doc 32 as 19A section 8 restates it, the half the conformance cases of channel 1 need; the snapshot builder, the rate limiter and the till simulator are the second part. **Slice** `openapi/sync.yaml` (served by the kernel: `build.gradle.kts` generates it into `kernel.sync.web.generated`, so `sync` is not a module): enrolment codes, enrolment, batches, heartbeat, the change log, the snapshot (501 until part 2), attachment presign; the envelope (`TillEvent`), the acknowledgement, the instructions, every refusal as a problem document. **Schema** (`kernel/V0080`, lane D): `device_sync_cursor` (the only sync state: last applied, last batch and its acknowledgement, the batch in flight, gap count, resend request), `sync_event` (one row per sequence a device sent, applied or quarantined; an event id applied once), `sync_quarantine` (raw, with its reason), `device_heartbeat`, `device_enrolment_code` (hash only), `location_snapshot_version` and `change_log` (written only through the `SECURITY DEFINER` function `kernel.change_log_append`, as `app_seed`). **Device authentication** (`DeviceAuth`): a token with `cls = DEVICE` and `dev` is the device, not a user; its scope is OWN at its shop from M1's `party.device` (read as the federation viewer, cached 60 s, emptied by M1's `device.*` events through `DeviceCacheInvalidator`); SUSPENDED and RETIRED are 403 with a revoke instruction signed Ed25519 (`TillSigner`, the key from `coop-erp.sync.signing.*`, an instance key when unset); ENROLLED or unassigned is 403 `sync.device_not_active`. The scope filter refuses a user token on a device operation (`sync.device_token_required`) and a device token anywhere else (`sync.device_token_not_allowed`). **Enrolment** (`EnrolmentService`): an administrator (`sys.device.manage`, with the catalogue's MFA flag, under the enforcement switch) issues a sixteen-character code for a device of its entity; the till presents it once, without a token, with its hardware serial, and receives its credential (`DeviceCredentials` in `kernel.api`, implemented by `KeycloakDeviceCredentials`: one confidential client per device whose client-credentials tokens carry `dev` and `cls = DEVICE`), the next sequence, the series of its position (and of its location when it is the primary till), the snapshot pointer and the signing key; `DEVICE_SYNC_ENROLLED` and `device.sync_enrolled.v1` for M1. **Batch ingestor** (`BatchIngestor`, `IngestTransactions`, `EventApplier`): shape and DR-1 limits (413), the application floor (426), the cursor claimed with `FOR UPDATE NOWAIT` and an in-flight marker (409 `sync.batch_in_flight`), the same batch again answered with its stored acknowledgement, a gap 409 with `expected_seq` (repeated: `SYNC_ANOMALY`), a replay answered from state, the new events in chunks of `sync.ingest.chunk_size`, each accepted into the outbox with the device as source and its sequence as `source_seq` (`DeviceEventWriter`; the module consumers get them from there) or quarantined (SCHEMA, DUPLICATE_ID, HASH over the bundle's document and lines by the `ContentHash` rule, TOO_LARGE, FORBIDDEN_FIELD) with `SYNC_EVENT_QUARANTINED` (ALERT) and `sync.anomaly.v1`; `sync.batch_received.v1` per batch; gzip bodies inflated by `GzipRequestFilter`. **Heartbeat** (`HeartbeatService`): the fleet-health fields of doc 32 section 6 into the kernel's table, the clock offset, `SYNC_CLOCK_DRIFT` (REVIEW) once per drift, `RESEND_FROM` with `SYNC_CURSOR_BEHIND` (REVIEW) when the device holds more acknowledged than central (a restore), `FLOOR_NOTICE`, the urgent-change flag; `SyncStatus` (`kernel.api`, which M1-06 declared with `drained(deviceId)` and a stub that answered false) is now `JdbcSyncStatus`: `drained` keeps M1-06's signature and reads in the caller's transaction under its scope (drained when the last heartbeat reported nothing pending, no batch is in flight and central holds all the device holds acknowledged; unknown is false), and `state(deviceId, ctx)` is added for M1's device list. The device directory takes an unassigned device's location from M1-06's `party.device.location_id`, and the device cache also empties on `device.revoked.v1`. **Change log**: `ChangeLog.append(targets, changes, applyFrom, urgent, ctx)` in the producer's transaction, one version per publication per shop; `GET /v1/sync/locations/{id}/changes?since=N` pages on version boundaries and says when a full snapshot is required (no version, older than `sync.change_log.retention`, or ahead of central). Ten `sync.*` configuration items (DR-1, DR-4, DR-5 and the rest), six audit types, eighteen messages in three languages. **Decisions** (the pull request lists them in full): paths `/v1/sync/...` where doc 32 writes `/sync/v1/...` (the slice rule); snake_case on the wire as doc 32 names the fields; the device credential is a provider client per device, not a kernel-signed token (the service account gains `manage-clients`; the provider caps the requested 24-hour token at the realm's session maximum, ten hours in the dev realm); ingestion runs in the request, where the web role is, until doc 35 routes an ingest role; the ledger `sync_event` instead of `event_inbox('sync')`, which has no sequence; FLAGGED is the consumers' to raise, the acknowledgement says APPLIED, DUPLICATE or QUARANTINED; `/changes` is an operation doc 32 does not name; a suspended device keeps its credential so that it can be told, a retired one loses it. **Not here** (part 2): the snapshot builder and contributors, the full snapshot files and their job, the rate limiter, the till simulator, the bundle handler into the document base and the kernel's consumer of the till's `audit.*`, the sequence-reset recovery, the change-log purge, and the conformance cases Two instances, Snapshot atomicity, apply_from, Counter transfer, Burst, Business-rule breach and Power loss on the till. **Proof**: the conformance cases of doc 32 section 11 this part covers, one class per group against the running application with device tokens: `BatchUploadConformanceIntegrationTest` (Lost acknowledgement, Gap, replay, overlap, a duplicate id quarantined, repeated gaps, the batch shape, gzip, not enrolled), `QuarantineConformanceIntegrationTest` (Malformed event, bundle hash, personal data), `CrashAndConcurrencyConformanceIntegrationTest` (Crash mid-batch at central, Concurrent batches, a dead instance's claim), `HeartbeatConformanceIntegrationTest` (Central point-in-time restore, the health fields, drift, urgent change, `SyncStatus`); and `DeviceAuthConformanceIntegrationTest`, `EnrolmentIntegrationTest`, `ChangeLogIntegrationTest`, `KeycloakDeviceCredentialsIntegrationTest` (the provider of the local stack), `JwtClaimsMapperTest`, `ScopeFilterPrincipalTest`, `BundleHashTest`, `SyncUnitTest`.
