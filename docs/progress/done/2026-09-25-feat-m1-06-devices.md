- **M1-06 devices (25 Sep, branch `feat/m1-06-devices`).** The device aggregate of 21A section 3.1 and the five device commands of section 6, done when scenario 6.6 of doc 21 (replacing a faulty till) runs. **Schema** (`m1party/V0009`): `party.device` gets `location_id` (NOT NULL; no handler wrote a device before), the template's location line on `own_read`, `own_write` and `own_update`, `ext_view`, and a check that only an ACTIVE or SUSPENDED device holds a position. **Commands** (`internal/device`, `POST /v1/party/devices` and `/{deviceId}/assign|suspend|reinstate|retire`, `GET` list and one): **EnrolDevice** (`sys.device.enrol`, MFA: the caller's own location, a known kind, a staging reference, a well-formed release number, a serial unique across the federation, the unique key translated for a serial another entity holds; ENROLLED; `DEVICE_ENROLLED`, `device.enrolled.v1`); **AssignDeviceToPosition** (`sys.device.enrol`, MFA: ENROLLED or ACTIVE; a till terminal; the position visible, ACTIVE and at the device's location; free, where a position held by a SUSPENDED device is free for its replacement and held by an ACTIVE one is occupied; the release at or above `m1.device.version_floor`; a reason; every device that gives a lane up drained (`kernel.api.SyncStatus`) or its loss recorded; then the old holder lets go, the device is ACTIVE at the position, and the counters of the position, and of the shop when it is the primary till, move through `NumberingService.holderChange`, which audits and publishes `series.holder_changed.v1`; `DEVICE_ASSIGNED` and `device.assigned.v1` for a free position, `DEVICE_POSITION_CHANGED` and `device.position_changed.v1` for a replacement or a move, plus a `DEVICE_OUTBOX_LOSS_RECORDED` ALERT when the loss was recorded); **SuspendDevice** (`sys.device.suspend`, MFA: ACTIVE; a reason LOST, STOLEN or FAULTY; SUSPENDED, keeping its position; `DEVICE_SUSPENDED`, `device.suspended.v1` and `device.revoked.v1`); **ReinstateDevice** (SUSPENDED; a reason; ACTIVE, back in its own lane or without one if it was replaced; `device.reinstated.v1`, which lifts the revoke); **RetireDevice** (not RETIRED; unassigned; a reason; `device.retired.v1` and `device.revoked.v1`). `DeviceQueries` lists and gets devices with the position, its number and the primary-till flag. **The revoke instruction** the sync gateway relays to the till (doc 32 section 9) is `device.revoked.v1`, with the serial and the cause, so K-08 acts without reading M1's tables. **Decisions, said out loud for the architect:** (1) `kernel.api.SyncStatus` is a new kernel contract (21A names "kernel SyncStatus.drained"); until K-08 its only implementation, `NoGatewaySyncStatus`, answers "not drained" for every device, the safe side, so every replacement needs the loss recorded until then. (2) A suspended device keeps its position (21A: "keeps position for return"), which is what makes "position free" and "the previous device" of the 21A pseudocode both true: the previous holder is read from the database, not taken from the request. (3) MFA on every enrol and assign, not only on re-assign as doc 21 section 4.5 has it, because the catalogue marks `sys.device.enrol` `mfa: true` (21A section 3, the seed excerpt) and the interceptor checks the permission. (4) Enrolment is by the owning entity in its own scope; the Federation staging table of doc 31 is in no DDL, so the staging reference is carried in the command and kept in the audit record. (5) A WORKSTATION or DRIVER_MOBILE does not take a till position, and a device is assigned only at its own location. (6) The two 21A permission codes are added beside the existing `sys.device.manage` (now unused; for the catalogue freeze, `CR-21A-1`). **Not here:** the device credential and its authentication (K-02), the enrol endpoint and the gateway's revoke, heartbeat and sequence reset (K-08), the asset register screen (M1-12), and the till position handlers themselves (M1-05, #102). **To reconcile when #102 merges:** its test fixture `insertDevice` must give `location_id`; `M1SeedLoaderTest` counts 22 permissions; the device package may then use `TillPosition` and `SeriesHooks` instead of `DevicePlaces`, and a later migration can add the foreign key `(current_till_position_id, location_id)` on the key #102 creates. **Proof** (`DevicesPostgresIntegrationTest`, as the application user, and `AppVersionTest`): scenario 6.6 end to end (suspend and revoke; the drained check refuses and commits nothing; the loss recorded, the three series move to the replacement with their numbers untouched and the kernel's `series.holder_changed.v1` from the old device to the new; the old device retired and revoked); a drained device needs no loss; a first assignment moves the position's and the primary till's counters, another position only its own; a move to another position needs the device drained; reinstatement into its own lane and without one after a replacement; every guard of the five commands as a failing case with its message id and nothing audited or published; a shop-scoped caller lists, reads and manages only its shop's devices, the Federation view reads all and writes nothing, and the policies alone refuse an insert or update at a sibling shop; over HTTP, enrol, a malformed release (400), assign, a second assign (422 with the message id), a suspend reason outside the enumeration (400), suspend, list.
