tested via `NegativeLotReviewJobPostgresIntegrationTest` covering no escalation before threshold, exact day-7 boundary escalation, Colombo midnight boundary crossings, valid acknowledgement exclusion, non-negative stock exclusion, negative-to-negative movement preservation (Wave 2 M5-07), recovery and recurrence, repeated execution deduplication, multi-tenant fleet isolation, PostgreSQL RLS scoping, entity-specific threshold overrides, audit/event payload verification, ledger immutability, and stale candidate revalidation;
