added Flyway migration `V0010__payment_orchestration_and_lankaqr.sql` introducing `integration.payment_intent` (tracks payment lifecycle across INITIATED, PENDING, PAID, FAILED, COD_PENDING, COD_COLLECTED, CANCELLED with amounts, provider references, idempotency keys, and structured metadata) and `integration.payment_event_log` (append-only ledger of lifecycle transitions, provider callback payloads, and settlement details), both protected with forced 4-class RLS (`own_read`, `own_write`, `own_update`, `fed_view`, `ext_view`) and explicit `app_rw` role grants;
