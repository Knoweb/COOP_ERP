- **M2-02 SKU aggregate and search (25 Sep, branch `feat/m2-02-sku`, pull request #115, by Pavani).** The catalogue item of 22A sections 3 and 6 in `m2catalogue/internal/sku`, the first operations of the M2 slice under `/v1/catalogue/skus` (`createSku`, `listSkus` with `q` for search, `getSku`, `updateSku`, `activateSku` with `target` LOCAL or SHARED, `deactivateSku`, `reactivateSku`), each mutation with its `Idempotency-Key` and `x-permission`. **The aggregate** `Sku`: a DRAFT with a code from `SkuCodeGenerator` (`SKU-` and eight letters of a 32-letter alphabet), the owner the caller's entity; DRAFT to LOCAL or SHARED, LOCAL or SHARED to INACTIVE with the prior status kept, and back; a SHARED SKU keeps its three names (B-I1); sold by weight needs KG, expiry tracking needs batch tracking; attributes at most 4 KB. **The guards** (`SkuRules`, `SkuGuards`): an entity-wide OWN scope, the English name, a unit and a tax category that exist, a reason for deactivation and reactivation, the owner for every change; the unit and the tracking flags cannot change once lots exist, behind an `InventoryLotQuery` seam that answers "no lots" until M5 supplies it (`NoInventoryLotQuery`). Audits `SKU_CREATED`, `SKU_ACTIVATED`, `SKU_UPDATED`, `SKU_DEACTIVATED`, `SKU_REACTIVATED` with before and after, and the matching `sku.*.v1` events. **The reads** (`CatalogueQueries`): list and search under row-level security as it stands (own rows, SHARED rows for everyone, all rows for the Federation view), ordered by the display language's collation, attributes only for the owner. `cat.sku.view` joined the three role templates. **Deviations**: no duplicate scoring and no `duplicate.suspected.v1` yet (22A section 6 asks for them on create and update; they come with the `duplicate_suspect` table); the list pages by offset where 22A section 4 asks for a cursor. **Proof**: `SkuHandlersPostgresIntegrationTest`, `CatalogueQueriesPostgresIntegrationTest`, `CatalogueHttpPostgresIntegrationTest` (the lifecycle through the generated contract), `SkuTest`, `M1SeedLoaderTest`.
