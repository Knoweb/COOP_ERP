- **M6: a till's session is applied before its receipts (29 Sep, branch `fix/m6-session-before-receipts`).** Found by the CR-30-1 desktop till trial: `receipt.issued.v1` (`m6.receipts`) and `till_session.opened.v1` / `.closed.v1` (`m6.sessions`) had two consumer queues, and the broker orders a device's events within a queue, never across two, so a receipt uploaded in the batch of its session was often applied first and flagged `SESSION_UNKNOWN` (one sale in three). `PosIngestConsumer` now applies all three types in one consumer, `m6.till`, whose queue keeps the device's `source_seq` order (26A section 10, doc 32); no kernel change, no migration. Decided on the architect's delegation over (b) deferring the receipt through bounded retries (the kernel's three in-place attempts would stall the queue and still flag a slow session) and (c) clearing the flag when the session arrives (flags and audit are insert-only). Proof: `TillSaleEndToEndIntegrationTest.aSessionUploadedWithItsReceiptsIsAppliedBeforeThemWhateverQueueDrainsFirst` (one batch, the queues read from the `@EventConsumer` annotations, receipt queues drained first; failed with three `SESSION_UNKNOWN` before, passes after), the m6pos and kernel sync suites, `TillSimulatorConformanceIntegrationTest`, `:app:test`, repo checks.
