- **Claims and returns, and transfer requests (29 Sep, branch `feat/m4-claims-transfer-requests`).** **M4-06 claims**: the buyer raises a claim (CLM, buyer ENTITY series, DISPUTES link) on a confirmed GRN within `trading.claim_window_days` for damaged, expired, wrong or poor goods (`POST /v1/trading/claims`, `del.claim.raise`), adds photographs to it (`/photos`, the kernel's presigned upload); the seller (`del.claim.decide`) approves in whole or in part once every photograph is verified, which issues the credit note on its invoice of the GRN in the same act (the SettleDiscrepancy path; `doc_credit_note.claim_document_id`), with or without the goods back, or rejects with a reason; the buyer sends approved goods back (`/return`) and M5's new `ClaimReturnConsumer` posts RETURN_TO_SELLER from the buyer's lot.
