- **K-10 notifications delivery, part 1 (25 Sep, branch `feat/k-10-notifications`, on top of CR-17A-3).** Doc 19 section 7 as 19A section 10 restates it: the kernel delivers, M9 owns what is said and through which provider. **What M9 implements** (`kernel.api`): `NotificationChannel` (one bean per channel, SMS, EMAIL, IN_APP: send, return the provider reference or throw), `NotificationRuleQueries` (the active rules for an event type and the templates in three languages) and `NotificationAudience` (the role kinds from M1, the CUSTOMER kind from M7 with the STATEMENTS consent); without those beans the dispatcher matches nothing and only direct sends work. **What the kernel does**: `Notifications.send` for the few direct cases (a reset code, a lockout notice), and `NotificationDispatcher`, an event consumer on every type (`@EventConsumer(types = "*")`, which the registry, the topic binding `#` and the replayer now understand; a wildcard consumer is handed the envelope with the payload inside) that matches M9's rules by type and predicate, resolves the EXPLICIT audience from the payload and the rest through the audience beans, and hands each recipient to `NotificationService`: a `notification_log` row QUEUED (`kernel/V0054`; the unique key rule, event, recipient is the idempotency, so a replayed event sends once), the suppressions from the register (the entity's kill switch `notification.<channel>.enabled`, the quiet hours `notification.sms.quiet_hours`, the same template to the same person inside an hour), rendering in the recipient's language with English fallback (`NotificationRenderer`: an M9 template through ICU MessageFormat, or a catalogue message id), the channel, then SENT with the provider reference and the rendered length, or a failed attempt with backoff (one, five, fifteen minutes) that the sweep `notification-retry` (every two minutes, a K-12 job) retries, three attempts inside 24 hours, then FAILED with a `NOTIFICATION_FAILED` ALERT. **Privacy** (ADR-27): the log holds a hash of channel and recipient and the body's length, never the number or the text; the recipient of a retry is held in memory on the instance that queued it, and a row that instance no longer holds is FAILED with a clear error rather than stored. **Not here**: the secondary provider after the third attempt (M9's `provider_config`), the customer consent check (M7), the SMS length rule at authoring (M9). **Proof** (`NotificationsPostgresIntegrationTest` with a test channel and a test rule store): a direct send is rendered in the recipient's language and logged without the number; a language without the text falls back to English; the same thing to the same person sends once, and a second inside the hour is SUPPRESSED; the kill switch and the quiet hours suppress with their reason; a failing provider is retried by the sweep on its back-off and given up with the ALERT after three; the dispatcher matches an event against a rule and its explicit audience, sends once on replay, and skips a predicate that does not hold; an unknown channel or no recipient is refused.
