- **Version floor ALERT audit event past grace (12 Oct, branch `fix/FIX-14-version-floor-alert`).** When a till uploads below the required application version floor after its grace period has expired (`afterGrace()`), `BatchIngestor` records a `SYNC_APP_VERSION_BELOW_FLOOR` audit event (severity `ALERT`) via `IngestTransactions`, in addition to logging (doc 31 section 6). Registered `SYNC_APP_VERSION_BELOW_FLOOR` in `seed/kernel/audit-event-types.yaml` and registered `sync.app_version_below_floor` in `i18n/kernel/{en,si,ta}.json`. In `AppVersionFloorConformanceIntegrationTest`, proved that uploads past grace produce a committed `ALERT` audit event with subject `device` and floor/version details, while uploads within grace produce none.
