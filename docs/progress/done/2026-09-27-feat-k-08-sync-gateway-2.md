- **K-08 sync gateway, second part (27 Sep, branch `feat/k-08-sync-gateway-2`).** What 19A section 8 and doc 32 still asked for after #114 and #126. **Snapshot builder** (`kernel.internal.sync.SnapshotBuilder`, served by `GET /v1/sync/locations/{id}/snapshot`, which answered 501 until now): the delta since the till's version (the change log after it, the last operation per row, the rows fetched from the table's contributor, a row the contributor no longer returns sent as a tombstone), or the full snapshot when the till holds no version, is ahead of central, or is older than the retention (DR-4); both read in one read-only REPEATABLE READ transaction, so the answer is exactly one version (doc 32 S5). **Contributor interface** `kernel.api.SnapshotContributor` (tables, rows by id, all rows; decimals as text), one contributor per table, checked at start. **Signed manifest** (`SnapshotManifest`): per table the counts and a SHA-256 over lines defined in the slice, signed with the till signing key (`TillSigner`, doc 32 section 9 "snapshot manifest hashes"); the manifest travels as the signed text. **Contributors**: M1 `ShopSnapshotContributor` (`location`, `till_position`) and `OperatorSnapshotContributor` (`operator`: TILL/BOTH users with a PIN and an ACTIVE role at the shop or entity-wide, offline permissions, `rv`); M2 `CatalogueSnapshotContributor` (`sku` with conversions, barcodes and tags inside the row; `tax_category` with rates). M3's price lists are M3's to contribute when M3 is built; M5 and M7 likewise. **Rate limiter** (`SyncRateLimiter`, doc 32 section 9; 19A section 8): per device a token bucket of batches per minute and one of bytes per hour, per instance a limit of batches in ingestion; 429 `sync.rate_limited` with `params.retry_after`; a batch refused for capacity costs the device nothing; limits `sync.rate.batches_per_minute` (20), `sync.rate.bytes_per_hour` (16 MB), `sync.ingest.max_concurrent` (32) in the register. **Till simulator** (`testsupport.TillSimulator`): an outbox with a dense sequence, uploads through the generated request and answer types (`SyncBatch`, `TillEvent`, `SyncAck`, `SnapshotDelta`, `Heartbeat`), waits as told on 429, a snapshot applied whole (verify signature and hashes, stage, swap with the version), a power cut injected mid-apply, rows held for apply_from and activated at day open; its hash and signature checks are written from the slice, not borrowed from the kernel. **Conformance cases of doc 32 section 11 now passing**: Snapshot atomicity, apply_from, Burst (twelve tills, sixty events each, two ingestion slots: every till drained, each event once, told to wait twelve times in the recorded run) in `TillSimulatorConformanceIntegrationTest`, and Two instances in `TwoInstancesConformanceIntegrationTest` (a second application on the same database; batches alternate; a resent batch at the other instance is DUPLICATE). With part 1 (Lost acknowledgement, Crash mid-batch, Gap, Malformed event, Concurrent batches, Central point-in-time restore), ten of the thirteen cases are covered: Counter transfer and Business-rule breach need the bundle handler (deferred), and Power loss on the till during issuance is the till's own local transaction (spike 1, till track). **No migration**: the builder reads `change_log` and `location_snapshot_version` (V0080), the limiter lives in memory. **Also** (own commit): the notification rule cache (`NotificationDispatcher`) is a `CacheFanoutListener` for `notification_rule.changed.v1`, the timer kept as the backstop (follow-up of #132). Tests: `SyncRateLimiterTest`, `NotificationRuleCacheFanoutTest`, the two conformance classes, `PartySnapshotContributorsIntegrationTest` (operator round trip) and `CatalogueSnapshotContributorIntegrationTest`.
