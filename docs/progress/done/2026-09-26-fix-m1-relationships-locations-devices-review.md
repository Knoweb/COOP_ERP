- **Relationships, locations and devices, the review fixes (26 Sep, branch `fix/m1-relationships-locations-devices-review`).** The M1-04, M1-05 and M1-06 findings of the review of 26 September, in `m1party/internal/relationship`, `location` and `device`. **Relationships**: a suspension is of the relationship, not of one period: `SuspendRelationshipHandler` suspends the row named and every other ACTIVE row of the pair in force today or later (each audited `RELATIONSHIP_SUSPENDED` and published), so trading does not come back the day the next row starts; `AmendRelationshipTermsHandler` amends the latest row of the pair only (`m1.relationship.not_latest`) and asks `TradePriceListCheck` when the price list changes (`m1.relationship.price_list_refused`, the same check as at activation); every handler loads its row with `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` (`RelationshipRepository.findByIdForUpdate`), so a Suspend and an Amend of one row run one after the other; the stub `AcceptingPriceListCheck` accepts the seeded lists only (`coop-erp.party.stub-price-lists`, the ids of the relationship seed; `m1.relationship.price_list_unknown` otherwise) and is `@ConditionalOnMissingBean`. **Locations**: `SetPrimaryTill` moves the shop's counters only to an ACTIVE device (a suspended device at the new position gets none) and only away from a drained one (`SyncStatus.drained`, else `m1.device.outbox_not_drained` unless `outboxLossRecorded`, new on the request and the command, with the `DEVICE_OUTBOX_LOSS_RECORDED` record against the old till); `SetPrimaryTill` and `RetireTillPosition` lock the location row and then the position row (`findByIdForUpdate`, always in that order); `LocationFacts.hasOperator` counts ACTIVE till users only; `MarkDormant` clears `connectivity_spec_met`, so `Reactivate`'s gate (doc 21 section 4.3) is a real check. **Devices**: `AssignDeviceToPosition` compares the floor with the release the till last reported (`SyncStatus.state`, the enrolment value only for a device that never reported); a device that moves leaves the series of its old lane, and the shop's when that lane was the primary till, with no holder (`NumberingService.releaseHolder`, new in the kernel, audited `SERIES_HOLDER_CHANGED` to null and published `series.holder_changed.v1`); the outbox-loss record names, per undrained device, the series of the position it gives up (`losses`, plus the flat `deviceIds` and `seriesIds`); the device, the position (`DevicePlaces.positionForUpdate`) and the position's holder are locked, and a `23505` on the one-device-per-position index is answered `m1.device.position_occupied`. **Not changed, raised instead**: whether an amendment may be dated before today, and how a row that has not started is corrected on its first day, are `CR-21A-2`; positions at a PLANNED or DORMANT shop (21A says only "location SHOP"): decided 27 Sep 2026, a position may be registered at a shop in any status (`CR-21A-5` item 2, branch `feat/decisions-m1-rules`), on the architect's delegation, because doc 21 flow 6.1 registers positions at PLANNED shops and a dormant shop may need a lane before it reopens. `make reset` is not needed (no migration). **Proof**: `RelationshipHandlersTest` (a replaced row, a refused new list, every later row suspended), `AcceptingPriceListCheckTest`, `RelationshipScenariosIntegrationTest` (two activations on two threads let exactly one through, a suspension waits for a lock another transaction holds, suspend with a later row, amend on a replaced row, a shop-scoped caller reads and changes nothing), `LocationsPostgresIntegrationTest` (a PENDING operator does not count, reactivation refused until connectivity is confirmed again, a suspended device gets no counters, an undrained old till needs the loss recorded, a closed series answers `series.closed`, the change waits for the lock, the Idempotency-Key replay of RegisterTillPosition), `DevicesPostgresIntegrationTest` (the old lane released, the loss named against the lane left, the reported release against the floor, an assignment waits for the lock), `M1SeedLoaderTest`.
