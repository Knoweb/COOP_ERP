- **M4-03 acceptance: accept and reject (27 Sep, branch `feat/m4-03-acceptance`, stacked on M4-02).** 24A section 10's allocation ticket, demo-minimal. **AcceptOrder** (`ord.order.accept`, 24A sections 6 and 6.2): the seller's entity-wide OWN scope, an order placed with it, SUBMITTED and not yet decided (an advisory lock per order serialises two decisions), the relationship still ACTIVE, a committed ETA not in the past, overrides that name a line of the order with a reason and allocate no more than its open request nor more than is available, a trade price per line. Each line is allocated the lesser of its open request and the seller's availability (`InventoryAvailability`, FCFS for the one order; the snapshot and the overrides are kept on `allocation_run`); the tier price is resolved at the ordered quantity (`TradePricing`, DR-2); `lock_at` is the ETA's start of day in the business zone less the relationship's `order_lock_hours_before_eta`. Rows: `allocation_run`, `order_allocation` ACCEPTED, `order_allocation_line` per line, all the seller's own (CR-24A-1 item 2). ORDER_ACCEPTED; `order.accepted.v1` and `order.allocated.v1`. **RejectOrder** (`ord.order.accept`): the same scope and state guards and a reason; `order_allocation` REJECTED; ORDER_REJECTED, `order.rejected.v1`. The order's status (ACCEPTED, LOCKED once `lock_at` passes, REJECTED) is derived by `OrderStatus`; a rejected order is no longer cancelled by the buyer. **Slice**: `POST /v1/trading/orders/{orderId}/accept`, `.../reject`. **Deferred for the demo, M4-03**: the LockClockJob and ORDER_LOCKED audit (the LOCKED state is derived from `lock_at` on read), the backorder review job, OverrideLockWindow, RecordOrderAdjustment, allocation across competing open orders and the PRO_RATA and QUOTA rules (M5's reservations). **Proof**: `AcceptanceHandlersPostgresIntegrationTest` (every guard with its failing case, the cap by availability through the register, an override and its reason, the lock time, decided once, audit and events), `OrderHttpPostgresIntegrationTest` (accept and reject over HTTP), the m4trading integration tests, `:app:test`, `check-i18n`, `check-permissions`.
