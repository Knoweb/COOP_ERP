- **An invoice line's VAT comes from the item's tax category and the rate in force (28 Sep, branch `fix/invoice-vat-by-tax-category`).** Found on the live demo: FED-INV-0000001 charged 18 % VAT on "Samba rice 5 kg", whose tax category in `demo/catalogue.psv` is EXEMPT, because M4-08 (#164) took one rate for every line from the demo item `m4.demo.vat_rate_percent` while M2 had no rate query. **M2** now publishes `CatalogueQueries.taxRateInForce(skuId, onDate, scope)` (22A sections 3 and 7): the SKU's tax category and the `catalogue.tax_rate` row whose effective range covers the date, as `TaxRateView`; EXEMPT and ZERO rated answer 0 from their seeded 0 % rows; empty when the SKU is not visible or no rate covers the date. It needs no new policy: `tax_category` and `tax_rate` are `authenticated_read`, and the SKU is read under catalogue RLS as `getSku` already was. **M4** `IssueInvoiceHandler` asks it per line at the invoice's tax point (`m4.invoice.tax_rate_missing` when empty, as before) and keeps the rate on the line (`tax_rate_percent`, shown per line as today); the posting mapper takes the invoice totals, so it needed no change. **Removed**: `m4trading.api.TaxRates`, `internal.integration.DemoTaxRates` and the configuration item `m4.demo.vat_rate_percent` (seed and README rows); a database that loaded it keeps an unused register row, which nothing reads. **Proof**: `InvoiceHandlersPostgresIntegrationTest` (rice at a standard 18 % and dhal EXEMPT on one invoice: VAT 172.80 on the rice only, gross 1452.80, VAT_OUTPUT posting 172.80; a 25 % rate effective the day after the tax point does not apply; a category with no rate on the tax point is refused), `CatalogueQueriesPostgresIntegrationTest` (the rate covering the day, the next rate from its first day, empty before any rate and for a SKU the scope cannot see, an exempt category answers 0), the integration tests of `m2catalogue`, `m4trading`, `demo`, `m8reporting`, `:app:test`.
