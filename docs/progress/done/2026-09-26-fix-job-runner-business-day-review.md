- **Job runner and business day, the review fixes (26 Sep, branch `fix/job-runner-business-day-review`).** The wave-1 and deep-review findings on K-12 and K-13 (#94), plus the partition functions of V0053. **The lock outlives a timed-out run** (`JobRunner`): a cancelled body may still be inside a database or socket call, so the ShedLock row is left to expire at `lockTimeout` instead of being released, and the next firing skips (doc 19 section 9, "never overlapped"). **A lock is held a little after the run ends**: the register's new `jobs.lock.at_least_for` (FEDERATION, five seconds by default), so a second instance whose clock fires the same schedule a moment later skips instead of running it again. **A critical job's lock must cover two runs** (`JobDefinition`, checked at registration: `lockTimeout > 2 * maxRuntime`); `gap-check` moves to PT35M. **The jobs have a thread pool of their own** (`JobScheduler`, one thread per job, not a bean), so a twenty-minute cut-off no longer stalls the outbox relay's `@Scheduled` poll or any other firing. **ShedLock's times are naive UTC** (`kernel/V0057`): its PostgreSQL statements write and compare `timezone('utc', CURRENT_TIMESTAMP)`, and against V0051's `timestamptz` columns a session in Asia/Colombo shifted every lock time by five and a half hours (found by the new tests; a deviation from the 19A DDL, below). **One close per business day** (`LocationBusinessDates.close`): the row is created if missing (`on conflict do nothing`), locked `for update`, and moved only where the business date is still the one read, so the trigger and the cut-off, or a redelivered event, make one change, one `DAY_CLOSED` and one `location.day_closed.v1`; the once-a-day guard is keyed on the business date (already past today), so a cut-off after midnight no longer swallows that evening's real close. **Every location has a row**: `LocationRegisteredListener` creates it on `location.registered.v1` (payload only, no import of M1), and the cut-off first gives a row to every location of `party.location` without one (read as the federation viewer, as `LocationOwners` does), so a location that never had a trigger close is cut off from its next night. **Issuance holds the date** (`BusinessDate.currentHeld`, `for share`): a day close waits for the issuing transaction, so a document committing while the day closes carries the day it was issued on; a document without a location takes the date of the location the issuer acts at. **The partition functions recreate a policy only when it is missing or ungated** (`kernel/V0056`, `ensure_audit_partitions` and `ensure_event_outbox_partitions`; `ALTER TABLE ... ROW LEVEL SECURITY` only when not yet set), so partition-maintenance no longer takes an ACCESS EXCLUSIVE lock on the live partitions every night. **Deviations**: one cut-off cron for the fleet, an entity-wide issuer's location-less document on the calendar date, and the ShedLock column type, below. `make reset` is not needed (two plain new migrations). **Proof**: `JobDefinitionTest`, `JobSchedulerTest`, `JobRunnerPostgresIntegrationTest` (lock held after a run, kept after a timeout, `locked_at` reads as now), `DayClosePostgresIntegrationTest` (row on registration, the cut-off's backfill from `party.location`, two concurrent closes make one change, a cut-off after midnight then the evening's close, a document issued while the day closes), `PartitionPoliciesPostgresIntegrationTest` (policy oids unchanged on a second run, an ungated policy put right), and the audit, document, config, idempotency, event, RLS-matrix, own-policy, schema, M1 location and hello suites.
