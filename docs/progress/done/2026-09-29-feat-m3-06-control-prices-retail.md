- **M3-06 and M3-07, control prices, the MRP policy and shelf prices (29 Sep, branch `feat/m3-06-control-prices-retail`).** **Schema** (`m3pricing/V0005__control_price_and_mrp_policy.sql`): `pricing.control_price` (exclusion per SKU and date range, B-I6; `everyone_reads`, Federation-owned inserts, UPDATE of `effective_to` only) and `pricing.mrp_policy` (one row per SKU and owner; `everyone_reads`); `advisory_read` on `price_list` and `price_list_line` for published ADVISORY versions. **Handlers** (23A section 7): `EnterControlPrice` (the Federation, second factor, SKU active, base unit, ceiling above zero, gazette reference, dates in order, no overlap but the ceiling in force on the first day, which is closed the day before) with `CONTROL_PRICE_ENTERED` and `control_price.entered.v1`; `RescindControlPrice` (last day within the range and not before yesterday, reason and gazette kept in the audit) with `CONTROL_PRICE_RESCINDED` and `control_price.rescinded.v1`; `SetMrpPolicy` (policy of the three, gaps only for PICKER, the owner's row replaced) with `MRP_POLICY_SET` and `mrp_policy.changed.v1`; `CreatePriceList` now takes RETAIL (an MPCS with no RETAIL list yet) and ADVISORY (the Federation); the `AuthoringValidator` refuses a RETAIL or ADVISORY line above the control price in force (today while drafting, `apply_from` at publication) and a RETAIL line above the lowest printed MRP in stock at the society's locations, and names the binding ceiling on every line (`SetLinesResult.Outcome` gains `ceilingKind`, `ceilingValue`, `ceilingRef`); `DraftNewVersion` carries the lines forward. **Queries**: `controlPrices` (the history), `controlPricesInForce`, `controlPriceFor`, `effectiveMrpPolicy` (the society's row, else the Federation's, else `pricing.default_mrp_policy`, PICKER gaps from `pricing.picker_gap_amount` and `pricing.picker_gap_percent`), `listMrpPolicies`, `advisoryLines`, and `resolveRetailPrice(location, sku, uom, qty, date)`, which loads the society's RETAIL line, the shop's batches with their MRP, the effective policy and the control price and calls the engine's `PriceResolver` (M3-09 contributes the same inputs to the till's snapshot). M5's lots reach M3 through `m3pricing.api.InStockBatchQuery`, which M5 answers (`InStockBatchesForPricing`). **Slice**: eight operations (control prices, rescission, MRP policies, the effective policy, the retail price, the advisory lines); web client regenerated. **Seeds**: `prc.controlprice.enter` (FEDERATION, MFA), `prc.mrp_policy.set`; three config items; three audit types; message ids in en, si and ta; demo roles (fed-pricing enters control prices, m101-manager authors and publishes the shelf list). **Web** (23A section 8): the shelf price list (RETAIL and ADVISORY, advisory column, binding ceiling per line, publish disabled while a line is refused, the price at a shop), the gazette (history, enter, rescind), the MRP policy; a row of links between the pricing screens. **Demo**: `DemoRetailPricing`, on the set-up day: control prices for Nadu rice 5 kg (Rs 1,100), white sugar 1 kg (Rs 275) and loose samba rice (Rs 250 a kg), PICKER for milk powder 400 g at Kuliyapitiya MPCS, and the society's published shelf list of 40 items (MRP, or the distributor's price plus a tenth for loose goods, never above the control price). **Proof**: `RetailPricingPostgresIntegrationTest` (flows 6.1, 6.4, 6.6: every guard with nothing committed, audit and events, the history, the effective policy, the ceilings of a shelf list, advisory lines, the shop price as list, MRP, control price and picker), `RetailPricingHttpPostgresIntegrationTest`, the RLS matrix and schema rules, `DemoDataLoaderIntegrationTest` (counts, idempotence, the town shop's price of Nadu rice), the module's vitest, type check, lint, `tools/check-i18n.mjs`; `web/e2e/pricing.spec.ts` (draft, refused above the control price, publish, price at the town shop) was type-checked and linted, not run against the stack.
