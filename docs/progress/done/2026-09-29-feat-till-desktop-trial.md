- **CR-30-1 desktop trial: the till as one Kotlin Multiplatform codebase, selling, printing and closing the day on a Windows PC (29 Sep, branch `feat/till-desktop-trial`; till track, first step).** `till/` is restructured into KMP modules per the research report 4.1: `core` (commonMain: sale, receipt numbering from the till position's RCT series, session and blind close, Z-report, snapshot verification and staging with held rows, the outbox and its batches, the kernel's content-hash rule, receipt and Z-report layouts in en/si/ta; `TillService` over ports), `sync` (Ktor sync client; JDK Ed25519, BouncyCastle Argon2id), `db` (SQLDelight; sqlite-jdbc-crypt, SQLCipher 4 format, raw key), `peripherals` (ESC/POS `GS v 0` in bands, cut, drawer kick; keyboard-wedge scan reader), `peripherals-jvm` (TCP 9100; PNG + `.bin` preview folder), `render` (Skia paragraph engine with bundled Noto Sans/Sinhala/Tamil, 1-bit), `ui` (shared Compose Multiplatform screens and controller), `app` (Android, now on the shared `HelloScreen`; Room removed), `desktop` (Compose Desktop, DPAPI or mode-600 key, `createDistributable`/`packageMsi`/`packageDeb`, `devEnrolmentCode` helper). Versions pinned in `till/gradle/libs.versions.toml` (Kotlin 2.3.21, CMP 1.11.1, AGP 8.13.2, Gradle 8.14.4). **Proof**: 48 JVM tests: commonTest for numbering (dense device sequence, re-enrolment never reuses a number, a power cut uses no number), session and Z-report, snapshot verification (valid, tampered row, forged signature, another shop, held rows), offline queue (sell offline, drain in order in batches, same batch id on a lost answer, partial acknowledgement), a content-hash vector computed by the backend's `BundleHash`; real Ed25519 and Argon2id; the encrypted database unreadable without its key; golden images of a Sinhala and a Tamil receipt and a Sinhala Z-report; the desktop screens driven headless with the keyboard through a sale and a close. Against the local stack (opt-in `DesktopStackTest`): `DESKTOP-TRIAL-S01` enrolled at Kuliyapitiya town shop on position 2, verified the signed snapshot (40 items), sold `M101-S01-T2-RCT-1..3`, printed receipt and Z-report PNGs, uploaded; central applied the receipts with no hash flag. CI's *Till tests and APK* job runs the JVM tests and builds the APK and the Linux distributable. Measurements and what the Android side needs next are in `till/README.md`.
