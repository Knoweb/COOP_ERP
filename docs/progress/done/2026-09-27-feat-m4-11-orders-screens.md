- **M4-11 (part), the trading screens: requisition book, order desk, delivery note, goods received note (27 Sep, branch `feat/m4-11-orders-screens`; demo lane).** doc 30 section 5.4 and 24A section 8, demo scope, in `web/src/modules/m4trading`. `/trading` is the trading desk: each register shown to the user whose job it is (the buyer's orders with `ord.order.draft`, the seller's order desk with `ord.order.accept`, the delivery notes sent with `del.note.*`, the deliveries on their way and the GRNs with `shop.grn.confirm`). `/trading/orders/new`, the requisition book: a seller from the buyer's ACTIVE relationships (M1), the delivery location (one of the buyer's own), items from M2's search, each line at the seller's tier price for its quantity (M3 `GET /v1/pricing/resolve/trade`), Submit drafts and issues the order in one step. `/trading/orders/:id`: submit and cancel (with a reason) for the buyer; for the seller at a SUBMITTED order the availability of each item (`GET /v1/trading/orders/availability`), a delivery date and Accept (the server allocates) or Reject with a reason; once accepted the allocated quantity, the tier price and what is on delivery notes, and the way to the delivery note. `/trading/delivery-notes/new?orderId=`: the warehouse the goods leave from, vehicle and driver, per line the open quantity and the batch, the first GOOD lot in FEFO order there (M5 balances); `/trading/delivery-notes/:id`: Issue (`del.note.issue`; the stock is reserved), Dispatch with vehicle and driver (`del.note.dispatch`), the drops and, for the receiver, Receive. `/trading/grns/new?deliveryNoteId=&dropId=`: the count at the drop's ship-to, received and damaged per item, the batch as printed prefilled from the seller's batch (M2 `getBatch`), a short line marked;
