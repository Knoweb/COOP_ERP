done when scenario 6.3 and the property tests pass. **Commands** (`m1party.api`, handlers in `internal/security/role`, all `gov.role.manage` with MFA): CreateRole (from a template or from scratch; a clone keeps `template_role_id` and `template_version_seen`; the Federation authors templates), AmendRole (set replace, version + 1, optionally taking the template's current version), RetireRole, AssignRole (entity-wide or at a location; a caller scoped to a location assigns there alone), RevokeRole (the row is deleted and audited), SetSodPair and RemoveSodPair (an entity adds or raises its own pairs; FEDERATION-scope permissions only in federation-owned roles; no role, and no person through two roles, holds both halves of a ROLE-mode pair; limits against `limits_schema`; never the last `gov.user.manage` holder; retire only when nobody holds the role; no ROLE raise while someone holds both. **Template drift** (doc 19 DR-4, assumes doc 10 E-04): `GET /v1/security/roles/{roleId}/diff` and the `templateUpdated` marker; nothing is pushed into clones. **Events** `role.changed.v1` (no userId: the kernel empties the whole permission cache), `role.assigned.v1` and `role.revoked.v1` (with `userId`: that user's entries), `sod_pair.changed.v1`; audit types ROLE_CHANGED, ROLE_ASSIGNED, ROLE_REVOKED, SOD_PAIR_CHANGED; 37 message ids in three languages; slice paths under `/v1/security/roles`, `/assignments`, `/sod-pairs`; the web client regenerated. **Migration** `m1security/V0010`: DELETE on `user_role`, `role_permission` and `sod_pair` with `own_delete` policies (named in `SchemaRulesIntegrationTest.DELETE_BY_DESIGN`: 21A makes revoke a delete, 17A says no DELETE anywhere; the guide's procedure is followed for these three tables only), the Federation's `federation_template_*` policies (the fifth class of the policy template) and `security.role_assignment_count` with a `definer_read` policy for app_seed on `user_role` and `app_user`. **Decisions**: RevokeRole is `POST /v1/security/assignments/revoke`, not DELETE (the idempotency hash covers path and body, not the query string); retiring does not raise the version (a retired template would otherwise mark every clone as drifted); the limits reader takes a small subset of JSON Schema (no library in the catalogue).
