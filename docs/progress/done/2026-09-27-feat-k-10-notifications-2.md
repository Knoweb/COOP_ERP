- **K-10 notifications delivery, part 2 (27 Sep, branch `feat/k-10-notifications-2`).** What 19A section 10 asks for and part 1 (#99) and the review fixes (#127, #130) left out. **Provider failover** (19A section 10: "secondary provider after the third"; test "provider failover"): `NotificationChannel` gains `role()`, PRIMARY by default or SECONDARY, at most one adapter per channel and role (two fail the start); the first three attempts go through the primary, and a channel with a secondary gets a fourth attempt through it (after the fifteen-minute backoff, still inside the 24 hours) before FAILED and the `NOTIFICATION_FAILED` ALERT. Which provider is which stays M9's `provider_config`; the kernel knows the role alone. **The active rules are cached** (19A section 10: "cached, invalidated by notification_rule.changed.v1"): `NotificationDispatcher` keeps M9's active rules per event type and entity and clears them when it sees `notification_rule.changed.v1`; since that event reaches one instance of the competing consumers only, every entry also expires after `notification.rules.cache_seconds` (new register item, FEDERATION, default 60, 0 reads every time). **Not here**: the EMAIL (and SMS) adapters themselves: 19A section 10 calls `NotificationChannel` "M9's adapter interface" and doc 19 section 7 gives the providers to M9, so a provider adapter in the kernel would be the coupling the interface exists to prevent; M9 builds them (an EMAIL adapter against Mailpit in compose is the natural first one). Decided 27 Sep 2026 on the architect's delegation (PR #145, `CR-19A-8` item 5): no provider adapter in the kernel, not even a development-only one; M9's first ticket builds the EMAIL adapter against Mailpit, as 19A section 10 and doc 19 section 7 say. Binding the consumer to the event types with an active rule only: 19A section 10 names `@EventConsumer(types = "*")`, so the consumer stays on every type with the early return, as #127 left it. No migration; `make reset` not needed. **Proof**: `NotificationsPostgresIntegrationTest` (the fourth attempt through the secondary is SENT with its reference; a failing secondary ends FAILED with one ALERT after four; a cached rule still matches until the change event and M9 is not asked again, then the retired rule matches nothing; a lifetime of 0 reads every time; the eleven earlier cases unchanged, the unserved channel now FAX since EMAIL has adapters in the test), the unit suite, `tools/check-i18n.mjs`, `tools/check-permissions.mjs`.
