The slice `openapi/m4trading.yaml` and the web module are registered with no operation and no route (M2-01's pattern); `m4trading/README.md` is the living guide with ten deviations. **Deferred for the demo, M4-01**: notification rules (M9 seeds them; K-10's rules are the mechanism), the PARTY masking views `v_invoice_party`/`v_invoice_line_party` (with M4-08, where the invoice exists), the claim, payment receipt, cheque, exposure and transfer request tables (with M4-06, M4-07, M4-09, M4-10), partitioning (with the base). **Proof**: `TradingSchemaIntegrationTest` (the fourteen tables, forced RLS, the policies of each, grants by column, no DELETE), `TradingRlsIntegrationTest` (an extension row read by owner and counterparty and by nobody else, written by the owner only, a shop session at its shop only, the seller's allocation read by the buyer and written by the seller only), `M4SeedLoaderTest` (eleven posting rows, idempotent), `RlsMatrixIntegrationTest` (the three template tables cell by cell), `OwnPoliciesTestTheClassIntegrationTest`, `SchemaRulesIntegrationTest`, `M1SeedLoaderTest` (66 permissions, 6 templates), the unit suite with ArchitectureTests, the web suite, `check-i18n`, `check-permissions`, `check-slices`, `check-schema-ownership`.
