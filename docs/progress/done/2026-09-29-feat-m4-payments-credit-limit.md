- **The buyer pays, and the credit limit warns: M4-07 and M4-09, demo scope (29 Sep, branch `feat/m4-payments-credit-limit`).** **RecordPaymentReceipt** (`POST /v1/trading/payment-receipts`, `bil.payment.record`): the seller's accounts record a payment (cash, transfer, deposit, or cheque with bank, number and date) from the seller's ENTITY series of PRC; it settles chosen invoices, or the buyer's open undisputed invoices oldest first, and what is left stays on the buyer's account (an overpayment is held, never refused); insert-only `trading.payment_allocation` rows, the invoice's `settled_amount` recomputed from them (V0006); PAYMENT_RECEIPT_RECORDED, `payment_receipt.recorded.v1`, `journal.postings_ready.v1`. **RecordChequeOutcome** (`POST /v1/trading/payment-receipts/{id}/cheque-outcome`): CLEARED, or BOUNCED, which issues a PRC reversal, writes the negated allocations and reopens the invoices (CHEQUE_BOUNCED, `cheque.bounced.v1`, `payment_receipt.reversed.v1`). **Exposure** (`GET /v1/trading/exposures?role=`): open invoices plus accepted orders not yet invoiced, less what is on account, beside M1's credit limit and the highest threshold reached; AcceptOrder publishes `exposure.warning.v1` when the acceptance crosses a threshold and accepts all the same (ADR-12). The invoice answers `settledAmount`, `paymentState` (OPEN, PART_PAID, SETTLED) and its `payments`; a credit note may not credit more than is still due (`m4.creditnote.exceeds_due`). **Screens**: the invoice's payment state, payments and payment form; `/trading/payments/:id` (cheque cleared or bounced); `/trading/accounts/:role/:counterpartyId` (exposure, invoices, payments, payment form); the order desk's exposure panel; the desk's payments and accounts registers. **Demo**: `DemoPayments` pays eight of the history's invoices (in full by transfer or cleared cheque, in part in cash, one by a cheque that bounced), idempotent by reference; Point Pedro MPCS buys from D102 on a limit its history nearly reaches. **Proof**: `PaymentHandlersPostgresIntegrationTest`, `PaymentHttpPostgresIntegrationTest`, `DemoDataLoaderIntegrationTest`, the m4trading, demo and m8reporting integration tests, `:app:test`, `check-i18n`, `check-permissions`, `check-slices`, `check-frozen-contracts`, web `tsc -b`, `eslint`, `vitest run`; `web/e2e/trading.spec.ts` step 11 written, not run locally.
