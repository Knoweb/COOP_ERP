- **M1-09 external grants (25 Sep, branch `feat/m1-09-external-grants`).** The time-boxed read-only access of a regulator or auditor, doc 21 section 4.6 and flow 6.5, as 21A section 6 specifies it. **Commands** (`m1party/internal/grant`, `POST /v1/security/external-grants` and `POST /v1/security/external-grants/{grantId}/revoke`, both `gov.external.grant` with MFA; `GET /v1/security/external-grants` is the Federation's register, active, expired and revoked): GrantExternalView guards the Federation as the caller (entity-wide OWN, the `FederationCaller` guard with its own message id), a grantee the Federation can see, of kind EXTERNAL, not deactivated, every entity of the scope existing, the window, and a reason, then one ACTIVE row owned by the Federation, `EXTERNAL_GRANT_ISSUED` and `external_grant.issued.v1`; RevokeExternalView guards an ACTIVE grant whose window has not passed and a reason, then REVOKED, `EXTERNAL_GRANT_REVOKED`, `external_grant.revoked.v1`; ExpireExternalGrant (no HTTP; the job's command) guards ACTIVE and due, then EXPIRED, `EXTERNAL_GRANT_EXPIRED`, `external_grant.expired.v1`. **The window** (`GrantWindow`, a pure function with its own unit test): a missing or past start is now, a future start is kept, the end must be after the start, and no later than `m1.external_grant.max_months` after it (a new FEDERATION item in the register, 1 to 12, default 12, sensitive; assumes doc 10 L-07); the months are added by the database, as the table's CHECK constraint adds them in the session's time zone, so that a grant the guard admits is never refused by the constraint on the last day of February. **Scope resolution for EXTERNAL** (as corrected on 26 Sep, branch `fix/m1-roles-users-grants-review`): the kernel's `JdbcUserScopes.grantsOf` (K-02) is the one path a request takes; the M1 query `activeGrantedEntities` this ticket added was never called outside its tests and is deleted, and the policy `grantee_read` (V0009) stays in the schema unread. The kernel reads the ACTIVE grants of an ACTIVE grantee whose window contains now, caches them a minute at most and never past the earliest end, and forgets them on the grant and user events, so a revoked grant admits nothing at once on the revoking instance and an ended one from the second it ends on every instance, whether or not the job has run. **Expiry job** `external-grant-expiry` (K-12 `@ScheduledJob`, hourly at five past, `coop-erp.m1.external-grant-expiry-cron`): in the system scope, every ACTIVE grant past its end through the handler, one transaction each; without a system entity it logs and changes nothing. **What an EXTERNAL caller reads** (`m1security/V0009`): `ext_view` on `security.role`, `role_permission`, `user_role` and `sod_pair` for the granted entities, read only; `security.app_user` is left out on purpose (it carries `pin_hash` and `provider_subject`, which RLS cannot hide by column). **Not here:** the token mapper putting the grants into the token (K-02 part 2): the kernel cannot import a module, so it will need a kernel-side interface M1 implements, or the mapper calls this query; `ext_view` on `party.entity` (the other `party` tables have it from their own migrations: `location` and `till_position` in m1party V0005, `entity_relationship` in V0007, `device` in V0009; corrected on 26 Sep); login and export auditing and export volume on the exception queue (K-02, K-10); the grants screen (M1-12). **Proof:** `ExternalGrantsIntegrationTest` runs flow 6.5 through HTTP (grant, resolution to the one society, the external session reads that society's security rows and nothing of the other and writes nothing, the register lists it, a thirteen-month grant refused with nothing recorded, revocation immediate, an ended grant admitting nothing before the job and marked, audited and announced by it) and every guard of the three commands with nothing committed; `GrantWindowTest` every case of the window.
