- **Revenue Estimation / Sales Mix Planner (Polgolla Member 1 ticket M1-03, branch `feat/PGL-M1-03-revenue-estimation-planner`):** implemented stateless sales mix planning and quantity estimation in M8 (`m8reporting`), allowing store managers and planners to evaluate required SKU stock quantities needed to reach a monetary revenue target based on live effective pricing; confirmed domain placement within reporting without introducing persistent database tables or Flyway migrations (leaving underlying pricing, inventory, targets, and ledgers unmutated); integrated public query seams `PricingQueries.resolveRetailPrice` and `PricingQueries.resolveCommercialPrice` from `m3pricing` (registered in `package-info.java` allowed dependencies) alongside `CatalogueQueries.getSku` from `m2catalogue` without accessing private module tables; implemented deterministic mathematical calculation in `ReportQueriesImpl.estimateRevenue`: target revenue share $\text{revenueShare} = \text{targetRevenue} \times (\text{mixPercent} / 100)$, raw required quantity $\text{rawQty} = \text{revenueShare} / \text{effectivePrice}$, whole-unit integer rounding for discrete items (`!soldByWeight`, scale 0, HALF_UP) and 3-decimal precision for weighted items (`soldByWeight`, scale 3, HALF_UP), estimated item revenue $\text{estimatedRevenue} = \text{roundedQty} \times \text{effectivePrice}$, item rounding variance $\text{roundingVariance} = \text{estimatedRevenue} - \text{revenueShare}$, and overall target variance $\text{targetVariance} = \sum \text{estimatedRevenue} - \text{targetRevenue}$; enforced input validation refusing non-positive targets (`m8.estimator.target_revenue_invalid`), empty mix (`m8.estimator.mix_empty`), duplicate SKUs (`m8.estimator.duplicate_sku`), non-100% total mix percentages (`m8.estimator.mix_must_equal_100`), missing/inactive SKUs (`m8.estimator.sku_not_found`), and unresolvable selling prices (`m8.estimator.unresolved_price`); protected endpoint under existing permission `rpt.target.view` with strict tenant and location scope isolation; declared OpenAPI operation `POST /v1/reporting/revenue-estimates` in `backend/app/src/main/resources/openapi/m8reporting.yaml` with idempotency key and typed request/response schemas;
