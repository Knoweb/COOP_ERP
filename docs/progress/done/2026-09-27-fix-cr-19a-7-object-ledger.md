- **CR-19A-7 revised: the kernel's upload ledger under `ObjectStorage` (27 Sep, branch `fix/cr-19a-7-object-ledger`).** Decided 27 September 2026 on the architect's delegation: keep `kernel.api.ObjectStorage` (#137) and M2's `catalogue.sku_image`, and enforce the K-09 rules once in the kernel for every module that owns objects without a document (19A section 9; rejected: generalising `document_attachment`, images as documents). A review of #137 had found that `presignPut` checked no state (a settled key got a new PUT URL), `write` took no scope and no limit and accepted the base key (the verified original could be overwritten), the module in the key was free text, the key's entity was never compared with the caller's, and `JobExecution.ownScopeOf` gave any job any entity's scope. **Migration** `kernel/V0060__object_upload.sql` (lane C, the next free number): `kernel.object_upload` (key, owner module, owner entity, type, length, announced hash, PENDING/VERIFIED/FAILED with the failure, `upload_expires_at`, `created_at`, `settled_at`), the RLS template on `owner_entity_id` without a location (own_read/write/update, fed_view, ext_view), SELECT and INSERT plus UPDATE of the six settling columns only, and V0058's trigger pattern: only a PENDING row changes, the identity columns never. **Service** (`ObjectStorageService`): `presignPut(key, type, length, hash, ctx)` requires an OWN scope of the key's entity and a transaction, inserts or renews the PENDING row in the caller's transaction, refuses `object.not_pending` once settled and `object.content_type_mismatch` on a renewal of another type; `verify(key, ctx)` answers `PENDING` while the PUT URL is valid, then settles VERIFIED, or FAILED (MISSING only after `coop-erp.object-store.upload-window-hours`, TOO_LARGE before reading, HASH_MISMATCH against the announced hash) with `OBJECT_VERIFIED` / `OBJECT_FAILED` (REVIEW, with the reason), conditionally and once; a settled row answers what it settled with (`Verification.settled`); `write` only to a derived key (base plus one segment, else `object.not_derived`) of a VERIFIED base, under `attachment.content_types` and `attachment.max_bytes`; `read` with the key's entity in scope (OWN or PARTY of it, the federation view, an external grant naming it) and a VERIFIED base; `presignGet(key, type, ctx)` for any scope with a class, because who may see an image is the module's decision (a SHARED SKU's image is shown wherever the SKU is), taken when it read the key under its own RLS, with the base VERIFIED. Ledger reads and settlements outside `presignPut` run in `SystemScope.inOwnTransaction` (REQUIRES_NEW): the scope customizer never restores a connection's scope, so the kernel never re-scopes a caller's connection. **The module in the key**: `ObjectStorage.keyOf(Class<?> anchor, entity, id)` derives it from the anchor's package and refuses an anchor of another module than the caller's, and every method refuses a key of another module than the calling class's (a stack walk; no key written by hand gets round it). **`JobExecution.ownScopeOf`** cannot be narrowed by the kernel (it cannot know where the entity came from): kept, the risk documented on the method and in the CR, and `ArchitectureTests.onlyScheduledJobsTakeAnEntitysScope` (with a bite fixture) lets only a `@ScheduledJob` method call it. **M2**: AttachImage anchors the key on its own class and passes the announced hash; the ThumbnailJob treats `PENDING` as not yet, fails an image at once when the kernel settled its upload MISSING, and fails an image the ledger never recorded (`object.not_found`, one attached before V0060); SettleImage keeps its own window guard. Problem ids `object.scope_mismatch`, `object.not_pending`, `object.content_type_mismatch`, `object.not_found`, `object.not_verified`, `object.not_derived` in en/si/ta. **Proof**: `ObjectStoragePostgresIntegrationTest` (the key names the caller's module only; the presign's row, renewal and limits; PENDING inside the window, then VERIFIED once with its audit and the replay; a wrong hash, a too large object and an object that never came FAILED with their audits, one inside the window left alone; a settled key gets no new URL and the trigger refuses any change; only a derived key of a verified object is written, within the limits, the original never; another entity's scope refused on every method, the federation view reads), `AttachmentsPostgresIntegrationTest` (unchanged cases, the store now `MemoryObjectStore`), `ImageHandlersPostgresIntegrationTest` and `ImageHttpPostgresIntegrationTest` through the kernel's real `ObjectStorage` (the test double of the whole interface is gone), `RlsMatrixIntegrationTest` (`kernel.object_upload` on the template, no exception), `SchemaRulesIntegrationTest` (the ledger's grants), `ArchitectureRulesBiteTest`, `S3ObjectStoreIntegrationTest`, `CatalogueSnapshotContributorIntegrationTest`, the sync suite, the unit suite, `check-i18n`.
