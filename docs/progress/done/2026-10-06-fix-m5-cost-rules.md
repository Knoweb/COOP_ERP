- **The cost rules of stock: a transfer in at its own cost, one crossing per posting, an out at a given cost for the repack reversal (7 Oct, branch `fix/m5-cost-rules`; code review wave 2, plan PR 10 split, item (5) of `2026-10-06-wave2-stock-movements.md`).** Closes M5-06, M5-07, M5-17 (the reversal half; the rounding half accepted and recorded in the M5 README), M5-20, building CR-25A-1 item 5 and D10's cost rule. `MovementType.reaverages()` is gone: every type that carries its own cost re-averages, now TRANSFER_IN (at the cost its TRANSFER_OUT left with, so the pair conserves value and a GRN that lands while stock is in transit averages against the right base) and SALE_REVERSAL (at the original SALE's cost; declared, posted by no one until the till's voids and refunds). `CostService` gains "out at a given cost" (`avg' = (Q·a − q·c)/(Q − q)` while something remains, else the last average, never below zero), used by `ReverseRepackHandler`'s REPACK_CONSUME at `repack.output_unit_cost`; `Movement.unitCost` on an out movement means that cost, null the average; a negative given cost is `m5.ledger.cost_invalid`. `LedgerService` judges a lot's crossing by its sign when locked against its sign at the end of the posting (negative before and after keeps `negative_since` and records nothing). **Proof**: `CostServiceTest` (both transit cases, the out-at-cost rule and its clamp, the repack reversal, the sale reversal, 5,000 random sequences with transfer pairs, 5,000 outs at a given cost conserving value), `LedgerGuardsTest`, `LedgerServicePostgresIntegrationTest` (the three crossing cases; the property test with a shop, transfer pairs, repacks into a second item and their reversals, the crossing invariant and dense numbers at both locations), `RepackPostgresIntegrationTest` (the pack average comes back after a reversal), `TransferPostgresIntegrationTest`.
