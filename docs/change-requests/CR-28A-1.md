# CR-28A-1 — Cost of goods sold must come from M5's stock movement fact, not from the receipt bundle

- **Status:** raised
- **Date raised:** 2026-09-18
- **Raised by:** Claude Code session, while retiring `docs/HANDOVER.md`, whose §4 restated this point in a form that contradicts three documents
- **Documents affected:** 28A §11 (assumption "Cost of goods sold basis in sales_daily") and the `sales_daily` projection in 28A §6; 25A §6.1 and §7.4 (one sentence on who stamps the cost); 26A §3 (till `stock_movement`, `unit_cost_at_movement NULL`)
- **Code affected:** none yet; M5, M6 and M8 have not started

## What is wrong or missing

28A §11 assumes, for the M5 and M6 developers:

> Cost of goods sold basis in sales_daily — Entity average cost at issue from the receipt line (M5's unit_cost_at_movement carried into the bundle by M6) — If different: join to movement facts instead

Three documents say the till cannot carry that cost:

- 26A §3 declares the till's local table as `stock_movement(... unit_cost_at_movement NULL ...)`.
- 32 §5.1 lists what a shop's snapshot holds for lots: "batch id, SKU, expiry, printed MRP, FEFO rank, quantity as last known". No cost travels to a till.
- 25 §3.3: "issues (SALE, WRITE_OFF, TRANSFER_OUT within the entity unchanged, REPACK_CONSUME) carry unit_cost_at_movement = avg_cost at that moment", computed by M5's `CostService`. 25A §7.4: "sale deduction: not a separate bundle - M6's receipt bundle lines carry batchId; m5.sales consumes receipt.issued.v1".

So the cost exists only at central, at the moment M5 posts the SALE movement, and M5's `stock.moved.v1` already carries it: doc 25 §5 lists its payload as "location, batch, condition, qty delta, type, cost at ...", and doc 28 §3 consumes `stock.moved.v1` as "one row per movement with type and cost" for stock cards and cost of goods sold.

The retired briefing HANDOVER.md §4 turned 28A's assumption into a requirement ("needs unit_cost_at_movement to travel in the receipt bundle from the till"), which would have sent the M6 developer looking for a cost the till does not have.

## Why it matters

Built from the receipt bundle alone, `sales_daily` has a null cost of goods sold for every till sale. Asking M6 to supply it would mean pushing entity average cost to every till in the snapshot, which changes doc 32 §5.1, exposes cost on shop devices, and still yields a stale figure. The fallback 28A names itself needs no new data.

## Proposed change

1. **28A §11:** replace the assumption with "Entity average cost at issue is taken from M5's `stock.moved.v1` (`unit_cost_at_movement`), joined to the receipt line by `document_id` and `document_line_id`; the receipt bundle carries no cost." Drop "carried into the bundle by M6".
2. **28A §6, `sales_daily`:** name `stock.moved.v1` as the source of the cost columns and `receipt.issued.v1` as the source of the revenue side. The movement event arrives after the receipt event, because `m5.sales` posts the movement in its own transaction, so the projection upserts the cost columns when it arrives; a day's row is not final until its movement events are applied, which the freshness stamp already expresses.
3. **25A §6.1 and §7.4:** one sentence stating that `ApplySale` stamps `unit_cost_at_movement` from the entity average at posting and that `stock.moved.v1` carries it. **26A §3:** keep `unit_cost_at_movement NULL` and add the reason.

## Impact if accepted

28A re-issued as v0.2 (assumption and projection source). 25A and 26A gain one sentence each, either as v0.2 or as a register note "amended by CR-28A-1". No change to docs 18, 25, 26 or 32. Code: none yet; when M8 starts, the `sales_daily` consumer subscribes to `stock.moved.v1`.
