## What is wrong or missing

1. **Credits** (M4MONEY-01, 02, 03, 05, 13): the same damaged units can be credited by a discrepancy settlement and then by a DAMAGED claim; a credit-note request may name a line twice; `ApproveClaim` silently caps an approved quantity at the billed one so the decision and the credit note disagree; per-slice rounding over-credits by up to half a cent a slice; after a cash receipt every credit above `gross - credited - settled` is refused (`m4.creditnote.exceeds_due`), so a paid invoice with damaged goods is a dead end, and because `SettleDiscrepancy` is one transaction the short quantity on the same discrepancy is blocked too; a partial approval locks the un-approved quantity for good while a rejection frees the whole. 24A §6 is silent on all of these; doc 24 §3.6 already says an unapplied credit note "is applied later with a link".
2. **Cheques** (M4MONEY-09): `trading.cheque` is keyed by receipt only; the same cheque can be recorded twice. 24A §3 has no constraint and doc 24 §3.7 no rule.
3. **The counterparty's columns** (RLS-10): doc 18 §3.7 promises PARTY readers masking of "cost, margin and internal notes"; `trading.v_document_party` exists in the template's text only; 24A §3 does not say which columns are internal.
4. **The buyer's postings** (M4MONEY-10, M9-02): the map's `GRN GOODS BUYER` and `INV GOODS BUYER` rows have no producer; the price basis of the GRN accrual is unstated; there are no CN BUYER rows.

## Why it matters

Section 3.6's "reduces the invoice's open balance" and the promise of #209 ("a billed unit is credited once") do not hold; a seller can pay out twice for one damage; the books of 800 buying societies have no payables.

## Proposed change

1. **24A §6, one credited-quantity rule**: for every credit path the invoice is locked (`lockForLinking`) before its lines and the credits already issued are read; `remaining = billed - credited` per line, `credited` summed over CN lines referencing the invoice line; IssueCreditNote refuses a line named twice and a quantity over the remainder; SettleDiscrepancy credits `min(damaged, remaining)` and may settle with no money; ApproveClaim refuses rather than caps; the last slice of a line credits `billed - credited` of net and tax exactly. **RaiseClaim**'s open quantity is `received - damaged_qty - claimedBefore` for every claim kind (the GRN's damaged quantity is the discrepancy's to credit); `claimedBefore` counts `approved_qty` for APPROVED, `claimed_qty` for undecided, nothing for REJECTED.
2. **24A §6 and doc 24 §3.6, the unapplied credit note**: a credit note is issued for the goods in full; its CREDITS link to the origin invoice carries `min(gross, due)` and is absent when nothing is due; its unapplied amount is `gross - Σ its CREDITS links` (a sum, never stored); it lowers the buyer's exposure as §6.3's `unappliedCredits`; **ApplyCreditNote** (`POST /v1/trading/credit-notes/{id}/apply`, `bil.creditnote.issue`; the seller's own issued CN, an open undisputed invoice of the same pair, `0 < amount <= min(unapplied, due)`; audit `CREDIT_NOTE_APPLIED`, event `credit_note.applied.v1`) writes a further CREDITS link. No refund (ADR-01). `doc_credit_note.invoice_document_id` keeps naming the origin invoice.
3. **24A §3 and doc 24 §3.7, the cheque**: a cheque is `(seller, payer, upper(btrim(bank)), ltrim(btrim(cheque_no), '0'))`; `dated` is not in the key; recorded again only when every earlier receipt of this seller from this payer carrying it has a BOUNCED outcome, else `m4.payment.cheque_recorded`; a guard under an advisory lock, no unique index. A payment is recorded against the pair's relationship of status ACTIVE or SUSPENDED (CR-21A-7).
4. **24A §3, the counterparty's columns**: internal (seller only) = `order_allocation_line.override_reason`, `order_allocation.lock_override_by`, moved to `trading.order_allocation_note` with `own_*` policies; every other column named in §3 is shared with the PARTY reader. `trading.v_document_party` is withdrawn from the text.
5. **24A §3.1 and doc 24 §3.9, the buyer's postings**: `ConfirmGrn` publishes `GRN GOODS BUYER` (INVENTORY / GRN_ACCRUAL) in the receiver's scope at `Σ received_qty × unit_cost`, the GRN line's cost being the delivery note's price snapshot, the invoice's own basis, so the accrual clears per GRN–invoice pair; the seller's `journal.postings_ready.v1` carries `counterpartyEntityId` and the BUYER lines of INV and CN, delivered to the buyer by CR-19A-13; new map rows `CN GOODS BUYER` (PAYABLE / INVENTORY net, PAYABLE / VAT_INPUT tax); PRC buyer rows are not published (doc 10 A-01).
6. **24A §6.3, exposure per order line**: the uninvoiced value of an accepted order line is `(allocated - fulfilled)` plus, per delivery line, `dispatched_qty` while no confirmed GRN names its drop, else `received_qty - invoiced_qty` of the GRN line, floored at 0, at `tier_price`; a short quantity is never owed. **24A §3**: `invoice_dispute.seq` (identity) orders dispute acts; `recorded_at` is for display.
