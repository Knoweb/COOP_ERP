# CR-21A-2 — Two rules of AmendRelationshipTerms the documents do not state: a backdated amendment, and a row that has not started yet

- **Status:** accepted (item 1 option (a); item 2 option (a))
- **Date raised:** 2026-09-26

Decided 27 Sep 2026: an amendment is never dated before today, and a row whose first day is still to come is corrected on that day by marking it REPLACED (PR #PRNUM), on the architect's delegation.

**Item 1, (a): no backdating.** `effectiveFrom` must be on or after today, the calendar date in the business time zone (`coop-erp.business-timezone`, Asia/Colombo; a relationship has no location, so `BusinessDate.current(location)` does not apply), besides 21A's "after the current row's first day". New code `m1.relationship.effective_from_in_past`. Reasons: M4 reads `LookupRelationship` for a date (24A §6.2 AcceptOrder, §6.3 RecordPaymentReceipt; the exposure and due dates of doc 24), and M8 reports terms by day; with (a) the answer for a day that has begun never changes, so no module has to copy the terms onto its documents to be safe, and a commercial renegotiation "from the start of the quarter" is recorded from today with the reason saying so. It is also the cheaper to reverse: allowing backdating later is a guard removed, while forbidding it after rows have been backdated would leave history already rewritten. Option (b) would put a rule on every future reader of the lookup, in modules not built yet.

**Item 2, (a): REPLACED.** An amendment dated exactly on the first day of the latest row, while that day is still after today, marks the row REPLACED (terms and range kept, as the record of what was agreed) and a new ACTIVE row takes its whole range. A row whose first day has come is never replaced: 21A's guard applies to it as written. A REPLACED row is never in force (the lookup and the A-I3 exclusion read ACTIVE rows only) and is amended no more (`m1.relationship.not_active`). Reasons: option (b), suspend and reopen, is worse than a workaround: since the review of M1-04 a suspension stops every ACTIVE row of the pair in force today or later, so correcting next month's row would stop this month's trading. Built: `m1party/V0011` (the status CHECK), `Relationship.replace()`, `AmendRelationshipTermsHandler`, `RelationshipResponse.status` gains REPLACED in `m1party.yaml` (version 1.1.0, `x-change-request: CR-21A-2`) and the generated web client, `RelationshipView`'s documentation. Doc 18 Part A, 21A §3.1, §5 and §6 take the fourth status and the date rule at their next issue.
- **Raised by:** Claude Code session, from the review of 26 September (M1-04, `AmendRelationshipTermsHandler`)
- **Documents affected:** 21A §6 (the guard "effective_from > current row's effective_from") and §6.1 (the pseudocode); doc 21 §4.2 (ACTIVE → ACTIVE (new row)); doc 18 Part A (`entity_relationship.status`) and 21A §3.1 (its CHECK) and §5 (`RelationshipResponse.status`) for item 2
- **Code affected:** `m1party/internal/relationship/AmendRelationshipTermsHandler`, `RelationshipQueriesImpl.lookupRelationship`; a migration `m1party/V0010` and the slice, for item 2

## 1. An amendment may be dated in the past

**What is wrong.** 21A §6 gives the amendment one date guard: "effective_from > current row's effective_from". Nothing says the new terms must start today or later. On 25 September the seller may amend from 1 July: the current row is closed on 30 June and a new ACTIVE row runs from 1 July.

**Why it matters.** `LookupRelationship(seller, buyer, date)` answers the row in force on a date (21A §7). July's orders were accepted under the July terms as they stood then (30 days, a limit of Rs 3,000,000); after the backdated amendment the lookup for 15 July answers the new row, and M4's exposure and due-date work for July reads terms that were never in force that day. The effective-dated history the design promises ("the old row is never edited", doc 21 flow 6.4) is kept row by row, but the answer to "what was in force on that day" changes. Whether that is wanted (a renegotiation that the parties agreed to apply from the start of the quarter) or forbidden (terms apply from the day they are recorded, never earlier) is a commercial rule that belongs in doc 21 §4.2.

**Proposed change.** Doc 21 §4.2 and 21A §6 state the rule, one of: (a) "effective_from on or after the business date of the amendment" (M4 then never sees a past day change); or (b) backdating is allowed, and M4 reads the terms in force at the time an order was accepted from the order, never again from the lookup. Until decided, the code keeps the guide's guard as written (any day after the current row's first day).

## 2. A row that has not started yet cannot be corrected on its first day

**What is wrong.** After an amendment dated 1 October, the pair has R1 [1 April, 30 September] and R2 [1 October, open], both ACTIVE. The review found the handler accepted a second amendment on R1 (which copied R1's closing date onto the new terms, so the change stopped on 30 September and R2 kept the old terms); the handler now amends the latest row only (`m1.relationship.not_latest`). A correction of R2 itself, though, can only be dated 2 October or later (the guard above), so a mistake in R2's terms cannot be put right for 1 October: R2 cannot be closed before it starts (the CHECK `effective_to >= effective_from`), and the status list of doc 18 Part A and 21A §3.1 (DRAFT, ACTIVE, SUSPENDED) has no state for a row that was replaced before it took effect.

**Why it matters.** A typo in a future row is fixed today by a one-day gap in the wrong terms, or by suspending the pair and opening a new DRAFT. Either is a workaround nobody wrote down.

**Proposed change.** One of: (a) a fourth status, REPLACED, for a row whose first day has not come, set by an amendment dated exactly on that row's first day, which then takes the row's range; the status joins doc 18 Part A, 21A §3.1's CHECK (a new migration, `m1party/V0010`), 21A §5's enum and the A-I3 exclusion (it excludes ACTIVE rows only, so nothing changes there); or (b) the rule that a row is amended only from the day after it starts, said in 21A §6, and a future row's terms are corrected by suspending and re-opening. Nothing is applied before acceptance.

## Impact if accepted

Doc 21 §4.2 and 21A §6 and §6.1 re-issued for item 1; for item 2 (a), doc 18 Part A, 21A §3.1 and §5 as well, plus `m1party/V0010`, the slice and the generated client, `Relationship`, `AmendRelationshipTermsHandler` and `RelationshipHandlersTest`. Item 2 (b) is a sentence in 21A §6 and no code.
