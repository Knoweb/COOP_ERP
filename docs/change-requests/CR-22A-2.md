# CR-22A-2 — Product image thumbnails are JPEG, and an image audits and publishes at both halves of its flow

- **Status:** accepted as revised (27 September 2026, on the architect's delegation); applied in the repository; doc 22 and 22A not re-issued yet
- **Decided 27 Sep 2026:** accepted as revised in PR #PRNUM, on the architect's delegation (see "Decision").
- **Date raised:** 2026-09-27
- **Raised by:** Claude Code session, M2 catalogue decisions (the "For the architect" items of pull request #137, M2-06 images)
- **Documents affected:** doc 22 §3.4 (thumbnails), §5.3 (events), §7 (`catalogue.thumbnail_px / max_thumb_kb`); 22A §5 (`LookupResult.thumbKey` example), §6 (AttachImage: audit and events)
- **Code affected:** `m2catalogue/internal/image/Thumbnailer`, `ThumbnailJob`, `ImageSettings`; `seed/kernel/config-items.yaml` (`m2.image.thumbnail_max_kb`); the audit codes and events of M2-06 as built in #137

## What is wrong or missing

Doc 22 section 3.4: "An M2 job (ImageThumbnailJob) produces the 128 px WebP thumbnail on attach"; section 7 sets `catalogue.thumbnail_px / max_thumb_kb` to 128 / 10. The JDK has no WebP writer, and M2-06 was asked for plain Java without a native library, so #137 wrote PNG, which for a photograph at 128 px is several times the 10 KB the design sets. Doc 32 section 5.2 holds the full snapshot of a shop "under 20 MB with thumbnails".

22A section 6 gives AttachImage one audit, `IMAGE_ATTACHED`, and one event, `image.attached.v1` "(after thumbnail)". The flow has two halves in two transactions: the attach (a PENDING row and a pre-signed PUT) and the job that settles the upload. Every command audits and publishes (AGENTS.md), and the job's half can end ACTIVE or FAILED. #137 therefore added `image.pending.v1` at the attach, `image.failed.v1`, and the audit codes `IMAGE_ACTIVATED`, `IMAGE_FAILED` and `IMAGE_RETIRED`.

## Options considered

1. **WebP through a native library** (libwebp over JNI, or a Java wrapper shipping its binaries): meets the design, but puts a platform-specific binary into the backend image, a second supply-chain path, and a job that fails on a platform the binary was not built for.
2. **PNG, as built**: plain Java, lossless, transparency kept; roughly 20 to 40 KB per product photograph at 128 px, so a shop of a few thousand items spends most of the snapshot budget of doc 32 on thumbnails.
3. **JPEG, within the size budget**: plain Java (the JDK writes baseline JPEG), about 4 to 8 KB per photograph at 128 px, which is doc 22's 10 KB; no transparency (a product card's background is white); every Android version decodes it.

## Decision

Decided 27 Sep 2026: accepted as revised (PR #PRNUM), on the architect's delegation.

- **The thumbnail is a JPEG** (option 3), fitted into `m2.image.thumbnail_px` (128), never enlarged, laid on white when the upload is transparent, at the first quality of 0.85, 0.75, 0.65, 0.55 or 0.45 that keeps it within `m2.image.thumbnail_max_kb` (10, the register's copy of doc 22's `max_thumb_kb`); when none does, the smallest is kept. The key is `<full key>/thumb.jpg`, the content type `image/jpeg`. The till fetches whatever key the snapshot names, so the format is not part of the sync contract.
- **The split of the audit and the events is confirmed.** AttachImage audits `IMAGE_ATTACHED` and publishes `image.pending.v1`; the job's SettleImage audits `IMAGE_ACTIVATED` and publishes `image.attached.v1` after the thumbnail exists, as 22A writes, or audits `IMAGE_FAILED` and publishes `image.failed.v1`; RetireImage audits `IMAGE_RETIRED` and publishes `image.retired.v1`. The three audit codes beyond 22A's `IMAGE_ATTACHED` stay.

Reasons: JPEG is the format the JDK writes that meets the size doc 22 sets for the reason doc 22 chose WebP (small thumbnails on a till's weak link), without a native binary; WebP stays possible later behind the same key-per-row scheme, since every row stores its own thumbnail key. The events follow what already happens: a consumer that re-sends a SKU to tills (M2-09's change log) must act when the thumbnail exists, not at the attach, and a failed upload needs its own fact for the person who attached it.

## Change made

`Thumbnailer` writes JPEG within the budget; `ThumbnailJob` keys `thumb.jpg` and passes `m2.image.thumbnail_max_kb`; the new configuration item in `seed/kernel/config-items.yaml`. `ThumbnailerTest` (JPEG output, the budget kept for a photograph, the smallest kept when no quality fits, a transparent PNG laid on white) and `ImageHandlersPostgresIntegrationTest` (the thumbnail produced and keyed as `thumb.jpg`).

## Impact

Doc 22 §3.4 ("JPEG of at most `max_thumb_kb`" for "WebP"), §5.3 (`image.pending.v1`, `image.failed.v1`), §7 (unchanged values); 22A §5 (`thumbKey` example `.../thumb.jpg`) and §6 (AttachImage's audit and event per half, the three codes) at their next re-issue.
