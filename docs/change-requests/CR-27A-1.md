# CR-27A-1 — The credit book: REOPEN, credits settle charges, the erasure guard and what erasure leaves, one society per person in v1

- **Status:** accepted (6 October 2026, on the architect's delegation; wave 2 code review); the documents are not re-issued yet
- **Date raised:** 2026-10-06
- **Raised by:** Claude Code session, wave 2 code review decisions (`wave2-fixreview-m7-credit.md` decisions 2 to 6 and 8), recorded in `docs/progress/deviations/2026-10-06-wave2-m7-credit-book.md` and `2026-10-06-wave2-m7-erasure-and-retention.md`
- **Documents affected:** 27A §6 (ChangeAccountStatus and the account state machine), §6.3 (allocation and ageing), §6.4 (`Anonymiser.apply`, the erasure guard), §7.3 (till facts on a closed account), §11 (the cross-society assumption); doc 27 §4.2 (states), §6.7 (retention), §7 (configuration items `nic_required_above_limit`, `retention_years`); doc 10 L-05 (the erasure wording for Federation legal gains one sentence)
- **Code affected:** `m7customers/V0003` (`lock_accounts_for_erasure`, the `app_seed` UPDATE policy on `customer_account`, `GRANT UPDATE (notes, outcome) ON data_subject_request`) and `V0004` (the CHECK on `account_history.action`); `ChangeAccountStatusHandler`, `Ledger`, the two payment handlers, `PostAccountTender`, `AnonymiseCustomerHandler`, `PrivacyExporter`, a new `DownloadAccessExportHandler`; `seed/kernel/config-items.yaml`

## What is wrong or missing

1. The state machine has no transition out of CLOSED. A till CHARGE or CPR posts on a CLOSED account by design (27A §7.3, "cash was taken"), after which every office command (payment, reversal, adjustment) refuses the account, and a second account for the pair is impossible (`UNIQUE (customer_id, owner_entity_id)`). The balance can never be settled.
2. 27A §6.3 allocates payments only. A CREDIT (void, refund) or a negative ADJUSTMENT lowers the balance and settles nothing, so ageing and "oldest open charge" age a charge the customer no longer owes, and the next payment is allocated to it; a society that suspends for arrears (doc 27 §6.3) would suspend a member who owes nothing.
3. 27A §6.4's erasure precondition is a balance count under no lock; the anonymiser touches no account; the snapshot learns of the erasure only at the next full snapshot; a till may upload a two-day-old ACCOUNT sale afterwards. Free text about the person survives in `data_subject_request.notes`/`outcome` and the audit `after` maps of two handlers; what erasure must leave (the ledger) is nowhere written down with its legal basis.
4. The access export is a GET with no officer guard and no audit, and it carries `nic_hash`. 27A's `export_object_key` is not built.
5. 27A §11 assumes a person can hold an account at two societies; the build cannot do it (society B cannot see A's customer), and the gap is recorded only in a test comment.

## Why it matters

A closed account with a till charge is money the society cannot record as settled; false ageing drives suspensions; the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 gives a right to erasure (section 16) that yields to retention required by law, and the design must say which is which; an unaudited export of a member's whole record breaks doc 27 §9.3.

## Proposed change

1. **Doc 27 §4.2 and 27A §6**: `CLOSED → SUSPENDED: ReopenAccount(reason)` on `ChangeAccountStatus` (`REOPEN`), `cus.account.manage`, no MFA, history `REOPENED`, audit `ACCOUNT_REOPENED`, event `account.reopened.v1`. The officer's path after a flagged till fact on a closed account is reopen, settle or reverse, close again.
2. **27A §6.3**: "A CREDIT or a negative ADJUSTMENT is allocated like a payment: a CREDIT first against the open charge of the same `document_id`, then oldest first; an ADJUSTMENT oldest first. A reversal re-applies the account's unallocated credit oldest first. `unallocated` is defined over the whole credit side (PAYMENT, REVERSAL, CREDIT, negative ADJUSTMENT), so `Σ open charges − unallocated = balance` holds by construction." The ledger property test asserts it.
3. **27A §6.4**: the precondition becomes "every account of the customer at every society CLOSED with zero balance, locked for the transaction (`customers.lock_accounts_for_erasure`, SECURITY DEFINER), none closed within `customers.erasure_wait_days` (FEDERATION, default 3)"; refusals `m7.privacy.open_balance`, `m7.privacy.account_open`, `m7.privacy.recently_closed`. A till fact that lands after erasure is posted, never rejected, flagged `ACCOUNT_CHARGED_CUSTOMER_INACTIVE` (status ANONYMISED), and settled through REOPEN. **Doc 27 §6.7 and doc 10 L-05**: erased or redacted are the identity (name, phones, NIC hash and last four, attributes, tags, consents) and the module's own free text about the person (`data_subject_request.notes = null, outcome = 'ERASED'`); retained for `customers.retention_years` (FEDERATION, default 7) under the accounting and legal-claims exemptions are postings, allocations, CPRs with their `reference`, the account row with number and history, adjustment reasons, kernel audit rows and the DSAR records with coded outcomes, keyed by the opaque customer id. No UPDATE grant on history, adjustment or document columns. Officer free text is guarded at write against phone and NIC values (`m7.field.personal_data`); reasons travel in the audit's `reason`, never in `after`.
4. **27A §6 (new) DownloadAccessExport**: `POST /v1/privacy/requests/{id}/export`, `cus.privacy.fulfil`, the request's society, FULFILLED, ACCESS, customer not anonymised, the responsible officer; audit `DSAR_EXPORT_DOWNLOADED` with whether the recomputed hash still equals `export_sha256` ("the hash at fulfilment"); `nic_hash` is not exported, `nic_last4` is. `export_object_key` waits for the kernel attachment API. **27A §6 AmendAccountLimits** accepts an optional `nic`, required when the limit rises above `customers.nic_required_above_limit` (doc 27 §7, now seeded: FEDERATION, default 0) and the customer holds none; one `NicCapture` serves OpenAccount, AmendAccountLimits and RecaptureNic (`2026-10-06-wave2-keyed-hashes.md`).
5. **27A §11**: "In v1 a person holds credit at one society. `account_holder_read` stays for the designed end state (ADR-15). `nic_holders` and `phone_holders` answer 'held elsewhere' without identifying the holder. The cross-society opening path is built after the joint-controller agreement (doc 10 L-02, ADR-26) is signed: a SECURITY DEFINER `identity_for_account_opening(phone)` answering id, status, a masked name and whether a NIC is held to an OWN scope; the second society inserts its CREDIT_ACCOUNT consent and its account row; the NIC check runs through `nic_holders`." Raised here, built later.

## Impact if accepted

- **Documents**: 27A re-issued (§6, §6.3, §6.4, §7.3, §11); doc 27 (§4.2, §6.7, §7); doc 10 L-05 gains the sentence on facts that arrive after erasure.
- **Code**: the two migrations; the handlers; three configuration items (`nic_required_above_limit`, `erasure_wait_days`, `retention_years`); the privacy page's download becomes a POST.
- **Tests**: reopen from CLOSED and refused from OPEN/SUSPENDED; close, till charge, reopen, office payment, close; the ledger property under random charges, credits, payments, reversals, adjustments; `PrivacyRequestsIntegrationTest` closes accounts and moves the clock before erasing; a privacy scan after erasure over the module's text columns.
- **Rejected**: settling on a CLOSED account; automatic reopening by a till fact; leaving credits unallocated; blocking erasure for ever on an account once used (the Act does not allow it); redacting history, adjustment and document columns; coded reasons only; building the cross-society path now; dropping `account_holder_read`.
