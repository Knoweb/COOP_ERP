# CR-18-2 — FEDERATION_VIEW and EXTERNAL_TIMEBOXED do not read personal data of natural persons by policy

- **Status:** accepted (6 October 2026, on the architect's delegation; wave 2 code review); the documents are not re-issued yet
- **Date raised:** 2026-10-06
- **Raised by:** Claude Code session, wave 2 code review decisions (`docs/reviews/wave2-fixreview-rls.md` RLS-01, RLS-02; `wave2-fixreview-till-web-kernel.md` TWK-30; `wave2-fixreview-m9-integration.md` M9-06), recorded in `docs/progress/deviations/2026-10-06-wave2-member-identity-visibility.md`
- **Documents affected:** 18 §3.7 (the four-class table: FEDERATION_VIEW "Everything, including margins and shrinkage"; "the Federation and external grantees read everything in their scope and write nothing"); 27 §5.2 (`LookupCustomer` "Federation view") and §9.5 ("Federation view-only"); 21A §3 (the read set a FEDERATION_VIEW session resolves); 29 §8 (the contacts table)
- **Code affected:** `m7customers/V0003` (drop `fed_view`, `ext_view` on `customers.customer`, `customer_phone`, `customer_consent`; `ext_view` on `data_subject_request`); `m9integration/V0006` (drop `fed_view` on `integration.notification_contact`); `kernel.internal.security.JdbcPermissionResolver` and `SliceOperations.readPermissions()` (the `x-federation-view: false` mark); `openapi/m1party.yaml`, `m1security`, `m7customers.yaml` (the marked operations); `RlsMatrixIntegrationTest.EXCEPTIONS`

## What is wrong or missing

Doc 18 §3.7 gives FEDERATION_VIEW "Everything" and doc 27 §9.5 says the customer record is "Federation view-only". Doc 27 §9.3 says "every read of a customer record outside the sales path is audited". A FEDERATION_VIEW session reads `customers.customer` (name, phone, NIC hash and last four, language, consents) by row-level policy, which no audit record can see. Doc 10 L-01 (joint controllers) and L-02 (the data-governance agreement per entity, ADR-26) are "not yet commissioned", so the lawful basis for the Federation to read the identity of several million members of 800 societies is not yet written. Section 5 (purpose limitation) and section 6 (data minimisation) of the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 ask for the narrowest reach while that is open. No Federation code reads `customers.*` at all; M8's aggregates come from the account tables. The same applies to an external grantee (regulator, auditor), who checks balances against accounts, not people, and to `integration.notification_contact` (an address is personal data of a named person).

## Why it matters

After the NIC hash is keyed (`2026-10-06-wave2-keyed-hashes.md`) the remaining exposure of the two policies is every member's name, phone and consents, readable without an audit row by any view-all role. The permission resolver hands a FEDERATION_VIEW session every GET permission of every slice (21A §3), so the permission layer does not narrow it either. M7CR-12 of the review was dropped for following the documents; this request changes the documents first, which is the right order.

## Proposed change

1. **Doc 18 §3.7**, a carve-out sentence in the FEDERATION_VIEW and EXTERNAL_TIMEBOXED rows: "Personal data of natural persons (a customer's name, phone, identity number and consents; a notification contact's address) is excluded: these classes do not read it by policy. It reaches the Federation or a grantee through a module's audited query or a named export when one is specified. The credit book (accounts, postings, allocations, adjustments, history) is read as before: money, no name."
2. **Doc 27 §5.2**: `LookupCustomer` loses "Federation view"; **§9.5**: "Federation view-only" becomes "the society only; the Federation and external grantees read the account tables, never the customer, phone or consent rows".
3. **21A §3** (and 19A §2 where the resolver is specified): "An operation marked `x-federation-view: false` in its OpenAPI slice is excluded from the set a FEDERATION_VIEW session resolves. Marked: M1 user, device and credential administration reads; the privacy-request reads and exports; M7 customer personal-data views. The rule in one sentence: the Federation reads its members' trading, stock, finance and reporting data; it does not read their staff administration or their customers' personal data."
4. **Doc 29 §8**: `notification_contact` is own-entity only; the Federation view reads nothing of it.

## Impact if accepted

- **Documents**: 18 re-issued (§3.7), 27 (§5.2, §9.5), 21A (§3), 29 (§8); versions set at re-issue.
- **Code**: the two migrations above; the slice mark and its reader; `RlsMatrixIntegrationTest` takes `onlyTheOwnerReads` for the four tables. `CustomerSnapshotContributor` and the privacy export run in OWN scope and are unaffected.
- **Tests**: the matrix rows change for four tables; one resolver test proves a marked operation is absent from a FEDERATION_VIEW set and present in an OWN one.
- **Rejected**: keeping "Everything" and auditing at the API only (the SQL read stays unaudited); a SECURITY DEFINER masking view for the Federation (no consumer in v1; add when one exists); a `federation_view` flag in the permission table (a migration and a seed for a fact the slice already knows).
