# CR-21A-7 — Administration guardrails: credential reset within rank, limits within the grantor's, the limits schema of the approval codes, a settlement relationship query

- **Status:** accepted (6 October 2026, on the architect's delegation; wave 2 code review); 21A not re-issued yet
- **Date raised:** 2026-10-06
- **Raised by:** Claude Code session, wave 2 code review decisions (`wave2-fixreview-m6-m8-m1admin.md` M1A-01, M1A-03, M1A-04, M1A-05, M1A-06; `wave2-fixreview-m5-m3.md` M5-09; `wave2-fixreview-m4-money.md` M4MONEY-08), recorded in `docs/progress/deviations/2026-10-06-wave2-m1-administration.md`, `2026-10-06-wave2-stock-approvals.md` and `2026-10-06-wave2-payments-and-cheques.md`
- **Documents affected:** 21A §3.3 (the permission catalogue: `limits_schema` of `inv.writeoff.approve` and `inv.adjust.approve`; the segregation pairs seed), §6 (handler specifications of ResetCredential, UpdateUser, DeactivateUser, CreateRole, AmendRole, AssignRole, OpenTradingRelationship, ActivateRelationship), §6.1 (`RelationshipQueries`, the frozen query contract); doc 21 §3 (the segregation defaults) and §6.4 (raising a credit limit) are the sources and do not change
- **Code affected:** `m1security/V0019` (`limits_schema` on two permissions; delete of the `gov.role.manage`/`gov.user.manage` pair; the user-manager count joined on `app_user.user_kind` if it is not already); `m1security` handlers named above; `m1party.query.RelationshipQueries`; `seed/m1party/permissions.yaml`, `sod-pairs.yaml`

## What is wrong or missing

1. `ResetCredential` (21A §6) guards scope, deactivation and applicability only: a holder of `gov.user.manage` resets any user's credentials, including the role manager's, and may receive the temporary password. `UpdateUser` can move a user to kind TILL (closing the back office) and `DeactivateUser` can deactivate them, with the same gap; `UpdateUser` also has no last-holder guard, and the "another user manager" counts include TILL-only users who cannot sign in.
2. `RoleGuards.withinGrantor` compares permission codes only; the day a `limits_schema` exists, a grantor with `max_value` 25,000 can grant 250,000.
3. The write-off bands of doc 25 §7 are "role limits (M1)", but no permission carries a `limits_schema`, M1 refuses limits on a permission without one, and the seeder never updates an existing row: no limit can be set, and M5's approval falls open.
4. `sod-pairs.yaml` seeds a `gov.role.manage`/`gov.user.manage` INSTANCE pair that is in neither doc 21 §3 nor 21A's seed, and no M1 handler calls `Sod.assertDistinct` for it; the seeded Entity Administrator template holds both codes.
5. `OpenTradingRelationship` stores a credit limit with no permission or MFA check; `ActivateRelationship` checks nothing; doc 21 §6.4 gates limit changes.
6. M4 refuses a payment from a SUSPENDED buyer because M1's `lookupRelationship` admits ACTIVE only; doc 21 §4 says a suspension leaves open documents unaffected.

## Why it matters

A society's administrator must be able to reset a cashier's password but not take over the role manager's account; a one-administrator society must keep working; a fail-open approval limit on the highest-fraud-risk lifecycle (doc 25 §9.4) is not acceptable at go-live; a suspended buyer's money must be recorded.

## Proposed change

21A §6 gains, per handler:

- **ResetCredential, UpdateUser (a kind change that removes the login), DeactivateUser**: refused with `m1.user.target_outranks_caller` when the target holds, at the entity, any permission whose catalogue entry has `requires_mfa = true` that the caller does not hold; `m1.user.credential_self` for a reset of one's own SECOND_FACTOR. **UpdateUser** takes `EntityLock` and refuses `m1.user.last_user_manager` as RevokeRole does; every "another user manager" count counts users whose kind has a login (BACK_OFFICE, BOTH).
- **CreateRole, AmendRole, AssignRole**: for each numeric property of a `limits_schema`, the granted value is at or below the grantor's effective value for that code at the entity (the highest over their holdings; a holding with no limits is unlimited); `m1.role.limit_exceeds_grantor`.
- **OpenTradingRelationship** with a non-null credit limit and **ActivateRelationship** of a draft carrying one: `bil.creditlimit.change` and a fresh second factor; Activate publishes `credit_limit.changed.v1` (null → the limit).

21A §3.3: `inv.writeoff.approve` and `inv.adjust.approve` carry `limits_schema = {"properties":{"max_value":{"type":"number","minimum":0}},"required":["max_value"]}`; the segregation seed is doc 21's pairs only (`gov.audit.review`/`gov.user.manage` at INSTANCE, enforced at the exception acknowledgement of 28A when built); the `gov.role.manage`/`gov.user.manage` pair is removed.

21A §6.1: `RelationshipQueries` gains `settlementRelationship(seller, buyer, onDate, scope)`: the pair's row of status ACTIVE or SUSPENDED in force on the day, else the most recent ACTIVE or SUSPENDED row; DRAFT and REPLACED never qualify. Additive on the frozen contract, as CR-21A-4 and CR-21A-6 were.
