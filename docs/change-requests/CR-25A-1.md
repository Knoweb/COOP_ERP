## What is wrong or missing

1. Nothing compares a lot's expiry with a date: the FEFO rank, availability, delivery and transfer picks and the pricing candidates all include expired lots, which are ranked first (M5-01).
2. The approval bands are never enforced: `requireWithinLimit` fails open when no limit is found, and no limit can be set because the two approval permissions carry no `limits_schema` (M5-09). A zero-cost line is band 1 whatever its quantity.
3. Doc 25 §3.8 requires witness ≠ requester and approver ≠ requester only; at a multi-staff location the witness may approve, so the "highest-fraud-risk lifecycle" (§9.4) needs two people (M5-10).
4. The count's book is read at submit (doc 10 E-10 taken literally), so sales between counting a shelf and pressing submit are surpluses that auto-post within tolerance (M5-13); tolerance is quantity-or-percent only, with no value cap and no exception-report entry for cleared variances, which §9.4 asks for (M5-14).
5. A repack's output quantity is unchecked against the recipe (M5-18); a repack reversal costs the output's consumption at the output item's current average, not at the repack's own cost (M5-17); TRANSFER_IN does not re-average, so an intake during transit is averaged against too small a base (M5-06).
6. An approved transfer request short of stock is sent short silently (M5-03); short pick rows are never re-resolved at dispatch (M5-02); a self-received transfer is unflagged (M5-16); a sale with no lot and an unknown batch never reaches the ledger (M5-05).

## Why it matters

Selling expired food is an offence; fail-open approval is the one control on giving stock away; a count that trades while counting must not create stock; cost must be conserved across transfers, repacks and their reversals or the entity average, and with it every margin report, drifts.

## Proposed change

1. **25A §6 (picks, availability, candidates)**: a lot is expired when `expiry_date < business date` (Asia/Colombo, passed in, never SQL `current_date`); expired lots are never picked for a delivery or a transfer, never counted as available and never price candidates; they stay in `balances` with a null rank; the sale fallback keeps them and writes REVIEW `SALE_OF_EXPIRED`; a delivery note to another entity also skips lots with less than `inventory.dispatch_min_shelf_life_days` left (§7: ENTITY, default 0); transfers inside an entity use expiry only. Open pick-list reservations are subtracted from `pickBatches` and `goodOnHand`.
2. **25A §6 ApproveWriteOff / ApproveAdjustment, §10, doc 25 §7**: the approver's authority is the grant's `max_value`; a grant without one approves up to `inventory.approval_band1_limit`; no grant at the entity is no authority (`m5.approval.limit_exceeded`); the line is valued at the entity average, else the lot cost, and a zero-cost line routes to band 2. Band 1 ≤ Rs 25,000 society accountant or branch manager; band 2 ≤ Rs 250,000 general manager; band 3 the chairman with the board resolution reference in the reason. The two figures stay ENTITY items.
3. **Doc 25 §3.8 and 25A §6 ApproveWriteOff**: the approver differs from the in-person witness (`m5.writeoff.approver_is_witness`); a single-staff location's remote witness, who holds approve, may also approve. **WitnessWriteOff**: accepted when no photograph is PENDING, each is COMPLETE or FAILED, and at least one is COMPLETE where required; FAILED ones are listed in the audit.
4. **25A §3, §6 SubmitCount**: `count_line.counted_at timestamptz` (nullable), stamped by the form when the line is entered; the line's book is `qty_on_hand − Σ qty_delta` of the lot's movements with `occurred_at > counted_at`; a line without it keeps the submit-time rule. A line auto-posts only within quantity/percent **and** `inventory.count_tolerance_value` (ENTITY, Rs 1,000); if the auto-posted total exceeds `inventory.count_autopost_value_cap` (ENTITY, Rs 10,000) every line goes to review; a surplus on a lot with book ≤ 0 is always reviewed; each cleared line is REVIEW `COUNT_TOLERANCE_CLEARED`.
5. **25A §6 ExecuteRepack, ReverseRepack; doc 25 §3.3**: `inventory.repack_yield_tolerance_pct` (ENTITY, 2): over-yield beyond it is refused on the web (`m5.repack.yield_out_of_range`), under-yield beyond it needs a reason and is REVIEW `REPACK_YIELD_EXCEPTION`; a till bundle flags and never refuses. `CostService`: TRANSFER_IN carries its own cost (the TRANSFER_OUT's cost at movement) and re-averages; an out movement "at a given cost" removes value at that cost, `avg' = (Q·a − q·c)/(Q − q)` when `Q − q > 0`, else the last average, clamped at ≥ 0, used by the repack reversal (at `repack.output_unit_cost`) and by SALE_REVERSAL (at the original SALE's `unit_cost_at_movement`); the half-rounding of a repack's output cost (at most Rs 0.05 per 1,000 packs) is accepted.
6. **25A §6 (the transfer request consumer, DispatchDelivery, ReceiveTransfer, ApplySale)**: a request is filled once; what could not be sent is REVIEW `TRANSFER_REQUEST_SHORT` and `TransferIssued.shortLines`; the shop raises a new request for the rest. Short pick rows are re-picked FEFO at dispatch; the remainder is REVIEW `PICK_SHORT_DISPATCHED`, never a negative posting. A self-received transfer is REVIEW `TRANSFER_SELF_RECEIVED`. A sale with no lot and an unknown batch posts against the SKU's newest batch in M2, creating the lot negative under the existing `SALE_WITHOUT_LOT` flow; `SALE_LINE_UNRESOLVED` stays for an unknown SKU only.
