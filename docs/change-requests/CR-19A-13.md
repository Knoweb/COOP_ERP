# CR-19A-13 — Counterparty delivery: a central event may run a registered consumer in the counterparty's scope

- **Status:** accepted (6 October 2026, on the architect's delegation; wave 2 code review); the documents are not re-issued yet
- **Date raised:** 2026-10-06
- **Raised by:** Claude Code session, wave 2 code review decisions (`wave2-fixreview-m9-integration.md` M9-02 D3 and `wave2-fixreview-m4-money.md` M4MONEY-10 E-1, which disagreed; resolved in `docs/progress/deviations/2026-10-06-wave2-buyer-postings.md`)
- **Documents affected:** doc 19 §5 (events: "a consumer runs in the scope of the event's owner"); 19A §5 (`@EventConsumer`, `EventConsumerDispatcher`, the inbox row per consumer); doc 24 §3.9 (the buyer's postings) and 24A §3.1 (the posting map's BUYER rows), both through CR-24A-3 item 5; doc 29 §3.3 and §6.2 (the export carries "GRN and INV (buyer)"), through CR-29-1 item 3
- **Code affected:** `kernel.api.EventConsumer` (a `party` attribute, default OWNER), `kernel.internal.events.EventConsumerDispatcher`; `m4trading.api.JournalPostingsReady` (`counterpartyEntityId`, BUYER lines); `m9integration.internal.journal.JournalPostingsConsumer` (SELLER lines only) and a new `BuyerJournalPostingsConsumer` (`m9.journal.buyer`); `seed/m9integration/posting-map.yaml` (CN GOODS BUYER rows)

## What is wrong or missing

Every `journal.postings_ready.v1` is published with the seller as owner (`OutboxWriter` stamps the session's `kernel.scope_entity()`), and `EventConsumerDispatcher` runs every consumer in that owner's scope. The posting map's `INV GOODS BUYER` rows (24A §3.1) therefore have no producer: an MPCS's journal export has no purchases, no payables and no VAT input (doc 29 §3.3 requires "GRN and INV (buyer)"), and its trial balance cannot match its supplier's statement. The 800 societies are buyers first.

Two ways were proposed. (a) The buyer acknowledges each supplier invoice by a command of its own, which publishes the buyer's postings in the buyer's scope. (b) The kernel delivers the seller's event to a consumer registered for the counterparty, in the counterparty's scope.

## Why it matters

The payable exists when the invoice is issued: the document is immutable, issued to the buyer, and already readable by the buyer (PARTY). Under (a) the books of 800 societies depend on an accounts clerk acknowledging every invoice, and a missed click is a missing payable; three-way matching, which is what an acknowledgement would stand for, is already M4's GRN discrepancy flow. Under (b) one kernel mechanism gives every two-party document its second set of books, which is what double-entry between trading partners needs. (b) is chosen.

## Proposed change

1. **Doc 19 §5 and 19A §5**: "A consumer runs in the scope of the event's owner. A consumer registered with `party = COUNTERPARTY` runs instead in the OWN, entity-wide scope of the payload's `counterpartyEntityId`, under these conditions, which the dispatcher enforces: the event's source is central (never a device); the payload names a `documentId` whose `kernel.document.counterparty_entity_id` equals the named counterparty (a module cannot write into an arbitrary entity's scope); the consumer has its own inbox row, so owner-side and counterparty-side delivery are idempotent separately. The notification dispatcher's `otherParty` rule is the same reading of a two-party event."
2. **Doc 24 §3.9 and 24A §3.1** (through CR-24A-3 item 5): the GRN's buyer postings are published by `ConfirmGrn` in the receiver's own scope (the receiver confirms the GRN); the INV and CN buyer rows travel on the seller's event as BUYER lines with `counterpartyEntityId`; `CN GOODS BUYER` rows are added to the map (PAYABLE / INVENTORY net, PAYABLE / VAT_INPUT tax); PRC buyer rows are not published (doc 10 A-01, the accountant's review).
3. **Doc 29 §3.3 and 29A §6** (through CR-29-1 item 3): M9 registers `m9.journal.buyer` (COUNTERPARTY) recording BUYER lines only; `m9.journal` records SELLER lines only; the handler dedupes by `(owner_entity_id, document_id)`.

## Impact if accepted

- **Documents**: 19 (§5), 19A (§5) re-issued; 24, 24A, 29, 29A through their own requests.
- **Code**: the dispatcher's party attribute and its two checks; M4's event gains two additive fields; M9's two consumers and the map rows. Interim, shipped first: `JournalPostingsConsumer` records the scope's side only and logs BUYER lines at WARN.
- **Tests**: a dispatcher test that a counterparty consumer runs in the counterparty's scope and is refused for a device event and for a counterparty that is not the document's; an M9 test that one invoice yields the seller's and the buyer's postings, each under its own `own_read`.
- **Rejected**: the acknowledgement command, its table, permission and web button; the seller publishing the buyer's postings (the outbox stamps the session's entity); M9 inserting under a SECURITY DEFINER function for the counterparty (a second narrow door, harder to test than RLS); removing the dead map rows.
