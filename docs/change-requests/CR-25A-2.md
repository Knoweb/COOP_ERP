- **Status:** raised
- **Date raised:** 2026-11-18
- **Raised by:** PGL-M2-03 manual verification & safety review (`fix/m2-stock-repricing-spoilage`)
- **Documents affected:** `18 Part B` (`B-I4`, `B-I5`, `I-F3`), `25 §3.1–3.4`, `25A §3, §6.1, §7`, `28A §3, §6`, `29A §6.2`
- **Code affected:** `m5inventory` (`internal/reprice/`, `internal/ledger/`, `api/MovementType.java`, `V0018` follow-up migration), `m8reporting` (`internal/projection/StockPositionProjection.java`, valuation reporting), `m9integration` (`internal/journal/`), `web/src/modules/m5inventory/StockRepriceModal.tsx`

## What is wrong or missing

The initial PGL-M2-03 implementation (`RepriceStockLotHandler` + `m5inventory/V0018__stock_repricing_and_spoilage.sql`) conflicts with core M5/M8/M9 invariants established in the authoritative design documents:

1. **Direct `stock_lot` quantity mutation breaks `B-I4` (`stock_lot.qty_on_hand = Σ stock_movement.qty_delta`)**:
   - Doc 18 §B-I4: *"stock_lot.qty_on_hand equals the sum of movements for that lot; a nightly reconciliation asserts it."*
   - Doc 25 §3.2 & `LedgerService.java`: `LedgerService.post` is the sole writer of `stock_lot.qty_on_hand`, `stock_movement`, and `entity_sku_cost`.
   - Current defect: On `GOOD <-> DAMAGED` condition reclassification, `RepriceStockLotHandler` executes raw `UPDATE inventory.stock_lot SET qty_on_hand = ...` and raw `INSERT INTO inventory.stock_lot` without posting balanced `stock_movement` rows or emitting `stock.moved.v1`.
2. **Missing document citation (`B-I5`) and non-UUIDv7 identifier**:
   - Doc 18 §B-I5: *"No stock_movement exists without a document_id."*
   - Current defect: Repricing issues no kernel document and uses `UUID.randomUUID()` instead of `Ids.next()`.
3. **In-place mutation of `stock_lot.unit_cost` violates acquisition-cost immutability (`I-F3`, Doc 25 §3.1 & §3.3)**:
   - Doc 25 §3.1 & §3.3: *"The lot keeps its acquisition cost for traceability and for claims; the entity average is the costing figure for cost of goods sold, valuation and write-off value. Both are stored; neither is recomputed retroactively. A DAMAGED lot carries the same unit cost as its GOOD sibling; the loss is recognised when written off or when marked down (M3)."*
   - Current defect: `V0018` introduces `SECURITY DEFINER` function `inventory.apply_lot_reprice` to `UPDATE inventory.stock_lot SET unit_cost`, overwriting original acquisition cost and blending costs directly on the lot row.
4. **Open pick-list reservations are ignored during `GOOD -> DAMAGED` reclassification**:
   - Doc 25 §3.4 & `InventoryQueriesImpl.RANKED_LOTS`: `available = GOOD qty_on_hand - open pick_list_line reservations` (accounting for `pick_override`).
   - Current defect: `RepriceStockLotHandler` checks only `qty <= qty_on_hand` without locking the source lot (`FOR UPDATE`) or subtracting active open reservations, allowing condition downgrades to undercut reserved pick lists.
5. **M8 `reporting.stock_position` and financial reports drift from M5**:
   - Doc 28A §3 & `StockPositionProjection.java`: `reporting.stock_position` updates exclusively from `stock.moved.v1`, and stock value tiles sum `qty_on_hand * unit_cost`.
   - Current defect: `StockRepriced` (`inventory.stock_repriced.v1`) has no M8 consumer, so condition splits and valuation changes leave `reporting.stock_position` stale.
6. **M9 accounting postings (`journal.postings_ready.v1`) are missing**:
   - Doc 29A §6.2 & `JournalPostingsConsumer.java`: accounting journal entries are recorded via `journal.postings_ready.v1`.
   - Current defect: Valuation adjustments (`value_adjustment != 0`) emit no balanced double-entry journal postings, causing GL-to-inventory-subledger mismatch.

## Why it matters

Allowing unsafe `POST /v1/inventory/lots/{stockLotId}/reprice` mutations breaks `B-I4` (causing nightly `StockReconcileJob` `STOCK_DRIFT` failures), erases lot acquisition costs needed for supplier claims, undercuts reserved pick-list stock, desynchronizes M8 reporting stock positions, and leaves M9 journal exports unbalanced against inventory valuation.

As an **immediate safety containment (Option B)** on branch `fix/m2-stock-repricing-spoilage`, `RepriceStockLotHandler` blocks all repricing mutations with `422 m5.reprice.temporarily_unavailable` before any DB read/write, and `StockRepriceModal.tsx` disables **Apply Reprice** with a trilingual notice while preserving existing repricing history (`GET /v1/inventory/repricings`).

## Proposed change (Option A — Pending Approval of Decisions D-1 to D-6)

1. **Condition Reclassification via Immutable Balanced Ledger Movements**:
   - Perform full and partial `GOOD`/`DAMAGED` reclassifications exclusively through `StockLedger.post(...)` as paired movements (`-qty` out of source condition, `+qty` into target condition, net `ΔQ = 0`) citing an issued kernel document (`B-I5`).
   - Copy original acquisition `unit_cost` when `LedgerService` initializes a new target condition lot; never overwrite `stock_lot.unit_cost`.
2. **Reservation Guard & Canonical Locking**:
   - Lock `stock_lot` rows in canonical `(location_id, batch_id, condition)` order (`FOR UPDATE`) matching `LedgerService`, check open pick-list reservations (`pick_list.status = 'OPEN'`, accounting for `pick_override`), and refuse `GOOD -> DAMAGED` when `qty > (qty_on_hand - reserved_qty)` (`m5.reprice.reserved_qty_exceeded`).
3. **Prospective Entity Weighted-Average Valuation (IAS 2)**:
   - Keep `stock_lot.unit_cost` and historical `stock_movement.unit_cost_at_movement` (and historical COGS) strictly immutable.
   - Apply valuation adjustments prospectively to `inventory.entity_sku_cost.avg_cost` under `FOR UPDATE` lock (`newTotalValue = entityQty * avgCost + valueAdjustment >= 0`), and revoke `EXECUTE` on `inventory.apply_lot_reprice` from `app_rw` in an additive migration.
4. **M8 Projections & M9 Accounting Postings**:
   - Emit `journal.postings_ready.v1` with balanced debit/credit lines for `value_adjustment != 0`.
   - Update `m8reporting` (`StockPositionProjection` + additive `reporting.inventory_valuation_adjustment` table with 4-class RLS) so stock position unit cost and P&L / Finance reports reflect valuation adjustments accurately.

### Architecture Decisions Requiring Project-Owner Approval

Nothing in Option A may be implemented until the following decisions are explicitly approved:

- **D-1 — Condition Reclassification Movement Type(s):** Approve exact `inventory.stock_movement.movement_type` enum value(s) — e.g. paired `CONDITION_CHANGE_OUT` / `CONDITION_CHANGE_IN` (recommended) vs. single `CONDITION_CHANGE` (`EITHER` requiring balanced pairs).
- **D-2 — Valuation Cost Baseline:** Confirm whether `value_adjustment` is computed against `entity_sku_cost.avg_cost` (recommended, so GL adjustment ties exactly to entity carrying value) or `stock_lot.unit_cost`.
- **D-3 — Upward Revaluation Ceiling (IAS 2.33):** Confirm whether upward adjustments above original acquisition cost are disallowed (recommended: write-downs and NRV reversals up to original cost only) or permitted under MFA + two-person approval.
- **D-4 — GL Account Roles & Journal Mapping:** Approve exact account role codes for inventory valuation write-downs (and reversals if permitted), and whether zero-value `GOOD -> DAMAGED` condition reclassifications emit a GL entry or stay subledger-only.
- **D-5 — Kernel Document Type & Approval / MFA Governance:** Approve the document type code (`ADJ` vs. new `RPR`/`REV`) satisfying `B-I5`, and whether repricing requires MFA (`requiresMfa = true`) and/or banded two-person approval.
- **D-6 — Condition Upgrade Scope (`DAMAGED -> GOOD`):** Confirm whether upgrading `DAMAGED -> GOOD` is permitted for all `inv.stock.reprice` holders or restricted to supervisor approval.
