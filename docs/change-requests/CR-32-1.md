# CR-32-1 — Sync contract: facts are accepted below the version floor; a quarantined event is resolved, not purged, and never stores a forbidden value; a revoked till keeps its outbox; the till's business date

## What is wrong or missing

1. **The floor.** Doc 31 §6 and doc 32 §3.3 step 1 refuse sync below the minimum version after the grace, and `BatchIngestor.checkFloor` answers 426. CR-30-1 point 4, accepted by the architect on 28 September 2026, says: "Below the minimum version, central stops sending new snapshots, but facts are always accepted: a sale that happened is a fact." That is also S4 and the AGENTS.md rule ("never reject a fact uploaded by a till"). The code follows the superseded text; CR-30-1 names docs 30, 26, 26A and 17A for re-issue but not 31 and 32.
2. **Quarantine.** Step 6 says "store raw"; for a `FORBIDDEN_FIELD` quarantine the raw event is the forbidden value (a phone or NIC in a payload), stored for good in a table with no retention. There is no procedure for what happens to a quarantined fact: a time-based purge would contradict S4 ("never lost") and §7 ("the document is not applied until the till resends a correct copy").
3. **A revoked till.** §9 says the unsent facts are "preserved for recovery by an administrator"; §8 has counter transfer and sequence reset, but no procedure gets rows out of a locked device, and the till as built deletes its outbox at acknowledgement.
4. **The business date at the till.** Doc 26 §3.5 and doc 19 §9 make the business date forward-only at central; the till's local date is forward-only too, so a wrong PC clock leaves every later receipt misdated with no supervised correction.

## Why it matters

A shop that cannot update for three weeks must still get its sales into the books; a forbidden value stored "raw" defeats the rule the quarantine exists to enforce; a revoked PC with two days of unsent sales is a loss unless the procedure is written; a misdated till day-close is a wrong business day in the kernel.

## Proposed change

1. **Doc 32 §3.3 step 1 and doc 31 §6**: "Below the version floor, after the grace, central still accepts the device's batches and answers with `FLOOR_NOTICE`; it withholds snapshots and change pages (`426 sync.app_below_floor`), so the till keeps selling on its last snapshot and the staleness warning (§5.2) tells the supervisor. Facts are always accepted (CR-30-1 point 4)." Operations: the floor is not raised above a shipped version until the updater of CR-30-1 exists.
2. **Doc 32 §3.3 step 6 and §7**: "Store raw in `sync_quarantine` with the reason, **except that for a FORBIDDEN_FIELD quarantine the values of the offending keys are replaced by `[removed]`** (the key stays, so the person sees what the till sent and where; the value is never needed for the repair). A quarantine row is **resolved**, never purged by time: an MPCS administrator marks it REPAIRED (the till resent a correct copy, §8) or DISCARDED, with a reason, through an audited command (`sync.quarantine.resolve`, event `sync.quarantine.resolved.v1`), once; the raw event is nulled `sync.quarantine.raw_retention_days` (default 30) after resolution; the row stays as the record that a fact was refused and why."
3. **Doc 32 §8 and §9, the revoked device**: "A revoked till honours only a revoke signed with central's key that names its device id and post-dates its enrolment; it locks, deletes its snapshot rows and keeps its settings, receipts, sessions and outbox. It resumes uploading its kept outbox from `last acknowledged + 1` when the administrator reinstates the device or issues a new enrolment code for the same device id (the cursor at central is unchanged). A device never reinstated has its unreceived numbers recorded as a documented gap by counter transfer." The till retains acknowledged rows for `sync.outbox.retention_days` (S8, DR-3: 7 days) and records every QUARANTINED outcome locally for the Z-report.
4. **Doc 26 §3.5, one sentence**: "The till's local business date is its cache of central's forward-only date. A supervisor may move it backwards when no session is open; receipts and sessions already issued keep the date they carry. The till refuses to open a date more than one day after central's last `server_time` when it has synced within 48 hours."

## Impact if accepted

- **Documents**: 32 re-issued (§3.3, §7, §8, §9); 31 (§6); 26 (§3.5). CR-30-1's list of documents to re-issue gains 31 and 32.
- **Code**: the kernel changes above (`V0084`); the 426 contract tests move from the batch path to the snapshot path; `m1.device.version_floor` (M1's assignment guard) is untouched; the till's outbox, anomaly table, instruction handling and business-date correction.
- **Tests**: a batch below the floor accepted with `FLOOR_NOTICE` and a snapshot refused; a FORBIDDEN_FIELD quarantine whose stored event holds `[removed]` and not the phone; a row resolved once and refused twice; the schema test pins the four-column UPDATE grant; a signed revoke fixture and an unsigned one on the till.
- **Rejected**: 426 on batches (refuses facts); a time-based purge of unresolved rows; an export-from-the-locked-till procedure (new design, no central side); a forward-only till date for ever.
